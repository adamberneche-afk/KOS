**Unit:** Foundations of Marketing

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 25

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Examining All Aspects of an Industry

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **011:** Demonstrate big-picture thinking.
  - **024:** Explain the marketing concept.
  - **073:** Describe the history of the sports, entertainment, and event industries.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"How did your grandparents buy groceries compared to how you buy food today? (Think: Milkman vs. Instacart). What changed? Was it the *product* or the *process*?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Marketing used to be simple: 'I have a cow, you have grain, let's trade.' Then it became yelling at people with TV ads. Now, it's whispering to them through influencers. Today, we trace the timeline of how power shifted from the *Seller* (who had the goods) to the *Buyer* (who has the choice)."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Check the "Future Prediction" section.

  - **Eras:** Did they list the 7 eras correctly?
  - **Prediction:** Did they offer a thoughtful idea for the "Next Era"?

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Which Era do you think was the hardest to be a business owner in? The 'Production Era' (high competition for resources) or the current 'Collaboration Era' (high competition for attention)? Explain why."

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: The Seven Eras of Marketing (Timeline Analysis)

**Description:**

Marketing hasn't always been about Instagram likes and Super Bowl commercials. It has evolved over centuries, changing as technology and society changed. To understand where we are going, we must look at where we started.

**The Task:**

Using the class slides, create a summary of the **Seven Eras of Marketing**.

**Part 1: The Timeline**

For each of the eras below, identify the **Time Period** and the **Primary Focus** (1 sentence per era).

  - Trade Era
  - Production Era
  - Selling Era
  - Marketing Department Era
  - Marketing Company Era
  - Relationship Marketing
  - Collaboration Era

**Part 2: The Shift**

Explain the difference between the **Selling Era** and the **Marketing Company Era**.

  - *Hint: In the Selling Era, businesses tried to trick people into buying. In the Marketing Era, businesses tried to... what?*

**Part 3: The Future (Critical Thinking)**

Based on what you see with AI, Virtual Reality, and privacy laws, write a **3-sentence prediction** for what the *next* Era of Marketing will be called.
