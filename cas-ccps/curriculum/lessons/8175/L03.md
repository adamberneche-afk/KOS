## LESSON 03: PERSONAL BRANDING (DEFINING YOUR USP)

**Unit:** Professional Development and Branding

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 25

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Demonstrating Professional Competencies

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **012:** Demonstrate career and life management skills.
  - **047:** Identify the components of branding. (Applied to the individual).
  - **107:** Identify personal skills and interests.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Write down three adjectives that your friends would use to describe you. Now, write down three adjectives that a teacher or boss would use to describe you. Are they different? Why?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Jeff Bezos once said, 'Your brand is what people say about you when you are not in the room.' If you applied for a job with the NFL or a record label today, what would your Google results say about you? Today, we are going to learn how to control that narrative."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Do not grade for "correct" answers, as this is personal. Grade for effort and completion.

  - Did they answer all three sections?
  - Is the "Unique Selling Proposition" specific or descriptive? Give higher points for descriptive answers.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"What is one 'weakness' you have that you could actually frame as a strength in a job interview?"

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Defining Your Personal Brand (The CEO of YOU)

**Description:**

It is important to understand your own personal brand. When you understand who you are and what you stand for, you can find new opportunities and ways to strengthen your weaknesses. A brand statement also helps others understand who you are, what you are great at, and how you differentiate yourself from others.

**The Task:**

Answer the questions below to create your personal brand statement. Be honest and dig deep.

**1. Your Value**

  - What value can you provide the world (or people) with your strengths and attributes?

**2. Your USP (Unique Selling Proposition)**

  - What is your Unique Selling Proposition?
  - What is the **one thing** that makes you stand out from the crowd? Why should a team pick you over the 100 other people applying for the job?

**3. Your Audience**

  - Who do you want to help, serve, or would benefit the most from what you can offer?

**Submission:**

Submit your answers in a text entry box or a Google Doc.
