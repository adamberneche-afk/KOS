## LESSON 04: PERSONAL BRAND ANALYSIS (PUBLIC FIGURES)

**Unit:** Branding and Professional Development

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 20

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Exploring Branding

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **002:** Demonstrate critical thinking and problem solving.
  - **046:** Explain the concept of branding.
  - **047:** Identify the components of branding.
  - **048:** Describe the characteristics of effective branding.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Think of a celebrity or athlete who 'ruined' their reputation recently. Without naming names, write down one action they took that damaged their brand. How hard do you think it will be for them to fix it?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"We talked about *your* brand yesterday. Now let's look at the pros. Why does 'The Rock' make millions just for showing up to an event, while other actors struggle to get an audition? It is not just about talent; it is about the Brand they built."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Look for depth in the "Motivation" and "How others describe this person" answers. The "Strengths and Weaknesses" section is a good check for critical thinking.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Based on the person you analyzed today, what is one specific 'Brand Rule' you should apply to your own life?"

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Personal Brand Analysis (The Celebrity Case Study)

**Description:**

Everybody has a personal brand. Whether you realize it or not, your actions mold your personal brand. You will be taking a closer look at a public figure's personal brand to see how they manage their reputation and career.

**The Task:**

Answer the following questions about the individual's personal brand. Please write in complete sentences.

**1. The Subject**

  - Who is the public figure you chose?
  - What makes this individual well known?

**2. The SWOT Analysis**

  - What is a major **Strength** this individual has?
  - What is a **Weakness** or challenge they face?

**3. The Public Perception**

  - How do others describe this person?
  - Does their public reputation match who they really seem to be?

**4. The Core Drivers**

  - What do you think motivates this individual?
  - What do you think they are passionate about outside of their job?

**Submission:**

Submit your responses in a text entry box.
