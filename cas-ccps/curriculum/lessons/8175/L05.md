## LESSON 05: SOURCES OF SUPPORT (BUILDING GRIT)

**Unit:** Professional Development and Career Resilience

**Duration:** 1 Class Period (Short Activity)

**Points:** 15

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Demonstrating Personal Qualities and Abilities

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **003:** Demonstrate initiative and self-direction.
  - **012:** Demonstrate career- and life-management skills.
  - **109:** Create a networking plan.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Who is the first person you call when you have bad news? Who is the first person you call when you have good news? Write down their names and why you trust them."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"We often hear the phrase 'self-made millionaire.' Spoiler alert: They do not exist. Every successful person stood on a foundation of support—mentors, friends, family, and teams. To have 'grit' doesn't mean you suffer alone; it means you know who to lean on so you can keep going."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Check for the specific constraint: **5 Sentences.**

  - Did they write at least 5 sentences?
  - Did they identify a community they are currently in AND one they want to join?

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Name one specific group, club, or organization at this school that you are NOT a part of, but could help you succeed if you joined."

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Sources of Support (Developing Grit)

**Description:**

If "grit" is developed through continuously facing setbacks without giving up, then your available support systems greatly increase your chances of developing "grit." These groups help us keep things in perspective and continue to search for success when we are met with adversity.

**The Task:**

Using **5 sentences**, explore this concept in your own life. Please answer the following questions in your response:

  - What communities are you a part of that will offer you this support?
  - How can you become a part of **new** communities that offer this support?
  - What have communities done for you in the past that helped you succeed?
  - Are you a part of any communities that hinder your success?

**Submission:**

Submit your paragraph as a Text Entry.
