## LESSON 06: SKILL DEVELOPMENT PLAN (THE SOFT SKILLS GAP)

**Unit:** Professional Development and Career Readiness

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 20

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Demonstrating Personal Qualities and Abilities

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **005:** Demonstrate work ethic.
  - **020:** Demonstrate professionalism.
  - **021:** Demonstrate reading and writing skills.
  - **112:** Describe the job interview process.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"What is a 'soft skill'? Can you teach someone to be patient or kind, or do they have to be born with it? Explain your answer."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"There is a famous viral video by Simon Sinek about 'Millennials in the Workplace.' He argues that addiction to phones and instant gratification is making it hard for young people to build careers. Today, we are going to watch it, analyze it, and build a plan to prove him wrong."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Check the word count and the "Plan" section.

  - **Word Count:** Did they meet the 100-word minimum?
  - **The Plan:** Did they list specific actions or vague promises? Award higher points for specific, actionable steps.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"What is one 'bad habit' (like checking your phone when bored) that you need to break before you walk into your first big job interview?"

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Skill Development Plan (The "Soft Skills" Gap)

**Description:**

Employers often say that while young employees have great technical skills, they sometimes lack "soft skills" like patience, face-to-face communication, and resilience.

**The Task:**

  - **Watch:** Watch the provided videos regarding skills in the modern workplace.
  - **Plan:** Write a **100-word plan** to practice these skills prior to starting a new job.
  - **Reflect:** In your plan, ensure you answer the following questions:

<!-- end list -->

  - What did you learn from the videos that was not expected?
  - How did this experience help shape your future career plans?
  - What is something specific you could do that would help you in an interview setting?

**Submission:**

Submit your plan as a Text Entry.
