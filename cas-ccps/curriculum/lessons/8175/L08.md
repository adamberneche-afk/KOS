### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Have you ever sat through a boring presentation where the speaker just read the slides to you? Write down one specific thing they did that made you tune out."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Slides are not a teleprompter. They are a billboard. If you are reading your slides to the audience, you are insulting their intelligence because they can read faster than you can speak. Today, we are going to learn the 'Rule of Six' and how to design slides that actually keep people awake."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

This is a visual check assignment.

  - **The Rule of Six:** Scan the slides. If any slide has a paragraph or more than 6 lines, deduct points immediately.
  - **The Script:** Ensure they submitted a script or speaker notes.

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: The "Rule of Six" Slide Challenge

**Description:**

Most PowerPoint presentations fail because the presenter treats the slides like a document. They fill the screen with text and read it to the audience. This is called "Death by PowerPoint." To be effective, you must follow the design rules used by professional marketers.

**The Rules:**

  - **The Rule of Six:** No more than six lines of text per slide. No more than six words per line.
  - **Images:** No more than 2 images per slide.
  - **The KISS Principle:** Keep It Simple. Light background, dark font. Minimal animations.

**The Task:**

You will create a short slide deck (3 to 5 slides) on a topic of your choice.

**Requirements:**

  - **The Script:** You must write a script for what you will say in the "Speaker Notes" section.
  - **The Visuals:** Your slides must strictly follow the **Rule of Six**.
  - **Design:** Use a high contrast design (Light background, dark font).

**Submission:**

Submit your Slide Deck (PowerPoint or Google Slides link) with the Speaker Notes included.
