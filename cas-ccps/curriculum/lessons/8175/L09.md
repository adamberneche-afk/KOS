## LESSON 09: DEFINING BRANDING (THE MENTOR CHALLENGE)

**Unit:** Branding Strategy and Communication

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 50

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Exploring Branding

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **002:** Demonstrate critical thinking and problem solving.
  - **021:** Demonstrate reading and writing skills.
  - **046:** Explain the concept of branding.
  - **049:** Explain the nature of brand loyalty.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Try to describe the color 'Blue' to someone who has been blind their whole life. You cannot use visual words like 'sky' or 'ocean.' You have to use feelings or other senses."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Albert Einstein once said, 'If you cannot explain it simply, you do not understand it well enough.' We have spent weeks learning marketing vocabulary. Today, the test isn't a bubble sheet. The test is whether you can translate that vocabulary into plain English for someone who knows nothing about business."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Use the provided rubric structure for speed grading:

  - **Clarity (20 pts):** Did they move beyond just saying "it is a logo"?
  - **The Promise (Critical Check):** Look for the section where they define branding as a "promise" or "trust."

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"What is the one brand you are most loyal to, and what specific 'promise' have they kept to you that makes you stay?"

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: What IS Branding? (The Mentor Challenge)

**Description:**

We have spent the year looking at logos, slogans, and the emotional connections companies make with consumers. Now, it is time to synthesize that information.

**The Scenario:**

Imagine that a younger student, or perhaps a relative with absolutely no business background, comes up to you and asks: "I keep hearing people talk about 'branding,' but I don't get it. What actually IS branding?"

**The Task:**

Step into the role of the teacher. Write out your response to this person (Minimum 250 words).

**Structure:**

  - **The Introduction:** Start by telling them what branding is **not**. Define it simply.
  - **The Examples:** Use a real example (like Apple or Coca Cola). Explain *why* people buy that brand.
  - **The Promise:** Explain how a brand is a promise to the customer. That trust is branding.
  - **The Conclusion:** Wrap it up by telling them why it matters.

**Submission:**

Submit your response as a Text Entry.
