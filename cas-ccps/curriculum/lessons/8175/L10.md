## LESSON 10: SERVICE EXPERIENCE REFLECTION

**Unit:** Customer Relations and Quality Management

**Duration:** 1 Class Period (Short Activity)

**Points:** 15

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Demonstrating Interpersonal Skills

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **009:** Demonstrate customer service skills.
  - **011:** Demonstrate big-picture thinking.
  - **021:** Demonstrate reading and writing skills.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"What is worse: A rude employee who gets your order right, or a polite employee who gets your order wrong? Why?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Why does Chick-fil-A generate more revenue per store than almost any other fast food chain, despite being closed on Sundays? It is not just the chicken. It is the 'My Pleasure.' Today, we are going to analyze how being treated well actually changes the way our brains value a product."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Check for the length constraint first.

  - **Length:** Did they meet the **10-sentence** minimum?
  - **Depth:** Did they answer the question about "Perceived Value"?

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Write down one specific action you could take at your current (or future) job to make a customer feel the way you felt in your story today."

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Service Experience Reflection (The Gold Standard)

**Description:**

In marketing, we often focus on the product, but the *experience* is what keeps customers coming back. Great service can make a cheap product feel luxurious, while bad service can ruin even the best product.

**The Task:**

Think of a time when you were impressed with the service you received from a company.

**Prompt:**

Please write a reflection responding to the following questions. **You must respond using at least 10 complete sentences.**

  - **The Action:** What exactly did the employee or company do that made them stand out from their competitors?
  - **The Emotion:** How did it make you feel as a customer?
  - **The Value:** What does this do to the perceived value of your purchase?

**Submission:**

Submit your reflection as a Text Entry.
