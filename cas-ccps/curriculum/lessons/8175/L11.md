## LESSON 11: CHHS BRAND AUDIT (INTERNAL ANALYSIS)

**Unit:** Branding and Market Identification

**Duration:** 1 to 2 Class Periods

**Points:** 30

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Demonstrating Professional Competencies; Developing an Overview of Marketing; Understanding the Importance of Sports, Entertainment, and Event Marketing; Exploring the Concept of Branding

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **021:** Demonstrate reading and writing skills.
  - **042:** Explain the concept of target marketing (Demographics and psychographics of CHHS's audience).
  - **047:** Analyze trends and innovations in the sports, entertainment, and event industries (Cultural and societal trends, like social media and mental health awareness).
  - **057:** Explain the concept of branding as it relates to sports, entertainment, and event marketing (The Cavalier brand: logo, colors and reputation).
  - **059:** Explain the concept of and reasons for rebranding (Cultural and societal trends that could drive a rebrand).

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Write down the first three words that come to your mind when I say 'Clover Hill High School.' Do not overthink it. Be honest."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Be honest. If Clover Hill High School was a person, what kind of shoes would they wear? Are we 'Air Jordans' or are we 'Crocs'?" This prompts a discussion on brand personality and perception.

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Focus assessment on Section 2 (Brand Quality). Read the student's critique carefully. If they utilize vocabulary words such as "Target Market" or "Demographics" correctly in context, award full credit.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Identify one specific thing regarding the school facility or culture that hurts our 'Brand Image' the most."

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: CHHS Brand Audit (The Home Field Advantage)

**Description:**

You walk the halls every day, but have you ever looked at Clover Hill High School as a **brand**? Just like a sports team or a clothing line, our school has an image, a target audience, and a reputation.

**The Task:**

Conduct a brand audit of Clover Hill High School. Write a **250 word (minimum) essay** covering the following three areas.

**1. Target Market Identification (Min. 75 words)**

  - Who are we serving?
  - Identify the target market (Demographics and Psychographics).

**2. Brand Quality Assessment (Min. 100 words)**

  - Assess the quality of the current "Cavalier" brand.
  - What do the logo, the colors, and the reputation say to the community?
  - Determine specific **areas for improvement**.

**3. Cultural and Societal Trends (Min. 75 words)**

  - Analyze current cultural and societal trends that may impact rebranding efforts (e.g., social media, mental health awareness).

**Submission:**

Submit your essay as a Text Entry.
