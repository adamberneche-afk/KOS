## LESSON 12: THE SWOT ANALYSIS (COMPANY EDITION)

**Unit:** Marketing Strategy and Analysis

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 25

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Exploring the Marketing Plan

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **101:** Explain competition as it relates to the sports industries.
  - **102:** Explain the components of the marketing plan.
  - **105:** Identify the components of a SWOT analysis.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Imagine you are the General Manager of the Washington Commanders. Name one internal Strength of the team (something they control) and one external Threat to the team (something they cannot control, like the economy)."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Before a general goes to war, he looks at the map. He looks at his own army (Strengths/Weaknesses) and the battlefield (Opportunities/Threats). In business, this map is called a SWOT Analysis. Today, we are going to map out the strategy for a major sports team."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Check for the distinction between **Internal** and **External**.

  - **Strengths/Weaknesses** must be Internal.
  - **Opportunities/Threats** must be External.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Why is a 'Threat' different from a 'Weakness'? Explain in one sentence."

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: SWOT Analysis (The War Room)

**Description:**

A SWOT Analysis is the foundation of any marketing plan. It helps businesses understand where they stand compared to the competition.

**The Task:**

Select a major Sports Team or Entertainment Company (e.g., The Commanders, Disney, Netflix). Complete a SWOT Analysis for them.

**1. Internal Factors (Inside the Building)**

  - List 2 **Strengths**: (e.g., Loyal fan base, star player).
  - List 2 **Weaknesses**: (e.g., Old stadium, high ticket prices).

**2. External Factors (Outside the World)**

  - List 2 **Opportunities**: (e.g., Expanding to international markets).
  - List 2 **Threats**: (e.g., Rising inflation, a new competitor).

**3. The Strategy**

  - Based on your analysis, give **one specific recommendation** to the General Manager.

**Submission:**

Submit your analysis as a Text Entry.
