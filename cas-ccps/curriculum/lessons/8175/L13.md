## LESSON 13: CHHS REBRAND PITCH AND FOCUS GROUP PREP

**Unit:** Marketing Research and Presentation

**Duration:** 2 Class Periods

**Points:** 40

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Demonstrating Personal Qualities and Abilities; Developing Interpersonal Skills; Exploring Public Relations and Media Relations; Exploring the Marketing Plan

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **001:** Demonstrate creativity and innovation.
  - **050:** Develop presentation skills as a form of business communication.
  - **085:** (Optional) Explain the internal and external aspects of public relations (Announcing the rebrand to students and staff first, then the community).
  - **103:** Describe the importance of research in developing a marketing plan.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"How do you know if a movie is good before you watch it? How do you know if a restaurant is safe before you eat there? List three ways we research before we buy."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"You identified the problems with our school brand yesterday. Today, you have to sell the solution. If you cannot put it on a slide, you cannot sell it."

**Mini-Lesson (Comp 85):** Public relations has two audiences. Internal PR speaks to the people inside an organization (players, staff, students); external PR speaks to fans, the media and the community. A rebrand that surprises its own people usually fails, so the inside comes first.

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Review **Slide 5 (Focus Group Questions)** first. This assesses the new skill (Competency 103). If the student has written open ended, non biased questions intended to gather data, they receive an A.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Write one 'Bad Question' (Yes/No answer) and one 'Good Question' (Open ended) that you could ask about our school logo."

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Project: CHHS Rebrand (The Pitch and Focus Group Prep)

**Description:**

You have analyzed the brand. Now, you have to sell the new vision. We are going to use this slide deck to conduct Focus Groups with students and staff. Your goal is to turn your written thoughts into visuals that will spark a conversation.

**The Assignment:**

Create a **5 to 7 slide presentation** that summarizes your Brand Audit.

**Slide Requirements:**

  - **Title and Hook:** Your new "Tagline" or "Theme" for the school.
  - **The Target Persona:** Visualize the student body with images.
  - **The Gap Analysis:** Visually contrast the "Current Reality" vs. the "Desired Future."
  - **The Vision:** Show how your vision aligns with modern trends.
  - **Focus Group Questions:** List **3 specific questions** you would ask a student focus group to see if your brand ideas resonate.
  - **The Rollout:** One slide on how you would announce the new brand to people inside the school (students and staff) and to people outside it (families and the community).

**Submission:**

Submit your presentation file.
