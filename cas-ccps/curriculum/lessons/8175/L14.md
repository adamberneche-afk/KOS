## LESSON 14: LOCAL BUSINESS RESEARCH AND PRESENTATION

**Unit:** Sports and Entertainment Marketing Strategy

**Duration:** 4 Class Periods

**Points:** 80 (Project)

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Demonstrating Interpersonal Skills

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **007:** Demonstrate listening and speaking skills.
  - **010:** Collaborate with team members.
  - **015:** Demonstrate information-literacy skills.
  - **050:** Develop presentation skills.
  - **106:** Identify occupations within the sports industries.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now - Day 1):**

"Think of your favorite local spot to hang out on a Friday night (bowling, movies, skating). Why do you go there instead of staying home? Is it the product or the environment?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"We talk a lot about Nike and the NFL, but the real money in this industry is made right here in our backyard. Why does the local bowling alley survive when Netflix exists? Today, you become a consultant. You are going to pick a local business, take it apart, and tell us how it survives."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

  - **The Script:** Grade this for completion. It must be word-for-word.
  - **The Presentation:** Use a simple 3-point rubric (Visuals, Vocal, Content).

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket - Day 4):**

"Whose presentation (besides your own) taught you something new about a local business? What was one specific thing they did well?"

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Project: Local Business Deep Dive (The Consultant's Report)

**Description:**

You will act as a consultant analyzing a local business in the Sports and Entertainment industry (e.g., Movie Theaters, Bowling Alleys, Music Venues, Laser Tag).

**The Task Overview:**

  - **Day 1: Research and Scripting:** Select your business. Analyze their Brand, Product, and Offerings. Write a word-for-word Script.
  - **Day 2: Slide Deck Creation:** Use your script to create a slide deck. Follow best practices (Minimal text).
  - **Day 3: Peer Feedback Loop:** Present to a partner. Provide and receive feedback.
  - **Day 4: The Pitch:** Presentations are shared with the class.

**Key Analysis Points:**

  - **Brand Analysis:** What is their reputation?
  - **Future Forecast:** What changes would you expect for this business in the next 5 years based on AI and trends?

**Submission:**

Submit your Script (Day 1) and Final Presentation (Day 4).
