## LESSON 15: OLYMPONOMICS (THE COST OF GLORY)

**Unit:** Economics of Sports and Entertainment

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 100 (Summative)

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Describing Economics of the Industry

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **015:** Demonstrate information-literacy skills.
  - **021:** Demonstrate reading and writing skills.
  - **098:** Explain economic concepts and their relationship to the sports industries.
  - **100:** Explain the concept of supply and demand.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"If you threw a party that cost 500 dollars but it made you the most popular student in the school for a year, was it a smart financial investment? Why or why not?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Hosting the Olympics is the ultimate flex for a country. But imagine spending 15 billion dollars for a two week party, only to have the stadiums sit empty for the next 20 years. Today, you are an economic analyst deciding if the 'glory' is worth the 'price tag.'"

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Since this is a 100 point summative, use a 50/50 split:

  - **Research (50 Points):** Did they cite 3 specific sources?
  - **Argument (50 Points):** Did they pick a side (Investment vs. Trap) and defend it with data?

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Define the term 'White Elephant' as it relates to sports stadiums. Give one example you found today."

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Olymponomics (The Economics of Hosting)

**Description:**

Hosting the Olympics is glamorous. It brings the eyes of the world to your city. But it also costs billions of dollars. Today, you are an economic analyst deciding if the "glory" is worth the "price tag."

**The Task:**

Complete the two parts below to determine if hosting is a smart play or a financial disaster.

**Part 1: The Research**

Research the **Economics of the Olympics**. specifically looking for:

  - **The Cost:** How much does it cost?
  - **The Revenue:** How does the city make money back?
  - **The White Elephants:** What happened to stadiums in Athens or Rio after the games?
  - **The Successes:** Look up the 1984 Los Angeles Olympics.

**Part 2: The Essay (200 Words)**

Write a **200 word persuasive essay** answering this prompt:

*"Based on economic data and historical examples, is hosting the Olympic Games a smart financial investment for a city, or a financial trap?"*

**Requirements:**

  - **Evidence:** Cite specific examples.
  - **Citations:** List at least **3 sources** at the bottom.

Type your essay in Google Docs, then paste the text into the submission box.
