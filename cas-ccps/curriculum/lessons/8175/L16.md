### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"A bottle of water costs 1 dollar at the grocery store, 3 dollars at a gas station, and 8 dollars at a baseball stadium. Is the water different? If not, why is the price different?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Price is the only part of the marketing mix that actually brings money in. If you set the price too high, no one buys. If you set it too low, you go broke. Today, we are going to learn the math behind the magic."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Check the **Break-Even Math** section.

  - **Formula:** Did they show their work? (Fixed Costs / Profit per unit).
  - **Strategy:** Did they correctly identify "Skimming" vs. "Penetration"?

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"If a competitor lowers their price, should you automatically lower yours? Why or why not?"

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: The Pizza Break-Even Challenge

**Description:**

Setting a price isn't just guessing. It is a mathematical balance between covering your costs and what the customer is willing to pay.

**The Task:**

You are opening a concession stand at the football stadium selling Pizza. You need to determine your price.

**Part 1: The Math (Break-Even Analysis)**

  - **Data:** Fixed Costs: 500 dollars. Variable Cost (per pizza): 5 dollars.
  - **Scenario A:** You sell the pizza for 10 dollars. How many pizzas must you sell to cover your 500 dollar rent? (Show your work).
  - **Scenario B:** You lower the price to 7 dollars. How many pizzas must you sell *now* to cover your rent?

**Part 2: The Strategy**

  - **Question:** Which pricing strategy would you use for a *new* pizza brand?

<!-- end list -->

  - **Price Skimming:** Setting a high price to look exclusive.
  - **Market Penetration:** Setting a low price to get people to try it.
  - **Justification:** Explain your choice in 2 sentences.

**Submission:**

Submit your calculations and strategy as a Text Entry.
