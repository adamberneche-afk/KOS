## LESSON 17: GETTING IT THERE (DISTRIBUTION AND VENUES)

**Unit:** Distribution and Channel Management

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 25

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Understanding Distribution

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **052:** Explain the nature of channels of distribution.
  - **053:** Describe the role of intermediaries.
  - **078:** Describe the importance of venue to the industry.
  - **086:** Explain ticket sales and distribution.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Have you ever tried to buy concert tickets and they sold out instantly, only to appear on another site for triple the price? Who made that extra money: The Artist or the Reseller?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"You made the album. You set the price. But how does it actually get to the fan? In the music industry, the 'Middleman' (or Intermediary) often makes more money than the talent. Today we look at the 'Supply Chain' of entertainment."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Focus on the **Ethics of Scalping** section. Look for critical thinking. Ensure they use the word "Intermediary" correctly.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Name one 'Intermediary' involved in getting a pair of Nikes from the factory in China to your feet in Richmond."

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: The Ticketmaster Problem (Distribution Channels)

**Description:**

Distribution is the process of getting the product to the customer. The path a ticket takes from the stadium to your phone involves "Intermediaries" (Middlemen), and each one charges a fee.

**The Task:**

Analyze the distribution channel of a major concert ticket.

**1. The Chain of Command**

  - Identify the **3 parties** involved in a standard ticket sale (The Producer, The Intermediary, The Consumer).

**2. The Venue Impact**

  - Why does the **Venue** (Stadium vs. Club) matter for distribution?

**3. The Ethics of the "Secondary Market"**

  - **Scenario:** A scalper uses a "Bot" to buy 100 tickets in 1 second, then resells them for double the price.
  - **Question:** Is this smart business (Supply and Demand) or unethical behavior? Should it be illegal?
  - **Response:** Write a 100 word opinion piece. Defend your stance.

Submit your analysis as a Text Entry.
