## LESSON 18: MAKE AN OLYMPIC SPORT (PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT)

**Unit:** Sports Product Planning and Sponsorship

**Duration:** 2 to 3 Class Periods (Project)

**Points:** 50

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Managing Sports Products

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **010:** Collaborate with team members.
  - **027:** Identify target markets.
  - **082:** Describe the development of a sports product.
  - **087:** Explain sponsorship and endorsements.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"The Olympics recently added Breakdancing, Skateboarding, and Surfing. If you were in charge of the IOC, what one video game would you add to the roster to get teenagers to watch?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"The Olympics are a business. The IOC is desperate for the 'next big thing' that appeals to your generation. Today, you are product developers pitching the first official E-Sport of the Olympic Games."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Focus on the **Sponsor Alignment**. Award points for explaining the *strategy* behind the sponsor choice, not just listing a famous brand.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Why does a TV network care more about *who* is watching (Target Market) than *how many* people are watching? Explain in one sentence."

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Project: The First E-Sports Olympic Games

**Description:**

You are responsible for creating a new sport to be introduced as a competition at the first E-Sports Olympic Games. As part of this process, you will also be identifying potential sponsors.

**The Task:**

Choose a partner. Create a presentation or proposal addressing the three sections below.

**Section 1: The New Sport (Product Development)**

  - **Name & Concept:** What is it called? How does it work?
  - **The Rules:** What are the rules of competition?
  - **The Appeal:** Why would this be a good event for the Olympics?

**Section 2: The Fan (Target Market)**

  - **Demographics:** Who is watching?
  - **Psychographics:** Why will your event appeal to this group?

**Section 3: The Money (Sponsorships)**

  - **Select Sponsors:** Identify specific companies that would want to pay to be associated with your sport.
  - **The Strategy:** Why would they be likely sponsors?
  - **Advertising:** How will advertising play a role in the event?

**Submission:**

Submit your project as a File Upload.
