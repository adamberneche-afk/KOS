## LESSON 19: ALBUM RELEASE PROJECT (THE DROP)

**Unit:** Entertainment Marketing and Product Management

**Duration:** 3 Class Periods + Presentation Day

**Points:** 100 (Project)

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Managing Sports and Entertainment Products

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **033:** Describe the product life cycle.
  - **077:** Explain factors motivating fans.
  - **083:** Describe the development of an entertainment product.
  - **092:** Create a promotional plan.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now - Day 1):**

"Vinyl records are back. Why would someone pay $30 for inconvenient plastic when Spotify is free? Write down one reason."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Today, you are not just students; you are the label executives. You have to take a raw audio file and turn it into a physical object that people want to hold, own, and display."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Use the **Checkpoints** to pace the grading.

  - **Day 1 (Product):** Pass/Fail check. Did they alter the sleeve AND the record?
  - **Day 2 (Timeline):** Spot check the "7 activities."
  - **Day 3 (Presentation):** Grade the "Cohesive Branding."

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket - Day 2):**

"Explain why we need a 'Public Appearance' in the promotional mix. Why isn't social media enough?"

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Project: The Album Drop (Product and Promotion)

**Description:**

After months in the studio, your band’s album is finally ready to be released. Now you just have to make people care\! You will be provided with a vinyl record for this assignment.

**Day 1 Checkpoint: The Product and The Brand**

  - **Alter the Sleeve:** Design a cover that clearly displays the Artist Name and Album Name.
  - **Alter the Vinyl:** Modify the record itself.
  - **Value Check:** Every alteration should add "perceived value."

**Day 2 Checkpoint: The Hype Cycle**

  - **Timeline:** Map out 2 months (1 month before release, 1 month after).
  - **Schedule:** Plan **7 unique promotional activities per week**.
  - **Required Events:** 1 Public Appearance, 1 Fan Interaction, 1 Interview.

**Day 3 Checkpoint: The Pitch**

  - **Presentation:** Create a pitch outlining your plan.
  - **Include:** Customer Profile, Branding Message, Ad Mockup, PR Talking Points, and Measurable Success Metrics.
