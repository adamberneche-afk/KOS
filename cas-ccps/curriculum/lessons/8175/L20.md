## LESSON 20: MY CAUSE MY CLEATS (PARTNERSHIP ANALYSIS)

**Unit:** Sponsorship and Public Relations

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 20

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Demonstrating Professional Competencies

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **002:** Demonstrate critical thinking and problem solving.
  - **008:** Demonstrate respect for diversity.
  - **011:** Demonstrate big-picture thinking.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Display three famous logos (e.g., Nike, Red Cross, Pepsi). Ask students to write down one word they feel when they see each logo."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"If you had a national TV audience for three hours, what is one message or charity you would put on your shoes for the world to see?"

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Scan student responses for the "Why." If the student identifies the athlete, the charity, and provides a genuine reason for the partnership, award full points.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"In one sentence, explain why it is smarter for a brand to partner with a charity than to just run a normal commercial."

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: My Cause My Cleats (The Power of Strategic Partnership)

**Description:**

When an athlete partners with a non profit, it is a powerful form of Co Branding. It builds their public image while driving massive awareness for a cause. We are looking at the NFL "My Cause My Cleats" campaign.

**The Task:**

Select one NFL athlete and the Non Profit organization they represented. Answer the following in a minimum of 100 words:

  - **Who** is the athlete and **what** is the organization?
  - **The Why:** Explain the connection. Did the athlete choose this cause because of a personal experience?
  - **The Impact:** From a marketing perspective, how does this partnership help the athlete's personal brand and the non profit organization?

**Submission:**

Submit your analysis as a Text Entry.
