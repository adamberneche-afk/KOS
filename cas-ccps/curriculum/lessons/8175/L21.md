## LESSON 21: WHO OWNS IT? (LICENSING AND LAW)

**Unit:** Legal Issues and Sponsorship

**Duration:** 1 to 2 Class Periods

**Points:** 40

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Exploring the Concept of Branding; Exploring Sponsorship and Endorsements

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **058:** Explain product licensing and brand protection (Licensing and protecting a team's brand).
  - **091:** Describe the components and relationships between stakeholders (Who gains in a naming-rights deal: the school, the brand and the fans).
  - **087:** Explain sponsorship and endorsements as they relate to the sports, entertainment, and event industries (Naming rights as a sponsorship).
  - **089:** Identify legal restrictions placed on endorsements and sponsorships (Ambush marketing and the rules that protect official sponsors).

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Look at the Madden NFL video game. How much money do you think the NFL gets just for letting EA Sports use their logo? (Answer: About 1.5 Billion dollars)."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"If I put a Nike Swoosh on a t-shirt I made in my basement and sold it, I would go to jail. That is trademark infringement. But if I pay Nike for the right to use it, that is **Licensing**. Today, we look at the biggest contracts in sports: Naming Rights and Ambush Marketing."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Check for the definition of **Ambush Marketing**.

  - **Correct:** A brand trying to look like a sponsor without paying for it.
  - **The Bid:** In the stadium activity, award creativity points for the "activation" ideas.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"What is the difference between a 'Sponsor' (who pays) and an 'Ambush Marketer' (who sneaks in)?"

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: The Naming Rights Bid and Ambush Marketing

**Description:**

Sports teams make massive amounts of money by selling the name of their stadium. This is called **Naming Rights**. Other brands try to cheat the system by advertising *near* the event without paying the fee. This is called **Ambush Marketing**.

**The Task:**

Complete the two sections below.

**Part 1: The Naming Rights Bid**

  - **Scenario:** Clover Hill High School is selling the naming rights to the football stadium.
  - **Select a Brand:** Choose a real company.
  - **The Pitch:** Write a 5 sentence proposal. Why does this brand want their name on our field?

**Part 2: Ambush Marketing Analysis**

  - **Define It:** What is Ambush Marketing?
  - **The Example:** Brands finding ways to get on camera without paying the Olympic Committee.
  - **The Verdict:** Is Ambush Marketing genius or dishonest? Explain your answer in 2 sentences.

**Submission:**

Submit your Bid and Analysis as a Text Entry.
