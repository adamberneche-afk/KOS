## LESSON 22: MY CAUSE MY CLEATS (THE CAMPAIGN PITCH)

**Unit:** Promotion and Product Planning

**Duration:** 2 to 3 Class Periods

**Points:** 50 (Project)

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Promoting Sports, Entertainment, and Events

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **001:** Demonstrate creativity and innovation.
  - **050:** Develop presentation skills.
  - **087:** Explain sponsorship and endorsements.
  - **093:** Describe the elements of sports promotional campaigns.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Look at your own shoes. If those shoes could talk, what would they say about the person wearing them?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"You’ve researched the athlete. You understand the cause. Now, you are the **Brand Manager**. It is your job to design the visuals and the strategy for the campaign."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Utilize a checklist approach.

  - Cleat design included?
  - Charity identified?
  - Fundraising idea included?

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"What is the single most important visual element on your cleat design, and why did you choose that color or symbol?"

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Project: My Cause My Cleats (The Campaign Pitch)

**Description:**

It is your job to design the visuals and the strategy for the "My Cause My Cleats" campaign. You are not just designing a shoe; you are designing a message that needs to raise money and awareness.

**Deliverables:**

Create a slide presentation (approx. 5 to 7 slides) that "pitches" your concept to the NFL.

**1. The Partners**

  - Identify the Athlete and the Non Profit.
  - Explain the "Why."

**2. The Strategy**

  - **Awareness:** How will these cleats get attention?
  - **Fundraising:** Propose **one specific idea** to raise money.

**3. The Visuals**

  - **The Cleats:** Insert a picture of your final cleat design.
  - **The Merch:** Create **one promotional item** that visually represents three brands at once: The Athlete, The Non Profit, and The NFL Team.

**Submission:**

Submit your presentation file.
