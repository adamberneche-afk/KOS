## LESSON 23: INDUSTRY PUBLICATION PROJECT (THE ZINE)

**Unit:** Product and Promotion Management

**Duration:** 5 Class Periods (Project)

**Points:** 100

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Demonstrating Professional Competencies

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **001:** Demonstrate creativity and innovation.
  - **010:** Collaborate with team members.
  - **014:** Manage time and resources.
  - **050:** Develop presentation skills.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now - Day 1):**

"Why do magazines like ESPN or Vogue still exist when everything is online? What is the difference between reading a printed 'Zine' and scrolling through a feed?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"You are not just students today; you are Editors-in-Chief. You are going to build a physical publication from scratch. This is a marathon, not a sprint, so you will need to manage your time and your team to make the deadline."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Use the **Checkpoints** to reduce your grading load. Grade Day 1 and 2 for completion. Grade the final product on "Professionalism" of binding/layout.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket - Day 4):**

"What is the hardest part of working in a group on a long-term project?"

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Project: The Industry Publication (Create Your Own Magazine)

**Description:**

Over the next several class periods, you will produce a professional publication (Magazine/Zine) that focuses on an industry of your choosing.

**The Requirements:**

  - **Cover:** Attractive, eye-catching front cover.
  - **Articles:** One article per group member (Must include one Opinion Piece and one News Piece).
  - **Advertisements:** One colorful, eye-catching advertisement per group member.
  - **Targeting:** All content must be relevant to your demographic.

**The Schedule (Checkpoints):**

  - **Day 1 (The Plan):** Form groups. Assign roles. Submit Topic and Target Market.
  - **Day 2 (The Draft):** Write articles. Complete a Peer Review.
  - **Day 3 (The Ads):** Create advertisement pieces.
  - **Day 4 (The Layout):** Compile the pieces.
  - **Day 5 (The Presentation):** Bring your finished, bound project to class. Give a group presentation highlighting your demographic, articles, and pricing strategy.

**Submission:**

Submit a digital PDF of your publication here, and hand in the physical copy.
