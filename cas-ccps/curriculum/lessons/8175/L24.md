## LESSON 24: THE ART OF THE SALE (METHODS AND PROCESS)

**Unit:** Promotion and Sales Strategy

**Duration:** 1 to 2 Class Periods

**Points:** 30

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Understanding Promotion

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **007:** Demonstrate listening and speaking skills.
  - **010:** Collaborate with team members.
  - **058:** Describe the types of promotion (Personal Selling).

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"When was the last time you bought something you did not plan to buy? Was it because of an ad you saw (Non-Personal) or a person who talked to you (Personal)?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Selling is not just for car salesmen. If you want a raise, a date, or a better grade, you are selling. Today, we are going to learn the difference between selling through a screen and selling face-to-face."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

This assignment has three parts.

  - **The Worksheet:** Check for the "Benefits vs. Negatives" analysis.
  - **The Process:** Ensure they listed the 6 steps correctly.
  - **The Roleplay:** Grade the written reflection based on whether they acknowledge that "Selling is listening."

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Which method of selling (Personal or Non-Personal) do you think is more effective for selling a luxury car? Explain why."

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Sales Training Camp (Methods and Roleplay)

**Description:**

There are two main ways products are sold: Personal Selling and Non-Personal Selling. To be effective in business, you must understand the pros and cons of both.

**The Task:**

Complete the three sections below based on the class lecture.

**Part 1: Methods of Selling Analysis**

  - **Personal Selling:** Define it. List 2 Benefits and 2 Negatives.
  - **Non-Personal Selling:** Define it. List 2 Benefits and 2 Negatives.

**Part 2: The 6 Steps of a Sale**

  - List the **6 Steps of the Selling Process** in order.
  - **Key Concept:** Complete this sentence: "Selling is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_."

**Part 3: The "No, No, Yes" Roleplay**

  - **Instructions:** Get into groups of two. Act as Salesperson and Customer.
  - **The Rule:** The Customer must reject the Salesperson **twice** before saying "Yes."
  - **Reflection:** How did it feel to be rejected twice? Did you have to change your strategy?

**Submission:**

Submit your responses as a Text Entry.
