## LESSON 25: BHM ENTREPRENEUR VALENTINE

**Unit:** Diversity and Information Literacy

**Duration:** 1 Class Period (Extra Credit or Summative)

**Points:** 10

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Demonstrating Respect for Diversity

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **001:** Demonstrate creativity and innovation.
  - **008:** Demonstrate respect for diversity.
  - **015:** Demonstrate information-literacy skills.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Name one invention or product you used this morning. Do you know who created it? Why do we often know the product name but not the business person behind it?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Who knows who invented the Super Soaker? (Answer: Lonnie Johnson). Today we are honoring the people who invented the businesses we love."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

This is designed as a quick check.

  - Did they include 3 facts?
  - Is it a card format?

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Which entrepreneur did you research, and what is one challenge they had to overcome?"

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Extra Credit: Black History Month Entrepreneur Valentine

**Description:**

For this opportunity, you will explore the intersection of history and business by researching a Black entrepreneur who has made a significant impact on their industry.

**Requirements:**

  - **Select a Subject:** Choose one Black entrepreneur.
  - **Conduct Research:** Gather information regarding their background and business ventures.
  - **Design a Card:** Create a Valentine's Day card dedicated to them.
  - **Content Integration:** You must incorporate **three specific facts** into the card's design or text.
