## LESSON 26: PREPARING YOUR ELEVATOR PITCH

**Unit:** Career Readiness and Networking

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 25

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Developing Job Search Skills

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **007:** Demonstrate listening and speaking skills.
  - **012:** Demonstrate career and life management skills.
  - **109:** Create a networking plan.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Studies show it takes only 7 seconds to make a first impression. If you met your dream employer right now, what is the very first thing you would say after 'Hello'?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Imagine you step into an elevator. The doors close, and you realize the only other person in there is the CEO of a major record label. You have exactly 30 seconds until the doors open again. Today, we write the script for that 30 seconds."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Check for specificity. In the "Strengths" and "Achievements" boxes, look for specific examples (e.g., "Led a team of 5 students") rather than vague adjectives.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"What is the 'Interesting Fact' about yourself that you included? Why do you think that specific fact makes you memorable?"

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Preparing Your Elevator Pitch (The 30 Second Commercial)

**Description:**

You only get one chance to make a great first impression\! An "Elevator Pitch" is a quick summary of your background and experience.

**The Task:**

Fill in **at least two to three specific examples** for each category below.

  - **Education:** Courses that inspired you.
  - **Top Strengths:** Specific skills.
  - **Achievements:** Awards, certifications, personal bests.
  - **Academic Projects:** Research projects or events you planned.
  - **Target Organizations:** What type of companies interest you?
  - **Inspiration:** Who do you look up to?
  - **Interesting Facts:** What sets you apart from the typical student?

Submit your completed document as a File Upload.
