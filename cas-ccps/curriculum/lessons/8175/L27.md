## LESSON 27: SPRING BREAK FIELD REPORT (MARKETING IN THE WILD)

**Unit:** Real-World Application and Promotion

**Duration:** 1 Class Period (Reflection Activity)

**Points:** 25

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Promoting Sports, Entertainment, and Events

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **002:** Demonstrate critical thinking.
  - **057:** Explain the role of promotion.
  - **088:** Explain the use of social media in sports marketing.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Did you buy anything over break because you saw it on TikTok, Instagram, or a billboard? If not, what is the last ad you remember seeing? Why did it stick?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Marketing does not stop when school stops. In fact, Spring Break is the 'Super Bowl' for brands targeting students. You were the target for the last seven days. Today, we are going to look back and see if they hit the bullseye."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Focus on the **"Process Description"** section. Did they try to explain *how* the ad was made? Award points for students who think about the work behind the ad.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Based on what you saw over break, is marketing getting more aggressive or more subtle? Give one reason why."

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Spring Break Field Report (Marketing in the Wild)

**Description:**

You have just spent a week away from school, but you were likely surrounded by business the entire time.

**The Task:**

Using at least **200 words**, write a reflection on the marketing you encountered over Spring Break.

**1. The Observation (The What)**

  - Tell me specific examples of marketing that you noticed (e.g., sponsored posts, billboards, movie trailers).

**2. The Reverse Engineering (The How)**

  - Describe the processes that you imagine went into the development of this marketing campaign.
  - How did they get it from an idea to what you experienced? Who had to approve the budget? Who designed the graphics?

**Submission:**

Submit your reflection as a Text Entry.
