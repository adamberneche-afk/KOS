## LESSON 28: MARKET RESEARCH (SURVEY DESIGN)

**Unit:** Marketing Research and Technology

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 20

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Exploring the Marketing Plan

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **002:** Demonstrate critical thinking (Avoiding bias).
  - **017:** Maintain working knowledge of IT systems (Google Forms).
  - **103:** Describe the importance of research.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"If I asked you, 'Why is our school the best school in the state?'—what is wrong with that question? Reshape it to be neutral."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"In marketing, bad data is worse than no data. If you ask biased questions, you get comfortable lies instead of hard truths. Today, we are going to learn how to ask questions so we can build a product people actually want."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Check for the question types immediately.

  - **Quantitative (2):** Are there multiple choice or scale questions?
  - **Qualitative (1):** Is there an open-ended text box question?
  - **Bias Check:** Read one question aloud. If it leads the witness, deduct points.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Explain the difference between Qualitative Data (Stories) and Quantitative Data (Numbers)."

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Designing Unbiased Surveys (Google Forms)

**Description:**

We are in the process of designing a new service. To do this successfully, we need to know what the customer wants. However, asking the wrong questions leads to wrong answers.

**The Task:**

  - **Create:** Open Google Forms and start a new blank form.
  - **Design Questions:** Create **three** survey questions.

<!-- end list -->

  - **Requirement A:** Include **two quantitative questions** (Multiple Choice or Scale).
  - **Requirement B:** Include **one qualitative question** (Open-ended paragraph).

<!-- end list -->

  - **Bias Check:** Review your questions. Do they suggest the "correct" answer? If so, rewrite them to be neutral.
