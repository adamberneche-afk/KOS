## LESSON 29: SURVEY REFLECTION

**Unit:** Marketing Research and Analysis

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 20

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Exploring the Marketing Plan

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **010:** Collaborate with team members.
  - **103:** Describe the importance of research.
  - **105:** Identify the components of a SWOT analysis.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Imagine you are a chef and you just cooked a new meal. Why is it important to taste it yourself before you serve it to a paying customer? Translate this logic to our marketing survey."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Right now, our class survey is a 'Frankenstein' monster. We all threw in different questions, styles, and formats. Before we send this out to the real world, we have to audit it. Today, we are doing a SWOT analysis on our own work."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Focus on the **Specific Changes** section. Award points only for actionable advice (e.g., "Change Question 4 to multiple choice").

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Why is it costlier to find an error in a survey *after* it has been sent out compared to finding it *before*?"
