### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Imagine you are selling a new energy drink. You *assume* your target market is teenage athletes. What happens to your business if you are wrong and your actual customers are tired office workers? How would you find out before you waste money on ads?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"In marketing, assumptions are dangerous. You might *think* you know who your customer is, but until you ask them, you are just guessing. Today, we stop guessing. You are going to build the tool that proves (or disproves) the market segments we developed last week."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Check the structure. Are there exactly **10 Closed Questions** and **2 Open Questions**? Do the questions actually ask about the customer's identity?

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Why do we need 'Closed Questions' (Multiple Choice) to verify segments? Why can't we just ask 12 open-ended questions?"
