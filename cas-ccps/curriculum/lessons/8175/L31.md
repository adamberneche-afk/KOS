## LESSON 31: SURVEY ANALYSIS (DATA INTO ACTION)

**Unit:** Marketing Research and Management

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 25

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Exploring the Marketing Plan

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **002:** Demonstrate critical thinking.
  - **019:** Apply mathematical skills to job-specific tasks.
  - **103:** Describe the importance of research.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"If 80% of students say they hate the cafeteria pizza, but the sales data shows it is the \#1 selling item, what is the truth?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Data is just noise until you tell a story with it. You have collected the answers. You have the charts. Now, you have to stop being researchers and start being decision-makers. Use the data to fix the problems."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Focus on the **Citation** aspect. Did they connect the suggestion to the data? Award full points only if the data *justifies* the decision.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Why is it safer for a business to make a decision based on data rather than 'gut feeling'?"

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Survey Analysis (Data Driven Decisions)

**Description:**

Collecting data is useless unless you use it to make improvements. Today, you will review the results of our class survey and act as a consultant to improve the operation of our league/event.

**The Task:**

Review the Google Forms summary charts and responses. Then, write a response addressing the following:

**1. The Data (3 Points of Interest)**

  - Using **100 words** (minimum), describe **three specific data points** from the survey that you find interesting or surprising.

**2. The Suggestion (Action Plan)**

  - Give **one concrete suggestion** for how our league/event should be organized or operated differently based on this feedback.

**3. The Justification (Cite Your Sources)**

  - You must **cite the survey data** to justify your suggestion.

**Submission:**

Submit your analysis as a Text Entry.
