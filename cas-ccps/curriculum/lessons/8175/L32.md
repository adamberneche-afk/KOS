## LESSON 32: AI AND THE FUTURE OF WORK

**Unit:** Career Readiness and Professional Development

**Duration:** 1 to 2 Class Periods

**Points:** 30

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Demonstrating Professional Competencies

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **013:** Demonstrate continuous learning and adaptability.
  - **015:** Demonstrate information-literacy skills.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"Raise your hand if you have used AI this week. In one sentence, write down what you used it for. Did it save you time, or did it just do the thinking for you?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"There is a prediction that 85 million jobs will disappear by 2025 due to automation, but 97 million new ones will be created. The question isn't 'Will a robot take my job?' The question is, 'Will I be the person who knows how to manage the robot?'"

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Check the bibliography first. Are there 3 distinct sources? Are the "5 Fastest Growing Industries" listed clearly?

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Based on your research today, does the future of AI make you feel excited or nervous? Give one reason why."

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Future Proofing Your Career (AI and the Job Market)

**Description:**

Artificial Intelligence is shifting the employment landscape faster than any technology since the internet. To succeed in this new economy, you must be adaptable.

**The Task:**

Conduct research on how AI will change the job market over the next 10 years.

**1. The Essay (200 Words)**

  - Provide a 200 word essay describing the predicted changes to the workforce.
  - Explain specifically how you can ensure that you are properly prepared for this changing landscape.

**2. The Data**

  - List the **five fastest growing industries** predicted for the next decade.
  - Identify **one specific job** within these industries that you would be interested in researching further.

**3. The Sources**

  - You must cite **at least three different sources** used in your research.

**Submission:**

Submit your work as a File Upload or Text Entry.
