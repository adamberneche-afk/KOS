## LESSON 33: THE PAPER TRAIL (RESUMES AND APPLICATIONS)

**Unit:** Career Readiness

**Duration:** 1 to 2 Class Periods

**Points:** 50

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Developing Job Search Skills

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **110:** Develop a résumé and cover letter.
  - **111:** Complete a job application.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"You are a hiring manager. You have a stack of 100 applications. How long do you look at each one before deciding 'Yes' or 'No'? (Answer: Average is 6 seconds). What are you looking for?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"You might be the best employee in the world, but if you look bad on paper, you will never get the chance to prove it. Your resume is your personal flyer. Today, we are going to build a resume that survives the '6-Second Test.'"

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Grade the **Formatting**. Zero tolerance for typos. Ensure consistency in dates and fonts.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"Why should you never put your home address on a modern resume posted online?"

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: The 6-Second Resume

**Description:**

Recruiters spend an average of **6 seconds** looking at a resume before deciding to keep it or trash it. Your resume must be clean, professional, and easy to read.

**The Task:**

Create a one page professional resume.

**Requirements:**

  - **Header:** Name, Professional Email, Phone Number, LinkedIn URL.
  - **Education:** School, Graduation Date, GPA.
  - **Experience:** List any jobs, volunteer work, or club leadership.
  - **Skills:** List Soft Skills and Hard Skills.
  - **The "Action Verb" Rule:** Every bullet point under your experience must start with an Action Verb.

**Submission:**

Submit your Resume as a PDF file.
