## LESSON 34: INTERVIEW SKILLS PRACTICE PLAN

**Unit:** Career Readiness and Job Search Skills

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 20

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Demonstrating Personal Qualities

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **003:** Demonstrate initiative and self-direction.
  - **112:** Describe the job interview process.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"What is a 'nervous habit' (like biting nails, saying 'like' too much) that you or someone you know does when stressed? Do you think interviewers notice these?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Athletes watch game film to fix their form. Yet, most people walk into an interview having never practiced or watched what 'good' looks like. Today, you are going to watch the tape, spot the skills, and build a plan to master them."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Focus on the **"Plan"** aspect. Did they list specific ways they will practice (e.g., "I will record myself answering questions")?

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"List one specific question from the video that you currently do NOT have a good answer for. How will you fix that by tomorrow?"

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: Interview Skills Practice Plan

**Description:**

Knowing *what* to say in an interview is only half the battle. *How* you say it—your body language, your tone, and your confidence—matters just as much.

**The Task:**

  - **Watch:** Watch the Video on Interview Skills.
  - **Plan:** Write a **100-word plan** to practice these skills prior to an interview opportunity.
  - **Reflect:**

<!-- end list -->

  - What did you learn from the video that was not expected?
  - How did this experience help shape your future career plans?
  - What is something specific you could do that would help you in an interview setting?

Submit your plan as a Text Entry.
