## LESSON 35: GOOGLE INTERVIEW SIMULATION (AI PREP)

**Unit:** Career Readiness and Job Search Skills

**Duration:** 1 Class Period

**Points:** 25

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Developing Job Search Skills

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **007:** Demonstrate listening and speaking skills.
  - **013:** Demonstrate continuous learning.
  - **112:** Describe the job interview process.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"What is the single scariest question you could be asked in a job interview? Why does it make you nervous?"

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Google receives millions of applications a year and hires about 0.2 percent of them. It is statistically harder to get a job at Google than it is to get into Harvard. Today, we are going to use Google's own AI tool to practice."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Check for volume and revision. Are there **5 distinct questions** listed? Do the answers look polished?

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"What is one specific piece of feedback the AI gave you about your speaking style?"

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Assignment: The Google Interview Challenge

**Description:**

Congratulations\! You have landed an interview\! Since we cannot fly to California today, we will use the **Google Interview Warmup** tool to simulate the process.

**The Task:**

  - **Access the Tool:** Go to Google Interview Warmup.
  - **The Process:** There are five questions that you will answer verbally.
  - **Review the Feedback:** After answering, the tool will analyze your response.
  - **Refine Your Answer:** Use the feedback to create a "perfected" written version of your answer.

**Submission:**

Copy and paste the **Question** and your **Final Answer** for all five questions into the text submission box.
