## LESSON 36: COURSE EVALUATION BROCHURE (THE EXIT INTERVIEW)

**Unit:** Professional Development and Capstone Review

**Duration:** 1 to 2 Class Periods

**Points:** 40

### I. COMPETENCY ALIGNMENT

**Duty Areas:** Promoting Sports, Entertainment, and Events

**Specific Competencies Covered:**

  - **001:** Demonstrate creativity and innovation.
  - **020:** Demonstrate professionalism.
  - **095:** Evaluate a promotional campaign.

### II. TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS

**Bell Ringer (The Do Now):**

"What is the difference between 'constructive criticism' and 'complaining'? Write one example of each regarding a teacher or a coach."

**The Hook (Classroom Opener):**

"Companies pay millions of dollars for 'Market Research' to find out what customers really think. Today, I am paying you in points. This is your chance to tell me the truth about this year—but you have to do it like a marketer."

**Quick Grading Tip:**

Focus on **Panel Four (Overview of Mr. Berneche)**. Did they list "Pros and Cons" respectfully? Award points for honesty if it is phrased professionally.

**Checkout Slip (Exit Ticket):**

"If you could remove exactly one assignment from the curriculum next year, which one would it be and why?"

### 

### III. CANVAS ASSIGNMENT TEXT (STUDENT VIEW)

**Title:** Project: The Course Brochure (Honest Feedback)

**Description:**

You will use your Marketing knowledge while providing Mr. Berneche **honest** feedback about the course you have experienced this year.

**The Task:**

Create a brochure for this course using a template or Canva.

**Brochure Requirements:**

  - **Front Cover:** A creative Logo.
  - **Panel TWO:** Showcase a **memorable project** from the course.
  - **Panel THREE:** What are **three careers** this class might prepare you for?
  - **Panel FOUR:** The Teacher Review (Pros and Cons of the teaching approach).
  - **Panel FIVE:** Share "Student Reviews" (quotes).
  - **Back Panel:** Contact Info for Mr. Berneche.

**Submission:**

Submit your Brochure as a PDF or Image file.
