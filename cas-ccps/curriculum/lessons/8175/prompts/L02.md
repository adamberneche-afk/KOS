## Prompt

Assignment: The Passion Test (Intrinsic Motivation)

Description:

To be successful in a high energy field like Sports and Entertainment Marketing, you need "drive." But drive comes from two places: Extrinsic Motivation (money, grades, rewards) and Intrinsic Motivation (doing something because you love it). The most successful people build careers around things they are inherently motivated to do.

The Task:

Please answer the following question in 2 to 3 sentences:

What is something that you do because you are inherently motivated to do it?

- It can be related to any of your interests (sports, art, coding, organizing, helping people), but please keep it school appropriate.
- Explain *why* you enjoy it.

Submission:

Submit your response as a Text Entry.

## Warm-up

What is one thing you would do for free for the rest of your life if money was not an issue? Be specific.
