## Prompt

Assignment: Defining Your Personal Brand (The CEO of YOU)

Description:

It is important to understand your own personal brand. When you understand who you are and what you stand for, you can find new opportunities and ways to strengthen your weaknesses. A brand statement also helps others understand who you are, what you are great at, and how you differentiate yourself from others.

The Task:

Answer the questions below to create your personal brand statement. Be honest and dig deep.

1. Your Value

- What value can you provide the world (or people) with your strengths and attributes?

2. Your USP (Unique Selling Proposition)

- What is your Unique Selling Proposition?
- What is the one thing that makes you stand out from the crowd? Why should a team pick you over the 100 other people applying for the job?

3. Your Audience

- Who do you want to help, serve, or would benefit the most from what you can offer?

Submission:

Submit your answers in a text entry box or a Google Doc.

## Warm-up

Write down three adjectives that your friends would use to describe you. Now, write down three adjectives that a teacher or boss would use to describe you. Are they different? Why?
