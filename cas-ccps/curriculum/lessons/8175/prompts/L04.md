## Prompt

Assignment: Personal Brand Analysis (The Celebrity Case Study)

Description:

Everybody has a personal brand. Whether you realize it or not, your actions mold your personal brand. You will be taking a closer look at a public figure's personal brand to see how they manage their reputation and career.

The Task:

Answer the following questions about the individual's personal brand. Please write in complete sentences.

1. The Subject

- Who is the public figure you chose?
- What makes this individual well known?

2. The SWOT Analysis

- What is a major Strength this individual has?
- What is a Weakness or challenge they face?
- What is an Opportunity they could use to grow their brand?
- What is a Threat that could damage their brand?

3. The Public Perception

- How do others describe this person?
- Does their public reputation match who they really seem to be?

4. The Core Drivers

- What do you think motivates this individual?
- What do you think they are passionate about outside of their job?

Submission:

Submit your responses in a text entry box.

## Warm-up

Think of a celebrity or athlete who 'ruined' their reputation recently. Without naming names, write down one action they took that damaged their brand. How hard do you think it will be for them to fix it?
