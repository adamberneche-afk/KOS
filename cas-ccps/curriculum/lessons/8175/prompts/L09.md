## Prompt

Assignment: What IS Branding? (The Mentor Challenge)

Description:

We have spent the year looking at logos, slogans, and the emotional connections companies make with consumers. Now, it is time to synthesize that information.

The Scenario:

Imagine that a younger student, or perhaps a relative with absolutely no business background, comes up to you and asks: "I keep hearing people talk about 'branding,' but I don't get it. What actually IS branding?"

The Task:

Step into the role of the teacher. Write out your response to this person (Minimum 250 words).

Structure:

- The Introduction: Start by telling them what branding is not. Define it simply.
- The Examples: Use a real example (like Apple or Coca Cola). Explain *why* people buy that brand.
- The Promise: Explain how a brand is a promise to the customer. That trust is branding.
- The Conclusion: Wrap it up by telling them why it matters.

Submission:

Submit your response as a Text Entry.

## Warm-up

Try to describe the color 'Blue' to someone who has been blind their whole life. You cannot use visual words like 'sky' or 'ocean.' You have to use feelings or other senses.
