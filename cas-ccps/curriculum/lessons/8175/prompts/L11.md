## Prompt

Assignment: CHHS Brand Audit (The Home Field Advantage)

Description:

You walk the halls every day, but have you ever looked at Clover Hill High School as a brand? Just like a sports team or a clothing line, our school has an image, a target audience, and a reputation.

The Task:

Conduct a brand audit of Clover Hill High School. Write a 250 word (minimum) essay covering the following three areas.

1. Target Market Identification (Min. 75 words)

- Who are we serving?
- Identify the target market (Demographics and Psychographics).

2. Brand Quality Assessment (Min. 100 words)

- Assess the quality of the current "Cavalier" brand.
- What do the logo, the colors, and the reputation say to the community?
- Determine specific areas for improvement.

3. Cultural and Societal Trends (Min. 75 words)

- Analyze current cultural and societal trends that may impact rebranding efforts (e.g., social media, mental health awareness).

Submission:

Submit your essay as a Text Entry.

## Warm-up

Write down the first three words that come to your mind when I say 'Clover Hill High School.' Do not overthink it. Be honest.
