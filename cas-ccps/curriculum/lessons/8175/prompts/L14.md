## Prompt

Project: Local Business Deep Dive (The Consultant's Report)

Description:

You will act as a consultant analyzing a local business in the Sports and Entertainment industry (e.g., Movie Theaters, Bowling Alleys, Music Venues, Laser Tag).

The Task Overview:

- Day 1: Research and Scripting: Select your business. Analyze their Brand, Product, and Offerings. Write a word-for-word Script.
- Day 2: Slide Deck Creation: Use your script to create a slide deck. Follow best practices (Minimal text).
- Day 3: Peer Feedback Loop: Present to a partner. Provide and receive feedback.
- Day 4: The Pitch: Presentations are shared with the class.

Key Analysis Points:

- Brand Analysis: What is their reputation?
- Future Forecast: What changes would you expect for this business in the next 5 years based on AI and trends?
- Ownership: Is the business a sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation, LLC or franchise? How does that change who makes decisions and who takes the risk?
- Behind the Scenes: In one or two sentences each, explain how the business plans, how it is managed, how it handles money, the skills its workers need, the technology it runs on, one labor issue, and one health, safety or environmental issue.

Submission:

Submit your Script (Day 1) and Final Presentation (Day 4).

## Warm-up

Think of your favorite local spot to hang out on a Friday night (bowling, movies, skating). Why do you go there instead of staying home? Is it the product or the environment?
