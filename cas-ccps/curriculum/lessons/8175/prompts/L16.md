## Prompt

Assignment: The Pizza Break-Even Challenge

Description:

Setting a price isn't just guessing. It is a mathematical balance between covering your costs and what the customer is willing to pay.

The Task:

You are opening a concession stand at the football stadium selling Pizza. You need to determine your price.

Part 1: The Math (Break-Even Analysis)

- Data: Fixed Costs: 500 dollars. Variable Cost (per pizza): 5 dollars.
- Scenario A: You sell the pizza for 10 dollars. How many pizzas must you sell to cover your 500 dollar rent? (Show your work).
- Scenario B: You lower the price to 7 dollars. How many pizzas must you sell *now* to cover your rent?

Part 2: The Strategy

- Question: Which pricing strategy would you use for a *new* pizza brand?

- Price Skimming: Setting a high price to look exclusive.
- Market Penetration: Setting a low price to get people to try it.
- Justification: Explain your choice in 2 sentences.
- Bundle It: Create a combo (for example, a slice and a drink) and give it a price. Explain why a fan would buy the bundle instead of the pizza alone.
- The Rest of the Mix: Price is one of the Ps. In one sentence each, describe your stand's product, place and promotion.

Submission:

Submit your calculations and strategy as a Text Entry.
