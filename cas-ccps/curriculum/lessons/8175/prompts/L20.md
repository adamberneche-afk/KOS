## Prompt

Assignment: My Cause My Cleats (The Power of Strategic Partnership)

Description:

When an athlete partners with a non profit, it is a powerful form of Co Branding. It builds their public image while driving massive awareness for a cause. We are looking at the NFL "My Cause My Cleats" campaign.

The Task:

Select one NFL athlete and the Non Profit organization they represented. Answer the following in a minimum of 100 words:

- Who is the athlete and what is the organization?
- The Why: Explain the connection. Did the athlete choose this cause because of a personal experience?
- The Impact: From a marketing perspective, how does this partnership help the athlete's personal brand and the non profit organization?

Submission:

Submit your analysis as a Text Entry.
