## Prompt

Assignment: The Naming Rights Bid and Ambush Marketing

Description:

Sports teams make massive amounts of money by selling the name of their stadium. This is called Naming Rights. Other brands try to cheat the system by advertising *near* the event without paying the fee. This is called Ambush Marketing.

The Task:

Complete the two sections below.

Part 1: The Naming Rights Bid

- Scenario: Clover Hill High School is selling the naming rights to the football stadium.
- Select a Brand: Choose a real company.
- The Pitch: Write a 5 sentence proposal. Why does this brand want their name on our field?

Part 2: Ambush Marketing Analysis

- Define It: What is Ambush Marketing?
- The Example: Brands finding ways to get on camera without paying the Olympic Committee.
- The Verdict: Is Ambush Marketing genius or dishonest? Explain your answer in 2 sentences.
- Sponsor or Endorser: Explain the difference between a sponsorship (a brand paying a team, venue or event) and an endorsement (a brand paying a person). Give one reason a brand might choose each.

Submission:

Submit your Bid and Analysis as a Text Entry.
