## Prompt

Project: My Cause My Cleats (The Campaign Pitch)

Description:

It is your job to design the visuals and the strategy for the "My Cause My Cleats" campaign. You are not just designing a shoe; you are designing a message that needs to raise money and awareness.

Deliverables:

Create a slide presentation (approx. 5 to 7 slides) that "pitches" your concept to the NFL.

1. The Partners

- Identify the Athlete and the Non Profit.
- Explain the "Why."

2. The Strategy

- Awareness: How will these cleats get attention?
- Fundraising: Propose one specific idea to raise money.
- The Promotion: Choose one sales promotion for your campaign (a giveaway, contest, coupon or limited-edition item) and give one advantage and one disadvantage of it.

3. The Visuals

- The Cleats: Insert a picture of your final cleat design.
- The Merch: Create one promotional item that visually represents three brands at once: The Athlete, The Non Profit, and The NFL Team.

Submission:

Submit your presentation file.

## Warm-up

Look at your own shoes. If those shoes could talk, what would they say about the person wearing them?
