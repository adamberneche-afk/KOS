## Prompt

Project: The Industry Publication (Create Your Own Magazine)

Description:

Over the next several class periods, you will produce a professional publication (Magazine/Zine) that focuses on an industry of your choosing.

The Requirements:

- Cover: Attractive, eye-catching front cover.
- Articles: One article per group member (Must include one Opinion Piece and one News Piece).
- Advertisements: One colorful, eye-catching advertisement per group member.
- Targeting: All content must be relevant to your demographic.
- Going Digital: In your presentation, explain two ways your magazine could make money online (digital ads, subscriptions, sponsored posts or video).

The Schedule (Checkpoints):

- Day 1 (The Plan): Form groups. Assign roles. Submit Topic and Target Market.
- Day 2 (The Draft): Write articles. Complete a Peer Review.
- Day 3 (The Ads): Create advertisement pieces.
- Day 4 (The Layout): Compile the pieces.
- Day 5 (The Presentation): Bring your finished, bound project to class. Give a group presentation highlighting your demographic, articles, and pricing strategy.

Submission:

Submit a digital PDF of your publication here, and hand in the physical copy.

## Warm-up

Why do magazines like ESPN or Vogue still exist when everything is online? What is the difference between reading a printed 'Zine' and scrolling through a feed?
