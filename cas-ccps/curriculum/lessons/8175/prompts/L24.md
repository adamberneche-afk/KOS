## Prompt

Assignment: Sales Training Camp (Methods and Roleplay)

Description:

There are two main ways products are sold: Personal Selling and Non-Personal Selling. To be effective in business, you must understand the pros and cons of both.

The Task:

Complete the three sections below based on the class lecture.

Part 1: Methods of Selling Analysis

- Personal Selling: Define it. List 2 Benefits and 2 Negatives.
- Non-Personal Selling: Define it. List 2 Benefits and 2 Negatives.

Part 2: The 6 Steps of a Sale

- List the 6 Steps of the Selling Process in order.
- Key Concept: Complete this sentence: "Selling is __________."

Part 3: The "No, No, Yes" Roleplay

- Instructions: Get into groups of two. Act as Salesperson and Customer.
- The Rule: The Customer must reject the Salesperson twice before saying "Yes."
- The Record: Write down what you sold, your opening line, the Customer's two rejections, what you said after each one, and the line that got the "Yes."
- Reflection: How did it feel to be rejected twice? Did you have to change your strategy?

Submission:

Submit your responses as a Text Entry.

## Warm-up

When was the last time you bought something you did not plan to buy? Was it because of an ad you saw (Non-Personal) or a person who talked to you (Personal)?
