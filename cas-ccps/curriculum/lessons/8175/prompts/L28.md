## Prompt

Assignment: Designing Unbiased Surveys (Google Forms)

Description:

We are in the process of designing a new service. To do this successfully, we need to know what the customer wants. However, asking the wrong questions leads to wrong answers.

The Task:

- Create: Open Google Forms and start a new blank form.
- Design Questions: Create three survey questions.

- Requirement A: Include two quantitative questions (Multiple Choice or Scale).
- Requirement B: Include one qualitative question (Open-ended paragraph).

- Bias Check: Review your questions. Do they suggest the "correct" answer? If so, rewrite them to be neutral.

Submission:

Click the "Send" button on your form, copy the link, and paste the URL as your submission.

Below the link, type your three questions exactly as they appear on the form, with the answer choices for the two quantitative questions. Then, in one or two sentences, say which question you rewrote after the Bias Check and what you changed.

## Warm-up

If I asked you, 'Why is our school the best school in the state?'—what is wrong with that question? Reshape it to be neutral.
