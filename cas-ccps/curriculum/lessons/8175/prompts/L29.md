## Prompt

Assignment: Survey Quality Control (The Audit)

Description:

We have compiled all of your individual questions into a single Google Form. We need to ensure the flow is logical, the tone is consistent, and the bias is removed.

The Task:

- Access: Click the link provided to view the current "Draft Version" of the class survey.
- Reflect: Read through the questions as if you were a customer taking the survey.
- Critique: Submit a response addressing the following:

- Strengths: What works well?
- Weaknesses: Identify questions that are confusing or biased.
- Opportunities: What could the survey find out about our customers that it doesn't ask yet?
- Threats: What could make the results wrong or unreliable (who takes it, how many people answer, the order of the questions)?
- The Fix: Give specific changes that should be made.

Submission:

Submit your reflection as a Text Entry.

## Warm-up

Imagine you are a chef and you just cooked a new meal. Why is it important to taste it yourself before you serve it to a paying customer? Translate this logic to our marketing survey.
