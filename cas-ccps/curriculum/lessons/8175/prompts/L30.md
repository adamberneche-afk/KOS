## Prompt

Assignment: Data-Driven Segmentation (Survey Build)

Description:

We have spent time developing "Market Segments." Now, we need to prove those segments actually exist and find out how to talk to them.

The Task:

Use Google Forms to create a complete market research survey.

Requirements:

- Question Structure:

- Create 10 Closed Questions (Multiple Choice, Checkbox, Scale).
- Create 2 Open Questions (Short Answer/Paragraph).

- Purpose of Data:

- Your questions should be designed to verify that the market segments we developed are correct.
- The data should give us insight into how to best reach these segments.

Submission:

When completed, click "Send," copy the link, and paste the URL to fill out the survey in this assignment.

Below the link, type all 12 questions with their answer choices. Then, for each market segment we developed, name the question or questions that check whether it exists, and the one that tells us how to reach it.

## Warm-up

Imagine you are selling a new energy drink. You *assume* your target market is teenage athletes. What happens to your business if you are wrong and your actual customers are tired office workers? How would you find out before you waste money on ads?
