## Prompt

Assignment: Survey Analysis (Data Driven Decisions)

Description:

Collecting data is useless unless you use it to make improvements. Today, you will review the results of our class survey and act as a consultant to improve the operation of our league/event.

The Task:

Review the Google Forms summary charts and responses. Then, write a response addressing the following:

1. The Data (3 Points of Interest)

- Using 100 words (minimum), describe three specific data points from the survey that you find interesting or surprising.

2. The Suggestion (Action Plan)

- Give one concrete suggestion for how our league/event should be organized or operated differently based on this feedback.

3. The Justification (Cite Your Sources)

- You must cite the survey data to justify your suggestion.
- Protect the Data: Do not include any respondent's name or email in your response. In one sentence, explain why survey data must be kept private.

Submission:

Submit your analysis as a Text Entry.

## Warm-up

If 80% of students say they hate the cafeteria pizza, but the sales data shows it is the #1 selling item, what is the truth?
