## Prompt

Assignment: The 6-Second Resume

Description:

Recruiters spend an average of 6 seconds looking at a resume before deciding to keep it or trash it. Your resume must be clean, professional, and easy to read.

The Task:

Create a one page professional resume.

Requirements:

- Header: Name, Professional Email, Phone Number, LinkedIn URL.
- Education: School, Graduation Date, GPA.
- Experience: List any jobs, volunteer work, or club leadership.
- Skills: List Soft Skills and Hard Skills.
- The "Action Verb" Rule: Every bullet point under your experience must start with an Action Verb.
- The Target: Find one real job or internship posting on TeamWork Online, Indeed, Handshake or a team's careers page that your resume could be sent to, and paste the link at the bottom.

Submission:

Submit your Resume as a PDF file.

## Warm-up

You are a hiring manager. You have a stack of 100 applications. How long do you look at each one before deciding 'Yes' or 'No'? (Answer: Average is 6 seconds). What are you looking for?
