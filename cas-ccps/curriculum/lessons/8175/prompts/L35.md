## Prompt

Assignment: The Google Interview Challenge

Description:

Congratulations! You have landed an interview! Since we cannot fly to California today, we will use the Google Interview Warmup tool to simulate the process.

The Task:

- Access the Tool: Go to Google Interview Warmup.
- The Process: There are five questions that you will answer verbally.
- Review the Feedback: After answering, the tool will analyze your response.
- Refine Your Answer: Use the feedback to create a "perfected" written version of your answer.

Submission:

Copy and paste the Question and your Final Answer for all five questions into the text submission box. Under each Final Answer, write one piece of feedback the tool gave you and what you changed because of it.

## Warm-up

What is the single scariest question you could be asked in a job interview? Why does it make you nervous?
