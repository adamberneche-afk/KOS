## Prompt

Project: The Course Brochure (Honest Feedback)

Description:

You will use your Marketing knowledge while providing Mr. Berneche honest feedback about the course you have experienced this year.

The Task:

Create a brochure for this course using a template or Canva.

Brochure Requirements:

- Front Cover: A creative Logo.
- Panel TWO: Showcase a memorable project from the course.
- Panel THREE: What are three careers this class might prepare you for?
- Panel FOUR: The Teacher Review (Pros and Cons of the teaching approach).
- Panel FIVE: Share "Student Reviews" (quotes).
- Back Panel: Contact Info for Mr. Berneche.

Submission:

Submit your Brochure as a PDF or Image file.

## Warm-up

What is the difference between 'constructive criticism' and 'complaining'? Write one example of each regarding a teacher or a coach.
