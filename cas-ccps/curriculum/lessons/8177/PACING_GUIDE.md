# **Master Pacing Guide: 8177 Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management**

**Instructor:** Mr. Berneche & Marketing Co-Teacher

**Location:** Clover Hill High School, Richmond, VA

**Duration:** 36 Weeks (Full Academic Year)

This pacing guide organizes the VDOE 8177 competencies and associated lesson plans into four 9-week quarters. The curriculum is designed to move students from foundational business concepts through hands-on venue operations (The Cavalier Shop), culminating in large-scale event planning and global macroeconomic theory.

## **Quarter 1: The Business of the Game & Brand Fundamentals**

*Focus: Introduction to the sports industry, corporate structures, workplace readiness, and the foundations of brand identity.*

  - **Week 1-2: Workplace Readiness & The Industry Ecosystem**

<!-- end list -->

  - Introduction to 8177 & Cavalier Expectations.
  - *Lesson:* **The Business of the Game (Franchise Analysis)** \* *Competencies:* 1-22 (Workplace Readiness), 24, 50, 52, 53, 86, 94 (Management, Local Economy, Business Structure, For-Profit vs. Nonprofit, Revenue, Competition).

<!-- end list -->

  - **Week 3-4: Brand Identity & Atmospherics**

<!-- end list -->

  - Defining how visuals and reputation drive consumer motivation.
  - *Lesson:* **The Brand Board Project**
  - *Competencies:* 1, 11, 21, 42, 45, 93, 97.

<!-- end list -->

  - **Week 5-7: The Management Perspective & The 4 Ps**

<!-- end list -->

  - Introduction to the Marketing Mix and creating Unique Selling Propositions.
  - *Lesson:* **The 4 Ps of the Fan Experience (Marketing Mix)**
  - *Lesson:* **CHHS School Store Company Description**
  - *Competencies:* 10, 11, 21, 23, 46, 47, 56, 93, 97.

*Focus: Transitioning to the physical venue (The Cavalier Shop). Students learn retail math, pricing strategies, and physical layout operations.*

  - **Week 10-12: Venue Layout & Crowd Flow**

  - *Competencies:* 1, 11, 22, 45, 72, 73, 92, 95.

<!-- end list -->

  - **Week 13-15: Retail Math & Sourcing**

<!-- end list -->

  - Learning margins, break-even points, and vendor relations.
  - *Lesson:* **Product Pitch & Financial Analysis**
  - *Competencies:* 2, 7, 19, 93, 96.
