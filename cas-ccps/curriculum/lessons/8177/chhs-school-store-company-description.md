# **Lesson Plan: Crafting the Management Perspective Company Description**

**Course:** Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management (8177)

**Instructor:** Mr. Berneche & Marketing Co-Teacher

**Location:** Clover Hill High School, Richmond, VA

## **1. Lesson Overview**

Students will analyze the unique business and management model of the Clover Hill High School Store (The Cavalier Shop) as a specialized sports and entertainment venue. They will learn to articulate a "Unique Selling Proposition" (USP) by writing a formal Company Description that highlights how the store manages its brand reputation and community niche to compete with global retailers.

## **2. VDOE Competency Alignment**

This lesson addresses the following competencies from the **8177: Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management** record:

  - **Competency 11:** Demonstrate big-picture thinking (analyzing the store's role within the larger CHHS athletic and event ecosystem).
  - **Competency 21:** Demonstrate reading and writing skills (composing a professional management-level description).
  - **Competency 23:** Examine aspects of an industry (specifically the niche retail sector within sports and school events).
  - **Competency 81:** Identify the elements of a brand (recognizing the "Cavalier" brand as the store's primary asset).
  - **Competency 93:** Describe factors that motivate consumers to attend sports, entertainment, and event occasions (connecting store traffic to school spirit and events).

## **3. Learning Objectives**

1.  Analyze the management model of a school-based enterprise (SBE) as a sports/entertainment niche.
2.  Identify the motivational factors that drive the CHHS community to choose the "Cavalier Shop" over external vendors.
3.  Write a professional company description (min. 5 sentences) that balances operational goals with brand identity.

## **4. Instructional Sequence**

  - **The Venue Context:** (5 mins) Discussion on why people buy merchandise at a stadium vs. at a mall.
  - **The Motivation Factor:** (10 mins) Brainstorming session: Why does a student buy a "Clover Hill" shirt here instead of a generic sports shirt on Amazon? (Links to Comp 93).
  - **Management Writing Workshop:** (20 mins) Students draft a description focused on the store as a "Community Hub" and "Brand Steward."

## **5. Company Description: The Cavalier Shop**

The Clover Hill High School Store operates on a unique, student-managed business model that functions as both a specialized retail venue and a real-world leadership laboratory. What truly makes us stand out from big-box competitors is our role as the primary steward of the Cavalier brand, offering exclusive merchandise that resonates with the specific spirit of our school’s sports and event culture. Customers choose to bring their business to us because every transaction directly supports student activities and provides our management students with high-level experience in venue operations and community engagement. Unlike impersonal online retailers, we leverage consumer motivations of school pride and immediate event-day needs to provide a high-value, professional shopping experience. Ultimately, choosing the school store is a commitment to fostering a culture of "Accountability, Integrity, and Resilience" while investing in the future leaders of the sports and entertainment industry.

## **6. Faculty & Student Handbook Compliance**

  - **Employee Professionalism:** In line with Faculty Handbook protocols, this lesson bridges curriculum goals with professional output, preparing students for industry-standard communication.
  - **Core Values:** Students are reminded that their written descriptions must uphold the "Respect" and "Integrity" values of CHHS, ensuring all competitive comparisons are professional and ethical.
