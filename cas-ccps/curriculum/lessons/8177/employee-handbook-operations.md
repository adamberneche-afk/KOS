# **Lesson Plan: Venue Operations & The Employee Handbook**

**Course:** Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management (8177)

**Instructor:** Mr. Berneche & Marketing Co-Teacher

**Location:** Clover Hill High School, Richmond, VA

**Target Grade Level:** 10-12

## **1. Lesson Overview**

Students will transition from marketing theory to venue operations by analyzing and implementing the Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for The Cavalier Shop. Students will learn the distinct roles necessary to run a specialized retail venue, mastering the opening, closing, and inventory procedures required to maintain safety, profitability, and brand integrity.

## **2. VDOE Competency Alignment**

This lesson addresses the following competencies from the **8177: Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management** framework:

  - **Competency 4:** Demonstrate integrity (Best practices for handling revenue and cash).
  - **Competency 5:** Demonstrate work ethic (Adhering to strict opening and closing procedures).
  - **Competency 10:** Collaborate with team members (Executing specific shift roles and responsibilities).
  - **Competency 14:** Manage time and resources (Executing efficient inventory counts).
  - **Competency 20:** Demonstrate professionalism (Following the Employee Handbook guidelines).
  - **Competency 85:** Explain the components of venue management (The mechanics of physically opening and closing a space).
  - **Competency 95:** Analyze personnel needs for an event (Assigning specific roles for a store shift).
  - **Competency 107 & 108:** Demonstrate workplace etiquette (Professional communication, dress code, and behavior on the sales floor).

## **3. Learning Objectives**

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

1.  Differentiate the roles and responsibilities of a venue management team.
2.  Execute proper opening and closing procedures for a retail environment.
3.  Apply best practices for inventory management and revenue reconciliation to prevent "shrink" (loss).
4.  Demonstrate professional accountability and workplace etiquette through the daily operations of The Cavalier Shop.

## **4. Instructional Sequence**

### **A. The "Franchise" Concept (10 Minutes)**

  - **Discussion:** Why does a Starbucks in Richmond run exactly the same as one in Seattle? Introduce the concept of Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
  - **Management Lens (Comp 85):** Explain that a venue cannot scale or be successful without repeatable systems.

### **B. Role Delegation & Etiquette (15 Minutes)**

  - **Direct Instruction (Comp 95, 107, 108):** Review the roles outlined in the handbook and discuss the non-negotiables of workplace etiquette (e.g., no cell phones on the register, greeting every customer).
  - **Activity:** Have students group up and role-play a scenario: A rush of customers comes in while the cash register paper jams. Who does what based on their job description, and how do they communicate the delay to customers politely?

### **C. Operations Walk-Through (60 Minutes - In Store)**

  - **Station 1: The POS & Revenue:** Walking through the cash drawer procedures.
  - **Station 2: Inventory Control:** Teaching the "First In, First Out" (FIFO) method and how to count stock.
  - **Station 3: Facility Management:** The physical opening/closing checklist (lights, facing, locking).

## **5. The Cavalier Shop: Employee Handbook & SOPs**

### **I. Hours of Operation**

  - **Standard Hours:** During all 4 lunch blocks (11:30 AM - 1:30 PM).
  - **Event Hours:** Specific home football/basketball games (Report time: 1 hour prior to kickoff/tip-off).
  - *Note: Employees are expected to arrive 10 minutes prior to shift start for the opening procedures.*

### **II. Roles & Responsibilities**

1.  **General Manager (Teacher/Instructor):** Oversees overall venue operations, final financial reconciliation, and final security checks.
2.  **Shift Manager (Student Leader):** Responsible for assigning tasks, resolving customer disputes, verifying inventory counts, and completing the Shift Summary Report.
3.  **Sales Associate (Cashier/Greeter):** Operates the Point of Sale (POS) system, welcomes guests using the CHHS Core Values, and maintains a clean checkout area.
4.  **Inventory Specialist:** Focuses on restocking shelves from the backroom, "facing" merchandise to look professional, and alerting the Shift Manager to low stock levels.

### **III. Opening Procedures**

1.  **Security & Safety:** Unlock doors; ensure the 36-inch ADA/fire code pathways are entirely clear of boxes.
2.  **Float Verification:** Shift Manager and GM count the starting cash drawer ("the float") and sign the daily log.
3.  **Atmospherics:** Turn on designated store lighting and approved venue music.
4.  **Merchandising:** Face all products (pull items to the front of the shelf), ensure size tags are visible, and turn on digital signage/POS iPads.

### **IV. Closing Procedures**

1.  **Venue Sweep:** Check fitting rooms (if applicable) and corners to ensure all customers have exited.
2.  **Restock & Recovery:** Inventory Specialists fold apparel, replace sold items from backstock, and sweep the floor.
3.  **Financial Reconciliation:** Sales Associate and Shift Manager "Z-Out" the register. Count the drawer, subtract the initial float, and match the cash/credit total against the POS daily sales report.
4.  **Secure the Venue:** Turn off all electronics, secure the bank bag with the GM, and lock all primary and secondary doors.

### **V. Best Practices: Inventory & Revenue**

  - **Revenue Integrity:** *Never* leave the cash drawer open between transactions. If a discrepancy occurs during reconciliation, immediately notify the GM.
  - **Inventory Control (Shrink Prevention):** Every item that leaves the store *must* be scanned through the POS, even if it is a promotional giveaway.
  - **The "Two-Person" Rule:** Cash handling and high-value inventory counts should always be performed by two employees simultaneously to ensure accountability and accuracy.

### **VI. Workplace Etiquette & Professionalism (Comp 107/108)**

  - **Dress Code:** Employees must wear their designated Cavalier Shop polo or lanyard while on shift to be easily identifiable to customers.
  - **Device Policy:** Personal cell phones are strictly prohibited while on the sales floor. If an emergency text must be sent, the employee must step into the backroom.
  - **Customer Interaction:** Every customer must be verbally greeted upon entry ("Welcome to the Cavalier Shop\!"). Employees must use professional language—avoiding slang and utilizing the "T.H.A.N.K.S." method if a customer has a complaint.

## **6. Faculty & Student Handbook Compliance**

  - **Integrity (CHHS Core Values):** Handling school funds requires absolute integrity. Students must understand that revenue mismanagement is both an academic and disciplinary violation.
  - **Safety & Security:** As outlined in the Faculty Handbook, unauthorized visitors are not permitted. During opening/closing, students must never prop open exterior doors (e.g., Door 10 or 11) for any reason.
