# **Lesson Plan: Venue Market Research & Survey Design**

**Course:** Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management (8177)

**Instructor:** Mr. Berneche & Marketing Co-Teacher

**Location:** Clover Hill High School, Richmond, VA

**Target Grade Level:** 10-12

## **1. Lesson Overview**

Students will act as Market Researchers for The Cavalier Shop. They will identify the primary target audience for the store (students, parents, or staff) and design a three-question, bias-free survey to discover how to best reach this audience and what drives their purchasing decisions. Students will utilize Google Forms to build their surveys, learning how question format dictates the quality of the data collected.

## **2. VDOE Competency Alignment**

This lesson addresses the following competencies from the **8177: Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management** framework:

  - **Competency 2:** Demonstrate critical thinking and problem solving (Identifying and eliminating bias in survey questions).
  - **Competency 18:** Demonstrate proficiency with technologies (Utilizing Google Forms for data collection).
  - **Competency 42:** Conduct market research (Developing the primary data collection instrument).
  - **Competency 46:** Determine a target market (Defining who to send the survey to).
  - **Competency 93:** Describe factors that motivate consumers to attend sports, entertainment, and event occasions (Asking questions that uncover consumer values and motivations).

## **3. Learning Objectives**

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

1.  Define a specific target market for a school-based enterprise.
2.  Differentiate between qualitative and quantitative research questions.
3.  Identify and eliminate "leading" or biased language in survey design.
4.  Construct a functional digital survey using Google Forms.

## **4. Instructional Sequence**

### **A. The "Who Cares?" Discussion (10 Minutes)**

  - **Target Audience (Comp 46):** Brainstorm with the class: Who actually has the purchasing power? Are we marketing to freshmen who want spirit wear, or parents who have the credit cards?
  - **Concept:** You cannot market to "everyone." If you market to everyone, you reach no one.

### **B. The "Bias Bootcamp" (15 Minutes)**

  - **Direct Instruction:** Show examples of bad, biased questions (e.g., "How much do you love our amazing new t-shirts?").
  - Discuss how to phrase questions neutrally to get actionable, honest data.
  - Explain the difference between Quantitative (measurable numbers/multiple choice) and Qualitative (open-ended feelings/ideas) data.

### **C. Digital Application Lab (35 Minutes)**

  - **Independent Work (Comp 18 & 42):** Students log into Google Workspace and create a Google Form.
  - They must input the three standardized questions (below) and ensure the settings are adjusted to easily share the link via Canvas or email.

## **5. Project Deliverables: The 3-Question Survey**

To discover how to find our customers and what they value, students will build a form with the following carefully constructed, unbiased questions:

**Question 1: Quantitative (Focus: Communication Channels / "How to find them")**

  - **Prompt:** *How do you most frequently receive news and updates regarding Clover Hill High School events? (Select up to two)*
  - **Format:** Checkboxes
  - **Options:** Canvas Announcements, CHHS Instagram, ParentSquare, Morning Announcements, Word of Mouth/Friends.
  - **Why it works:** It doesn't assume they already follow the store. It tells us exactly where we should spend our time marketing.

**Question 2: Quantitative (Focus: Consumer Motivation / "What they value")**

  - **Prompt:** *On a scale of 1 to 5, how important is it to you that the profits from your merchandise purchases directly fund CHHS student activities?*
  - **Format:** Linear Scale (1 = Not at all important, 5 = Extremely important)
  - **Why it works:** It measures the "value" of community reinvestment objectively without forcing a "yes" or "no."

**Question 3: Qualitative (Focus: Product Demand)**

  - **Prompt:** *What is one specific type of product or service you would like to see offered at The Cavalier Shop that is currently unavailable?*
  - **Format:** Short Answer / Paragraph
  - **Why it works:** It is completely open-ended. Providing a drop-down list here introduces bias by limiting their imagination to our preconceived ideas.

## **6. Faculty & Student Handbook Compliance**

  - **Respect & Privacy (Core Values):** Instruct students to set the Google Form to *not* collect email addresses automatically. This ensures respondents feel safe giving honest feedback, aligning with the school's culture of respect and empathy.
  - **Appropriate Distribution:** Remind students that if we deploy this survey, we must utilize approved CCPS communication channels (like an approved Canvas announcement or flyer) rather than spamming student emails.
