# **Lesson Plan: Venue Promotion & Flyer Design**

**Course:** Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management (8177)

**Instructor:** Mr. Berneche & Marketing Co-Teacher

**Location:** Clover Hill High School, Richmond, VA

**Target Grade Level:** 10-12

## **1. Lesson Overview**

To drive foot traffic to The Cavalier Shop, students will take on the role of Graphic Designers/Promotions Managers. They will create a promotional flyer designed specifically to be printed in black and white on standard school copiers. The flyer must effectively communicate the store's schedule, location, and featured pricing to the student body, utilizing high-contrast design principles to capture attention in a busy high school hallway.

## **2. VDOE Competency Alignment**

This lesson addresses the following competencies from the **8177: Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management** framework:

  - **Competency 1:** Demonstrate creativity and innovation (Designing a visually appealing layout without relying on color).
  - **Competency 14:** Manage time and resources (Designing within the physical and financial constraints of black-and-white school printers).
  - **Competency 18:** Demonstrate proficiency with technologies, tools, and machines (Utilizing graphic design software like Canva, Adobe Express, or Google Slides).
  - **Competency 21:** Demonstrate reading and writing skills (Writing clear, concise, and persuasive copy for the flyer).
  - **Competency 85:** Explain the components of venue management (Clearly communicating venue location and operating hours to the public).

## **3. Learning Objectives**

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

1.  Apply graphic design principles (contrast, hierarchy, and negative space) to create an effective black-and-white print advertisement.
2.  Formulate clear promotional copy that communicates essential venue information.
3.  Utilize digital design software to produce a print-ready marketing asset.

## **4. Instructional Sequence**

### **A. The "Copier Constraint" Discussion (10 Minutes)**

  - **Direct Instruction:** Discuss the reality of small-business and school-based marketing budgets. We can't always afford full-color glossy prints.
  - **Design Concept:** Introduce how to use stark contrast (heavy blacks, bold whites), varied typography sizing, and drop shadows to make a black-and-white image "pop" on a cinderblock wall.

### **B. Visual Hierarchy (10 Minutes)**

  - **Activity:** What is the most important piece of information? (The hours? The location? The prices?) Teach students to size their text based on what the reader needs to see from 10 feet away versus 2 feet away.

### **C. Promotional Design Lab (40 Minutes)**

  - **Independent Work (Comp 18):** Students log into their preferred design platform (e.g., Canva) and begin constructing their flyers.
  - They must import the provided school store logo and ensure it translates well into grayscale.

## **5. Project Deliverables: The Promotional Flyer**

Each student must submit a digital, print-ready PDF of their flyer. To receive full credit, the flyer **must** include the following elements:

1.  **The Brand:** The official school store logo.
2.  **The Schedule:** Clearly stated hours of operation: **8:00 AM - 8:30 PM**.
3.  **The Location:** Clearly stated as **2nd Floor, between Room 301 and 401**.
4.  **The Hooks:** A listing of at least three specific items sold at the store, along with their prices, to entice shoppers.
5.  **The Format:** The entire design must be optimized for **Black and White** printing (no muddy grays or unreadable light-colored text).

## **6. Faculty & Student Handbook Compliance**

  - **Facility Maintenance (Posting Policies):** Before any winning flyer designs are printed and hung around the school, students must be reminded of the CHHS policy for posting materials. Flyers may only be hung in approved locations (e.g., designated bulletin boards, specific brick/cinderblock walls) and must use approved blue painter's tape to avoid damaging the paint.
  - **Approval:** All publicly posted materials must be approved by the Student Activities Director or Administration prior to distribution.
