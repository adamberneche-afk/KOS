## Prompt

Assignment: The Cavalier Shop Company Description

Write a company description for the Cavalier Shop: a short, professional paragraph that explains what the store is, who it serves, and why customers choose it over Amazon, Walmart or a mall store.

Part 1: Plan It. Before you write, answer these in a few words each:

- What does the store sell, and who are its customers?
- Name two reasons a student or parent would buy a Clover Hill shirt here instead of online.
- What is the store's unique selling proposition (USP): the one thing it offers that big stores can't?

Part 2: Write It. Write a company description of at least 5 sentences, in a professional voice (no slang, no "I think"). It must:

- say what the Cavalier Shop is and how it is run
- name its customers
- state its USP
- explain how it supports school spirit and events
- end with a sentence about what customers are really supporting when they buy

Part 3: Check It. Underline or bold your USP sentence.

Use these terms accurately: company description, target market, unique selling proposition, brand, school-based enterprise.

## Background

Every business plan starts with a company description: a short paragraph that tells investors, customers and partners who you are and why you matter. A good one is clear, confident and specific. It doesn't brag, and it doesn't list every product. It explains what makes the business different.

The Cavalier Shop is a school-based enterprise: a real store run by students, inside the school, as part of their class. It can't beat Amazon on selection or Walmart on price. It doesn't have to. Customers choose it for other reasons:

- It's the official home of the Cavalier brand. Students design and make its products right here in the classroom, on the store's own sublimation printer, heat press and large-format printer, so its Clover Hill gear can't be bought anywhere else.
- It's right here on event days. Need a shirt for a spirit day or a hat before a game tonight? It's down the hall, not two days away in shipping.
- Every purchase supports students. The money goes back into student activities, and the designers, makers and employees are students learning to run a business.
- It's part of school pride. Wearing Cavalier gear says you belong to the community.

That one thing a business offers that its competitors can't is its unique selling proposition (USP). A strong company description builds around the USP.

How to write it, sentence by sentence:

- Sentence 1: what the business is and how it is run.
- Sentence 2: who its customers are.
- Sentence 3: what makes it different (the USP).
- Sentence 4: why customers choose it over the competition.
- Sentence 5: what customers are really supporting when they buy.

## Key terms

- Company description: a short, professional summary of what a business is, who it serves and what makes it different.
- Target market: the specific group of customers a business is trying to reach.
- Unique selling proposition (USP): the one thing a business offers that its competitors don't.
- Brand: the reputation and feeling people connect with a business.
- School-based enterprise: a business run by students, inside a school, as part of their learning.
- Competitor: another business trying to win the same customers.

## Example

A sample company description for an imaginary minor-league team store (yours is about the Cavalier Shop).

"The Riverside Rapids Team Store is the official retail shop of the Riverside Rapids baseball club, located inside Rapids Field and open during every home game. Its customers are season-ticket holders, families attending games and fans across the region. Unlike sporting goods chains, the Team Store sells limited-edition gear designed with the players, released only on game nights. Fans choose the store because they can grab a new cap on the way to their seats and wear it the same night. Every purchase helps fund the club's free youth baseball clinics, so fans support the next generation of players every time they shop."
