# **Lesson Plan: Venue Layout & The Cavalier Shop Planogram**

**Course:** Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management (8177)

**Instructor:** Mr. Berneche & Marketing Co-Teacher

**Location:** Clover Hill High School, Richmond, VA

## **1. Lesson Overview**

Students will apply event management and venue operations principles by designing a planogram for The Cavalier Shop. The lesson treats the store as a "mini-venue," focusing on how physical layout, crowd flow, and "Atmospherics" impact the fan/customer experience and operational efficiency.

## **2. VDOE Competency Alignment**

This lesson addresses the following competencies from the **8177: Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management** record:

  - **Competency 1:** Demonstrate creativity and innovation.
  - **Competency 11:** Demonstrate big-picture thinking (Venue layout impact on total event/store success).
  - **Competency 22:** Demonstrate workplace safety (Maintaining egress and fire safety codes).
  - **Competency 85:** Explain the components of venue management (Atmospherics and physical layout).
  - **Competency 86:** Describe how venue layout influences customer/fan behavior (Traffic patterns and purchase points).
  - **Competency 92:** Explain the process for planning an event (Visualizing the "event" of a store opening/shift).
  - **Competency 95:** Analyze personnel needs for an event (Strategic placement of staff within the planogram).

## **3. Learning Objectives**

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

1.  Define "Planogram" within the context of venue and event management.
2.  Analyze how layout influences traffic flow and "fan" engagement.
3.  Create a professional map of the store that maximizes "Hot Zones" for revenue while maintaining safety standards.

## **4. Instructional Sequence**

### **A. The Venue Manager’s Walk-Through (10 mins)**

  - **Activity:** Stand at the store entrance.
  - **Discussion:** Treat the store as a "stadium concourse." Ask: "Where is the bottleneck? Where do we put the 'high-demand' items to keep people moving safely?" (Relates to Competency 86).

### **B. Core Concepts: Atmospherics & Operations (15 mins)**

  - **Venue Management (85):** Discuss lighting, music, and the "Cavalier Brand" environment.
  - **Safety (22):** Identify fire exits and the "36-inch rule" for ADA accessibility and emergency egress.
  - **Staffing (95):** Decide where student employees should stand to best assist "fans" and prevent loss.

### **C. Creative Lab: Designing the Venue Map (45 mins)**

Students draft a 2D planogram. They must strategically place:

  - **Concession-style Impulse Buys:** High-margin small items near the checkout.
  - **The "Main Attraction":** New spirit wear arrivals in the most visible "Hot Zone."
  - **Information Stations:** Clear signage for event/product info.

## **5. Planogram Requirements**

Each student project must include:

  - **Crowd Flow Analysis:** Arrows showing the predicted path of the customer.
  - **Merchandise Zoning:** Grouping items logically (e.g., "The Cold Weather Zone").
  - **Safety Compliance:** Clearly marked exits and unobstructed paths.
  - **Staffing Nodes:** Marked spots where "Team Members" should be stationed for optimal service.

## **6. Faculty & Student Handbook Compliance**

  - **Safety (Faculty 22):** All designs must prioritize fire code compliance—no merchandise may block Door 10 or 11 access if the store is used as an entry point.
  - **Integrity & Respect:** Promotional signage in the planogram must use "positive and appropriate language" (CHHS Core Values).
