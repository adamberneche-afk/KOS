# **Lesson Plan: Survey Deployment & Statistical Significance**

**Course:** Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management (8177)

**Instructor:** Mr. Berneche & Marketing Co-Teacher

**Location:** Clover Hill High School, Richmond, VA

**Target Grade Level:** 10-12

## **1. Lesson Overview**

Having designed the market research survey for The Cavalier Shop, students will now execute the data collection phase. To make accurate management decisions, the class must achieve a statistically significant sample size of 323 unique responses from the CHHS student body. Students are tasked with a fieldwork quota of obtaining 15 unique, verifiable survey responses to contribute to this overall venue management goal.

## **2. VDOE Competency Alignment**

This lesson addresses the following competencies from the **8177: Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management** framework:

  - **Competency 4:** Demonstrate integrity (Collecting authentic data without falsifying responses or taking the survey multiple times).
  - **Competency 7:** Demonstrate listening and speaking skills (Approaching peers and pitching the survey effectively to gain compliance).
  - **Competency 14:** Manage time and resources (Strategizing how and when to get 15 unique peers to complete the task).
  - **Competency 42:** Conduct market research (Deploying the survey instrument to the target market).
  - **Competency 43:** Analyze market research data (Preparing the sample size needed for accurate future analysis).

## **3. Learning Objectives**

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

1.  Define "statistical significance" and explain why a minimum sample size (323) is required for a population the size of CHHS.
2.  Develop an interpersonal "pitch" to persuade peers to complete a market research survey.
3.  Execute a data collection quota ethically and efficiently.

## **4. Instructional Sequence**

### **A. The Math of Marketing: Statistical Significance (15 Minutes)**

  - **Direct Instruction:** Explain why we need exactly 323 responses. (Introduce the concept of population size vs. sample size and margin of error).
  - **Discussion:** What happens to a business if they base their inventory orders on the opinions of only 5 people? (Inventory waste, lost revenue).

### **B. The Deployment Strategy (15 Minutes)**

  - **The Quota:** Introduce the individual goal: **15 unique responses per student**.
  - **The "Pitch":** How do you ask someone to take a survey without sounding like a spammer? Have students practice a 10-second elevator pitch to use on their peers at lunch or in the halls.

### **C. Ethics in Data Collection (10 Minutes)**

  - **Integrity Check (Comp 4):** Discuss the severe real-world consequences of falsifying data in business. Explain the parameters: no duplicates, no faking emails, one entry per person.

## **6. Faculty & Student Handbook Compliance**

  - **Integrity (CHHS Core Values):** Falsifying survey data is a direct violation of the school's core value of Integrity and undermines the entire class project. Fraudulent entries will result in a zero for the assessment.
  - **Respecting Instructional Time:** Students are reminded that they may only canvas for survey responses during approved times (e.g., lunch block, Cavalier Morning/homeroom, or before/after school). They are not permitted to disrupt other teachers' classes to collect data.
