ASSIGNMENT: Examining All Aspects of an Industry (S0-U2)

CRITERIA

Criterion 1: Examining All Aspects of an Industry
- 8177-23 Examine aspects of planning within an industry/organization.
    * Evidence of development of vision and mission statements
    * Evidence of setting of performance goals and objectives
    * Evidence of review of previous performance (e.g., productivity, profit)
    * Evidence of evaluation of current assets
    * Evidence of formulation of strategic and operational plans
    * Evidence of use of planning tools (e.g., market research, budget analysis, decision-making models, competitive analyses)
    * Evidence of determination of human, natural, technology, and capital resource needs
    * Evidence of forecasting of trends
    * Evidence of anticipation of changes in the business climate (e.g., economic factors, laws, regulations, taxes)
    * Evidence of anticipation of and compensation for organizational and industry risk
- 8177-24 Examine aspects of management within an industry/organization.
    * Evidence of effect of the organization’s structure and culture on operations
    * Evidence of process for accomplishing goals, using available human, natural, technology, and capital resources
    * Evidence of ways of ensuring open communication channels
    * Evidence of ways of enabling workers to fulfill their responsibilities
    * Evidence of evaluation of workers’ performance
    * Evidence of provision of training and job-growth opportunities to workers
    * Evidence of assurance of worker equity, access, and safety
    * Evidence of resolution of conflicts
    * Evidence of performance of employment functions (e.g., recruiting, hiring, retaining, discharging)
- 8177-25 Examine aspects of financial responsibility within an industry/organization.
    * Evidence of accounting processes
    * Evidence of financial decision-making processes, including budget development
    * Evidence of methods of acquiring capital
    * Evidence of management of financial operations, including payroll, transactions, records, and reports
- 8177-26 Examine technical and production skills required of workers within an industry/organization.
    * Evidence of industry-related technical skills (e.g., communication, mathematics, science, technology, time-management, and creative-thinking skills)
    * Evidence of industry-related production skills (specific skills used for production of goods or services)
    * Evidence of industry-related interpersonal and team-player skills
- 8177-27 Examine principles of technology that underlie an industry/organization.
    * Evidence of technological systems used in the industry
    * Evidence of mathematical, scientific, social, ethical, and economic principles underlying the technological systems
    * Evidence of impact of energy systems, fuel sources, and other technological systems on the production of goods and services
    * Evidence of use of emerging and alternative energy resources in the production of goods and services
    * Evidence of generation and distribution of energy to industries/organizations for use in creating goods and services
- 8177-28 Examine labor issues related to an industry/organization.
    * Evidence of workers’ rights and responsibilities (e.g., wages, benefits, working conditions)
    * Evidence of role of employment contracts and agreements
    * Evidence of role of certification, licensure, and other requirements for specific jobs/occupations
    * Evidence of role of labor organizations and other worker advocacy groups (e.g., professional/trade associations)
- 8177-29 Examine community issues related to an industry/organization.
    * Evidence of the effects of the organization on the community (e.g., provision of jobs, tax revenue, and goods/services; involvement in community programs/activities; environmental impact)
    * Evidence of the effects of the community on the organization (e.g., employee base; local taxes and regulations; local government services such as roads, schools, utilities; other local services)
- 8177-30 Examine health, safety, and environmental issues related to an industry/organization.
    * Evidence of responsibility for workers’ health and safety
    * Evidence of laws/regulations and practices affecting workers’ health and safety
    * Evidence of health and safety hazards
    * Evidence of health and safety programs
    * Evidence of responsibility for the environment
    * Evidence of laws/regulations and practices affecting the impact on the environment
    * Evidence of sustainability initiatives

PASSING STANDARD
The work passes when the planned work product shows evidence for every competency listed above, in the student's own words, applied to the class conglomerate (its divisions, school store, events and esports program) or a real organization in sports, entertainment or events, and uses the unit vocabulary accurately (Industry dimensions, Vertical integration, Conglomerate, Org chart, Labor relations). Each criterion needs specific examples, not general statements. Work that restates definitions without applying them, or leaves any listed competency without evidence, does not pass.
