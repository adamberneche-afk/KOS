ASSIGNMENT: Industry Overview & Career Landscape (S1-U1)
COURSE: 8175 Sports Entertainment and Event Marketing
UNIT OBJECTIVE: Students can explain the importance, history, trends, and hospitality dimensions of the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
PLANNED WORK PRODUCT: Industry landscape document: a one-page brief per student that places the conglomerate inside the real industry — which segment, which trends affect it, which career roles are represented by each division position.
COURSE ROLE: Marketing rotation students focus on consumer-facing trends — streaming, social media, digital engagement. They identify which trends affect their upcoming rotation tracks.
COMPETENCIES ASSESSED: 8175-44, 8175-45, 8175-46, 8175-47

CRITERIA

Criterion 1: Understanding the Importance of Sports, Entertainment, and Event Marketing
- 8175-44 Explain the importance of the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * What is the sports and fitness industry?
    * What is arts and entertainment?
    * What role do recreation and tourism play in the sports, entertainment, and event industries?
    * the importance of each industry to the consumer (e.g., escape, fun, social connection, health, education, economic inclusiveness)
    * provides jobs
    * benefits other businesses
    * adds to tax revenue
- 8175-45 Describe the past, present, and future of the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * evolution of the industries from the social, technological (e.g., artificial intelligence [AI]), and economic perspectives
    * expansion of the industries to national and global audiences
    * key developments and people within the industries
- 8175-46 Explain hospitality as it relates to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * the importance of the fan experience
    * knowledge of the components of the corporate experience (e.g., networking, sponsorship agreements, partnerships)
    * the concept of hospitality as the friendly reception and treatment of guests
    * the necessity of security at sports, entertainment, and event occasions
    * challenge of getting fans/people to events when the ability and comfort of viewing from home is better than ever before
- 8175-47 Analyze trends and innovations in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * industries, such as
    * competitive e-sports
    * fantasy sports and sports betting
    * women’s sports
    * streaming services
    * automation
    * name, image, and likeness (NIL) and influencer marketing
    * trends, such as
    * audience options
    * recreation opportunities
    * leisure interests
    * the development of multipurpose venues

PASSING STANDARD
The work passes when the planned work product does what the assignment asks and shows each competency listed above in at least one specific way, in the student's own words, applied to the class conglomerate (its divisions, school store, events and esports program) or a real organization in sports, entertainment or events, and uses the unit vocabulary accurately (Sports, entertainment & events industry, Hospitality, Experience economy, Esports, Niche event, Stakeholder). Specific examples, not general statements. Work that restates definitions without applying them, or leaves a listed competency with no evidence at all, does not pass. Judge each competency only by what this assignment asks the student to do. The lines under a competency are examples of what evidence can look like, not a checklist: one specific, well-done example that fits the task is enough, and a part of a competency the task does not ask for is never required.
