Industry Overview & Career Landscape (S1-U1)

Students can define the role of the sports/entertainment/event manager, identify management traits, evaluate career opportunities, and analyze emerging events.

What you will make: Industry landscape document: a one-page brief per student that places the conglomerate inside the real industry — which segment, which trends affect it, which career roles are represented by each division position.

The work: Students research where esports and gaming sit within the broader sports and entertainment industry. They position the conglomerate — its school store, apparel production, media, and events — within the real industry landscape.

Go deeper:

- Manager traits and functions (Tasks 40–42): students self-assess against the traits of successful managers and identify which management functions their specialization role requires most.
- Organizational chart (Task 44): students draft the conglomerate org chart — all six divisions, all management roles, all reporting lines. This becomes a living document updated throughout the year.
- Niche and emerging events (Task 48): esports is the niche event case study. Students analyze how the school league fits the emerging event framework.
- Facilities management (Task 45) and on-site revenue (Task 46): Events Manager and Store Manager students connect these concepts to their specific division roles.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Define the role of the sports, entertainment, and event manager.

- Identify functions sports, entertainment, and event managers must perform to have success with consumers, partners, and employees.

- Document the different roles in sports, entertainment, and event management using an organizational chart.

- Explain on-site revenue streams as they relate to sports, entertainment, and event facilities.
- Identify how e-commerce and digital transactions affect sports, entertainment, and event businesses.
