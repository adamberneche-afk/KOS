ASSIGNMENT: Business Structure & Organization (S1-U2)
COURSE: 8175 Sports Entertainment and Event Marketing
UNIT OBJECTIVE: Students can explain what a business is in the sports/entertainment context and compare different types of business ownership.
PLANNED WORK PRODUCT: Conglomerate structure document: formal description of the business structure — what type of organization it is, how divisions relate to each other, who has authority over what, and how the school store's required status fits into the overall design.
COURSE ROLE: Marketing students analyze the business structures of three real sports, entertainment, or esports organizations and present findings to the class. Focus on how structure affects marketing decisions.
COMPETENCIES ASSESSED: 8175-68, 8175-69

CRITERIA

Criterion 1: Understanding the Business Structure of Sports, Entertainment, and Event Marketing
- 8175-68 Explain business as it relates to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * a definition of business
    * an understanding of business activities (e.g., management, accounting, finance, marketing, production, procurement)
- 8175-69 Compare the types of business ownership and their roles in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * for-profit businesses
    * proprietorship
    * partnership
    * corporation
    * cooperative
    * franchise
    * nonprofit organizations
    * advantages and disadvantages of each type

Judge each competency only by what this assignment asks the student to do.
