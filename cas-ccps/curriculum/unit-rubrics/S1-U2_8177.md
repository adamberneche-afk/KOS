UNIT OBJECTIVE: Students can explore organizational structures within the industry and explain for-profit vs. nonprofit operation.
COMPETENCIES ASSESSED: 8177-52, 8177-53

CRITERIA

Criterion 1: Explaining the Structure of Sports, Entertainment, and Event Businesses
- 8177-52 Explore the organizational structures within the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    * Evidence of professional sports leagues
    * Evidence of sports recreation leagues
    * Evidence of general business studios
    * Evidence of venues (public vs. private)
    * Evidence of governing bodies (e.g., National Collegiate Athletic Association [NCAA], International Olympic Committee [IOC])
    * Evidence of parks and recreation authorities
    * Evidence of collegiate athletic programs
- 8177-53 Explain the advantages and disadvantages of operating as a for-profit or nonprofit organization.
    * Evidence of advantages of for-profit (e.g., business growth, investment potential)
    * Evidence of disadvantages of for-profit (e.g., loss of revenue, business risk)
    * Evidence of advantages of nonprofit (e.g., tax exemptions, more public support)
    * Evidence of disadvantages of nonprofit (e.g., skepticism, insufficient volunteers, complex regulatory requirements)
    * Evidence of evaluation of which alternative best aligns with the objectives

PASSING STANDARD
The work passes when it shows evidence for every competency listed above, in the student's own words, applied to a real or realistic organization in sports, entertainment or events, and uses the unit vocabulary accurately (What students do, Business ownership type, Organizational structure, Vertical integration, For-profit organization, Nonprofit organization).
