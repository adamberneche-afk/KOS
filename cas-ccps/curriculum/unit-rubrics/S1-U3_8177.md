## Rubric

```
ASSIGNMENT: Entrepreneurship & Business Launch (S1-U3)
COURSE: 8177 Sports Entertainment and Event Management
UNIT OBJECTIVE: Students can identify entrepreneurship opportunities, describe the entrepreneurial mindset, map a business using the Business Model Canvas, and explain how small businesses drive economic growth.
PLANNED WORK PRODUCT: Completed BMC for the conglomerate (full group) + one-page division canvas per specialization role (8177) + brief participation summary per 8175 student documenting their contribution to the canvas.
COURSE ROLE: Management specialization students each own a BMC section that maps to their role: General Manager owns key activities and key partnerships; Store Manager owns revenue streams; Production Manager owns key resources and cost structure; Wholesale Manager owns key partnerships and cost structure.
COMPETENCIES ASSESSED: 8177-54, 8177-55, 8177-56, 8177-57

CRITERIA

Criterion 1: Exploring Entrepreneurship in Sports, Entertainment, and Event Management
- 8177-54 Identify entrepreneurship opportunities related to sports, entertainment, and event management.
    * Evidence of startup funding sources
    * Evidence of creating and operating a business
    * Evidence of franchise opportunities
    * Evidence of trends and emerging markets
- 8177-55 Describe the entrepreneurial mindset.
    * Evidence of ability to recognize opportunity
    * Evidence of comfortable with risk
    * Evidence of creative and innovative
    * Evidence of future orientation
    * Evidence of flexible and adaptable
    * Evidence of initiator and self-reliant
    * Evidence of critical thinker and problem-solver
    * Evidence of communicative and collaborative
- 8177-56 Identify the steps involved in starting a new business, using the Business Model Canvas (BMC).
    * Evidence of Value proposition—What value is being delivered to the customer? Which customer pain points are being addressed?
    * Evidence of Customer segments—For whom is value being created? What products and services are being offered to each customer segment?
    * Evidence of Customer relationships—What type of relationship is being maintained with each customer segment? What are the expectations of customers? How are they established?
    * Evidence of Channels—Which channels are to be focused on to reach the desired customer segments? How are those channels integrated? Which ones are the most cost-effective?
    * Evidence of Key partners—Who are the key partners? Key suppliers? Which key resources are being acquired from them? Which key activities do the partners perform?
    * Evidence of Key activities—What key activities do the value propositions require (e.g., product distribution, research and development strategy)?
    * Evidence of Key resources—What key resources do the value propositions require (e.g., office, hosting requirements, human resources, financial backing, transportation, electricity)?
    * Evidence of Cost structure—What are the most important cost drivers in the business model? Which key resources and activities are most expensive?
    * Evidence of Revenue streams—How will the business make money? Who are the customers willing to pay, and for what benefit? How would they prefer to pay? How are they currently paying? How does each stream add up to the total revenue?
- 8177-57 Describe how small businesses drive job creation, income generation, and economic growth.
    * Evidence of job creation
    * Evidence of economic activity
    * Evidence of local economic impact
    * Evidence of driving innovation
    * Evidence of community investment
    * Evidence of customer relationships
    * Evidence of charitable contributions

PASSING STANDARD
The work passes when the planned work product shows evidence for every competency listed above, in the student's own words, applied to the class conglomerate (its divisions, school store, events and esports program) or a real organization in sports, entertainment or events, and uses the unit vocabulary accurately (Entrepreneurship, Entrepreneurial mindset, Business Model Canvas (BMC), Value proposition, Revenue stream, Cost structure). Each criterion needs specific examples, not general statements. Work that restates definitions without applying them, or leaves any listed competency without evidence, does not pass.
```

## Prompt template

```
Entrepreneurship & Business Launch (S1-U3)

Students can identify entrepreneurship opportunities, describe the entrepreneurial mindset, map a business using the Business Model Canvas, and explain how small businesses drive economic growth.

What you will make: Completed BMC for the conglomerate (full group) + one-page division canvas per specialization role (8177) + brief participation summary per 8175 student documenting their contribution to the canvas.

Your role: Management specialization students each own a BMC section that maps to their role: General Manager owns key activities and key partnerships; Store Manager owns revenue streams; Production Manager owns key resources and cost structure; Wholesale Manager owns key partnerships and cost structure.

The work: Students complete a Business Model Canvas for the conglomerate and then a simplified canvas for their own division. Management students lead the exercise; marketing students contribute the customer segment and value proposition sections from a marketing perspective.

Go deeper:

- Entrepreneurship opportunities (Task 54): beyond the conglomerate, what businesses could a student start using the skills and equipment in this program? Students identify at least one real entrepreneurship opportunity tied to their specialization.
- BMC in depth (Task 56): management students present their division canvas to the class and defend their choices — what is the value proposition of the Events Division? How does it differ from the School Store's?
- Small business economic impact (Task 57): students calculate the potential economic impact of the conglomerate on the school community — how much money could circulate through the school store, the league, and events in one school year?

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Identify entrepreneurship opportunities related to sports, entertainment, and event management.
- Describe the entrepreneurial mindset.
- Identify the steps involved in starting a new business, using the Business Model Canvas (BMC).
- Describe how small businesses drive job creation, income generation, and economic growth.

Use these terms accurately: Entrepreneurship, Entrepreneurial mindset, Business Model Canvas (BMC), Value proposition, Revenue stream, Cost structure.
```
