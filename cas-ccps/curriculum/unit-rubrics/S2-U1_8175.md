## Prompt template

```
Branding, Licensing & Legal Foundations (S2-U1)

Students can explain branding, describe branding in sports/entertainment, explain product licensing and brand protection, and describe the rebranding process.

What you will make: Conglomerate Brand Identity Guide — name, logo, color palette, typography, voice, and usage rules. Minimum 4 pages. Includes a one-page legal summary: what is owned, what is licensed, what is prohibited.

Your role: Brand & Creative rotation students lead design execution. They produce logo drafts, present options to both classes, and incorporate feedback before finalizing. They also research one real sports or entertainment brand's trademark and licensing structure.

The work: Students in Creative Studio develop the conglomerate brand identity: name, logo, color palette, typography, and brand voice. This is not a design exercise in isolation — it is done with reference to licensing rules, trademark basics, and the ethical framework that governs what the brand can claim.

Go deeper:

- Branding as marketing strategy (Task 57): how does the conglomerate brand create an emotional connection with the school community? Students articulate the brand promise — what does interacting with this program feel like?
- Rebranding (Task 59): students study one major rebrand in sports or entertainment and present a case analysis — what triggered it, what changed, what was the outcome, and what could the conglomerate learn from it?
- Licensing revenue (Task 58): how could the conglomerate eventually license its brand to other student organizations or school departments? Students sketch a hypothetical licensing model.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Explain branding.
- Explain the concept of branding as it relates to sports, entertainment, and event marketing.
- Explain product licensing and brand protection.
- Explain the concept of and reasons for rebranding.

Use these terms accurately: Brand, Trademark, Licensing, Brand protection, Rebranding, ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act), Trade dress.
```
