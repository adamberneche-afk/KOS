## Prompt template

```
Branding, Licensing & Legal Foundations (S2-U1)

Students can identify ethical issues, investigate social/environmental concerns, explain licensing purposes, and describe how legal requirements shape operations.

What you will make: Conglomerate Brand Identity Guide — name, logo, color palette, typography, voice, and usage rules. Minimum 4 pages. Includes a one-page legal summary: what is owned, what is licensed, what is prohibited.

Your role: General Manager and relevant management students research VHSL licensing requirements, ADA compliance for events, and contract basics (Task 69). They produce the legal summary page of the Brand Identity Guide and draft a simple licensing acknowledgment for any external use of the conglomerate brand.

The work: Students in Creative Studio develop the conglomerate brand identity: name, logo, color palette, typography, and brand voice. This is not a design exercise in isolation — it is done with reference to licensing rules, trademark basics, and the ethical framework that governs what the brand can claim.

Go deeper:

- Ethical issues (Task 63): students identify at least three ethical issues that could arise in the conglomerate's operations — a design that appropriates cultural imagery, a sponsorship from a controversial brand, a social media post that misrepresents results.
- Contracts (Task 69): what contracts does the conglomerate need? Students identify at least three: a vendor agreement for blank garment suppliers, a licensing acknowledgment for brand use, and a WBL participation agreement.
- ADA compliance (Task 72): Events Manager students research ADA requirements for the school's event spaces. What accommodations must be in place for esports matches open to the public?
- Legal requirements and operations (Task 73): how do VHSL rules, school board policy, and Virginia state law each constrain what the conglomerate can do? Students map the regulatory layers.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Identify ethical issues related to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Investigate how social and environmental concerns affect decisions in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Identify purposes, types, and terms of contracts.
- Examine the role of labor and antitrust law in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Identify unions relevant to labor relations in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Explain how the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) has influenced the planning of events and design of facilities.
- Explain how legal requirements influence operations in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Describe the effects of national, state, and local laws and regulations on the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Identify the purposes of licensing.
- Describe external factors affecting the sports, entertainment, and event industries.

Use these terms accurately: Brand, Trademark, Licensing, Brand protection, Rebranding, ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act), Trade dress.

Write your response in the response zone below.
```
