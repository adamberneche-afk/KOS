## Prompt template

```
Product Development & Market Positioning (S2-U2)

Students can explain product/service planning, the product life cycle, market research role, primary research survey design, product positioning, and uncontrollable factors.

What you will make: Two deliverables: (1) School community survey — designed, distributed, and analyzed by Marketing Advisor rotation students. Minimum 5 questions, minimum 30 responses. (2) Product catalog v1.0 — SKU list with product name, description, target customer, positioning statement, and suggested price range for each item.

Your role: Marketing Advisor rotation students lead both deliverables. Brand & Creative rotation students contribute product visualization sketches for the catalog. Sales & Customer Experience rotation students contribute insights on what they observe customers asking for.

The work: Marketing Advisor rotation students produce the first product catalog — a structured SKU list with positioning statements for each item. What does the school store carry? What custom items does Apparel & Merch produce? What are the product tiers (standard vs. premium)? Students back every product decision with market research data from a school community survey.

Go deeper:

- Product life cycle application (Task 61): students plot three existing school merchandise items on a product life cycle curve — which are in growth, which are in maturity, which have declined? How does this affect what the store should carry?
- Survey design (Task 63): students apply research design principles — unbiased questions, sample size, response scale — and present their methodology alongside their findings. This is a real research document, not a worksheet.
- Positioning statements (Task 64): each SKU in the product catalog has a one-sentence positioning statement written to the structure: 'For [target customer], [product name] is the [category] that [differentiating benefit] because [reason to believe].'

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Explain product and service planning in sports, entertainment, and events.
- Explain product life cycle.
- Describe the role of market research.
- Create a survey to conduct primary research.
- Explain the role of product positioning.
- Describe the impact of uncontrollable factors on sports, entertainment, and event product development.

Use these terms accurately: Product life cycle, Product positioning, Primary research, SKU (Stock Keeping Unit), Uncontrollable factors, Market research.

Write your response in the response zone below.
```
