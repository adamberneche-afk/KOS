## Rubric

```
ASSIGNMENT: Economics of Sports, Entertainment & Events (S3-U1)
COURSE: 8175 Sports Entertainment and Event Marketing
UNIT OBJECTIVE: Students can explain economic concepts, how the business cycle affects the industry, supply and demand, and competition in the sports/entertainment marketplace.
PLANNED WORK PRODUCT: Two deliverables: (1) Economic environment brief — one page per student summarizing current conditions affecting the conglomerate's market: consumer spending trends, material cost trends, and competitive landscape. (2) Wholesale sourcing decision brief — Wholesale Manager produces a one-page document recommending whether to place the first order now or delay, based on economic conditions and cost analysis.
COURSE ROLE: Marketing rotation students produce the economic environment brief with emphasis on consumer behavior and competitive landscape. Promotions & Sponsorship rotation students analyze how economic conditions affect sponsorship availability for the esports league.
COMPETENCIES ASSESSED: 8175-98, 8175-99, 8175-100, 8175-101

CRITERIA

Criterion 1: Describing the Economics of the Sports, Entertainment, and Event Industries
- 8175-98 Explain economic concepts and their relationship to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    * Evidence of definitions of major economic concepts
    * Evidence of scarcity
    * Evidence of opportunity cost
    * Evidence of trade-offs
    * Evidence of incentives
    * Evidence of monopoly
    * Evidence of identification of controllable economic factors (e.g., price, value)
    * Evidence of identification of uncontrollable economic factors (e.g., income, population)
- 8175-99 (Optional) Explain how the phases in the business cycle affect the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    * Evidence of a description of the business cycle phases (e.g., prosperity, recession, depression, recovery)
    * Evidence of factors that affect the business cycle (e.g., government regulations, consumer behaviors, ROI)
    * Evidence of examples of the business cycle from sports, entertainment, and events (e.g., pricing factors, cost of customer acquisition)
- 8175-100 Explain the concept of supply and demand.
    * Evidence of a definition of supply and demand with examples from the sports, entertainment, and event industries
    * Evidence of the relationship of supply and demand to price and value associated with the sports, entertainment, and event industries
- 8175-101 Explain competition as it relates to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    * Evidence of direct competition
    * Evidence of indirect competition
    * Evidence of price competition
    * Evidence of non-price competition

PASSING STANDARD
The work passes when the planned work product shows evidence for every competency listed above, in the student's own words, applied to the class conglomerate (its divisions, school store, events and esports program) or a real organization in sports, entertainment or events, and uses the unit vocabulary accurately (Supply and demand, Business cycle, Opportunity cost, Economic indicator, Direct competition, Economic impact, Consumer confidence). Each criterion needs specific examples, not general statements. Work that restates definitions without applying them, or leaves any listed competency without evidence, does not pass.
```

```
Students can explain economic concepts, how the business cycle affects the industry, supply and demand, and competition in the sports/entertainment marketplace.

The work: Before placing the first purchase order, the Wholesale Manager conducts an economic environment check: what is the current cost of raw materials? Has inflation affected blank garment pricing? What is the school community's spending capacity? This unit gives students the vocabulary and framework for that analysis.

Go deeper:

- Business cycle application (Task 99, optional): students map the sports and entertainment industry's performance across the last two economic cycles — 2008 recession and 2020 pandemic. What contracted first? What recovered last? What does this tell the conglomerate about financial resilience?
- Competition analysis (Task 101): students conduct a competitive analysis for the school store — who else serves the same customer with similar products, and what is the conglomerate's defensible advantage?
- Supply and demand visualization (Task 100): students graph a demand curve for esports merchandise using the survey data collected in Stage 2. At what price does demand fall significantly? At what price does it peak?

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Explain economic concepts and their relationship to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- (Optional) Explain how the phases in the business cycle affect the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Explain the concept of supply and demand.
- Explain competition as it relates to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.

Use these terms accurately: Supply and demand, Business cycle, Opportunity cost, Economic indicator, Direct competition, Economic impact, Consumer confidence.
```
