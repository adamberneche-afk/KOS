```
ASSIGNMENT: Economics of Sports, Entertainment & Events (S3-U1)
COURSE: 8175 Sports Entertainment and Event Marketing
UNIT OBJECTIVE: Students can explain economic concepts, how the business cycle affects the industry, supply and demand, and competition in the sports/entertainment marketplace.
COMPETENCIES ASSESSED: 8175-98, 8175-99, 8175-100, 8175-101

CRITERIA

Criterion 1: Describing the Economics of the Sports, Entertainment, and Event Industries
- 8175-98 Explain economic concepts and their relationship to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    * Evidence of definitions of major economic concepts
    * Evidence of scarcity
    * Evidence of opportunity cost
    * Evidence of trade-offs
    * Evidence of incentives
    * Evidence of monopoly
    * Evidence of identification of controllable economic factors (e.g., price, value)
    * Evidence of identification of uncontrollable economic factors (e.g., income, population)
- 8175-99 (Optional) Explain how the phases in the business cycle affect the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    * Evidence of a description of the business cycle phases (e.g., prosperity, recession, depression, recovery)
    * Evidence of factors that affect the business cycle (e.g., government regulations, consumer behaviors, ROI)
    * Evidence of examples of the business cycle from sports, entertainment, and events (e.g., pricing factors, cost of customer acquisition)
- 8175-100 Explain the concept of supply and demand.
    * Evidence of a definition of supply and demand with examples from the sports, entertainment, and event industries
    * Evidence of the relationship of supply and demand to price and value associated with the sports, entertainment, and event industries
- 8175-101 Explain competition as it relates to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    * Evidence of direct competition
    * Evidence of indirect competition
    * Evidence of price competition
    * Evidence of non-price competition

PASSING STANDARD
The work passes when it shows evidence for every competency listed above, in the student's own words, applied to a real or realistic organization in sports, entertainment or events, and uses the unit vocabulary accurately (What students do, Supply and demand, Business cycle, Opportunity cost, Economic indicator, Direct competition, Economic impact, Consumer confidence). Each criterion needs specific examples, not general statements. Work that restates definitions without applying them, or leaves any listed competency without evidence, does not pass.
```
