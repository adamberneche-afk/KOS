## Rubric

```
ASSIGNMENT: Market Research (S3-U2)
COURSE: 8175 Sports Entertainment and Event Marketing
UNIT OBJECTIVE: Students can describe the role of market research, create a primary research survey, and explain research's importance in developing a marketing plan.
PLANNED WORK PRODUCT: Three deliverables: (1) Market segmentation analysis — identifying and describing at least three distinct customer segments within the school community. (2) Full research project report — problem statement, methodology, data, analysis, findings, and recommendations. (3) Research presentation — 10-minute presentation to both classes, with Q&A, followed by a formal handoff document to the Wholesale Manager and League Manager.
COURSE ROLE: Marketing Advisor rotation students lead the research project design and execution. Brand & Creative students contribute any visual data displays. All 8175 rotation tracks review the segmentation analysis and identify how it affects their specific function.
COMPETENCIES ASSESSED: 8175-62, 8175-63, 8175-103

CRITERIA

Criterion 1: Describing Product Development
- 8175-62 Describe the role of market research.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * define primary research and secondary research
    * emphasize that market research involves obtaining necessary information to make sound product-development decisions (e.g., data to ensure that products are relevant to target markets)
- 8175-63 Create a survey to conduct primary research.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * identifying survey goals
    * determining the audience for the survey
    * deciding on the appropriate length of the survey
    * using different types of questions (e.g., multiple choice, open-ended)

Criterion 2: Exploring the Marketing Plan
- 8175-103 Describe the importance of research in developing a marketing plan.
    * Description should include the use of both primary and secondary market research methods.

PASSING STANDARD
The work passes when the planned work product does what the assignment asks and shows each competency listed above in at least one specific way, in the student's own words, applied to the class conglomerate (its divisions, school store, events and esports program) or a real organization in sports, entertainment or events, and uses the unit vocabulary accurately (Market research, Market segmentation, Primary research, Secondary research, Price sensitivity, Data triangulation, Recommendation). Specific examples, not general statements. Work that restates definitions without applying them, or leaves a listed competency with no evidence at all, does not pass. Judge each competency only by what this assignment asks the student to do. The lines under a competency are examples of what evidence can look like, not a checklist: one specific, well-done example that fits the task is enough, and a part of a competency the task does not ask for is never required.
```

```
Market Research (S3-U2)

Students can describe the role of market research, create a primary research survey, and explain research's importance in developing a marketing plan.

What you will make: Three deliverables: (1) Market segmentation analysis — identifying and describing at least three distinct customer segments within the school community. (2) Full research project report — problem statement, methodology, data, analysis, findings, and recommendations. (3) Research presentation — 10-minute presentation to both classes, with Q&A, followed by a formal handoff document to the Wholesale Manager and League Manager.

Your role: Marketing Advisor rotation students lead the research project design and execution. Brand & Creative students contribute any visual data displays. All 8175 rotation tracks review the segmentation analysis and identify how it affects their specific function.

The work: Students conduct a full market research project on the school community as a consumer base. Segmentation analysis identifies which student groups are most likely to buy esports merchandise, attend events, or use the school store. Findings are presented to the full class and formally handed to the Wholesale Manager as sourcing guidance.

Go deeper:

- Research in marketing plan context (Task 103): students connect each research finding to a specific section of the marketing plan framework — where does the segmentation analysis go? Where do the price sensitivity findings go? This is not abstract; it is building the Stage 8 deliverable section by section.
- Primary research design (Task 63): students evaluate the Stage 2 survey against research design principles — what worked, what introduced bias, what should be redesigned for the next research cycle?
- Secondary research sourcing (Task 62 context): students identify three secondary sources relevant to the conglomerate — VHSL participation data, national esports audience demographics, school enrollment data — and explain how each informs a specific business decision.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Describe the role of market research.
- Create a survey to conduct primary research.
- Describe the importance of research in developing a marketing plan.

Use these terms accurately: Market research, Market segmentation, Primary research, Secondary research, Price sensitivity, Data triangulation, Recommendation.
```
