Market Research (S3-U2)

Students can explain market research applications, identify data sources, develop a market segmentation analysis, design a full research project, and evaluate results.

What you will make: Three deliverables: (1) Market segmentation analysis — identifying and describing at least three distinct customer segments within the school community. (2) Full research project report — problem statement, methodology, data, analysis, findings, and recommendations. (3) Research presentation — 10-minute presentation to both classes, with Q&A, followed by a formal handoff document to the Wholesale Manager and League Manager.

Your role: 8177 management students conduct the segmentation analysis (Task 81) and develop the full research project (Tasks 82–84) as the deeper assessment. Wholesale Manager uses findings to finalize the first purchase order. League Manager uses findings to set initial league registration targets and merchandise SKU priorities.

The work: Students conduct a full market research project on the school community as a consumer base. Segmentation analysis identifies which student groups are most likely to buy esports merchandise, attend events, or use the school store. Findings are presented to the full class and formally handed to the Wholesale Manager as sourcing guidance.

Go deeper:

- Market segmentation analysis (Task 81): management students produce a formal segmentation document with at least three segments, each described by demographic, psychographic, and behavioral characteristics, with a recommended marketing approach for each.
- Full research project development (Tasks 82–83): the 8177 research project goes beyond the 8175 survey — it includes observational data (store traffic patterns, lunch period behavior) alongside survey data, and uses secondary sources to triangulate findings.
- Results evaluation (Task 84): management students write a formal evaluation of the research project — what did it answer, what did it fail to answer, what would the next research cycle address, and what decisions should be made based on the evidence?

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Explain market research and its use in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Explain the need for market research for all functions of marketing.
- Identify sources of data for market research in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Describe external factors affecting the sports, entertainment, and event marketing process.
- Develop a market segmentation analysis.
- Describe the steps in developing a market research project.
- Develop a market research project.
- Evaluate the results of a market research project.

Use these terms accurately: Market research, Market segmentation, Primary research, Secondary research, Price sensitivity, Data triangulation, Recommendation.
