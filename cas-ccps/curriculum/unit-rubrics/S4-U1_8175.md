## Prompt template

```
Pricing Strategies & Revenue (S4-U1)

Students can explain pricing factors and identify pricing strategies used in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.

What you will make: Three deliverables: (1) Pricing worksheet — completed for every SKU in the product catalog, with strategy rationale. (2) Conglomerate budget v1.0 — income and expenditure projections for the semester, built by management students using the budget template below. (3) Break-even analysis — how many units of the first jersey run must sell to cover COGS and overhead?

Your role: Sales & Customer Experience rotation students complete the pricing worksheets for each SKU, drawing on market research price sensitivity data. Marketing Advisor rotation students review and challenge the pricing rationale — does each price reflect the positioning strategy from Stage 2? Promotions rotation students identify which pricing strategies create the most promotable story.

The work: Every SKU in the product catalog gets a pricing worksheet completed: COGS from the Wholesale Manager's purchase order, overhead allocation, strategy selection, final proposed price, and a one-sentence rationale. Store Manager compiles into the store's official price list. Production Manager reviews for feasibility — does the margin justify the production run?

Go deeper:

- Pricing strategy comparison (Task 67): students evaluate three different pricing strategies for the same SKU and present the tradeoffs — which strategy maximizes short-term revenue, which builds long-term market share, which best supports the brand positioning from Stage 2?
- Psychological pricing application: students redesign the store's price list using psychological pricing principles and calculate the projected revenue difference on a 50-unit run.
- Event pricing: how does pricing an esports match ticket differ from pricing a jersey? Students apply the pricing framework to a service (event attendance) and identify which factors change.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Explain pricing and the factors that affect pricing decisions.
- Identify pricing strategies as they relate to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.

Use these terms accurately: Cost of goods sold (COGS), Gross margin, Cost-plus pricing, Value-based pricing, Break-even point, Fixed cost, Variable cost, Revenue stream, Income statement.

Write your response in the response zone below.
```
