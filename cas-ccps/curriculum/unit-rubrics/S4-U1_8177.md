```
ASSIGNMENT: Pricing Strategies & Revenue (S4-U1)
COURSE: 8177 Sports Entertainment and Event Management
UNIT OBJECTIVE: Students can identify budget components, identify revenue sources and expenditures, and analyze financial statements.
COMPETENCIES ASSESSED: 8177-85, 8177-86, 8177-87, 8177-88

CRITERIA

Criterion 1: Exploring Revenues and Expenditures
- 8177-85 Identify the components of a budget.
    * Evidence of fixed expenses (e.g., rent/mortgage, insurance, cost of borrowing)
    * Evidence of variable expenses (e.g., salaries, supplies, equipment, utilities, taxes, maintenance, marketing)
    * Evidence of income (e.g., sources of revenue)
- 8177-86 Identify revenue sources related to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    * Evidence of food/beverage options
    * Evidence of premium services such as at-seat service, suites, and touch screen order delivery
    * Evidence of exclusive sponsorships
    * Evidence of parking
    * Evidence of ticketing/registration
    * Evidence of restaurants
    * Evidence of retail stores and kiosks
    * Evidence of merchandising
    * Evidence of corporate partnerships
    * Evidence of licensing and royalties
    * Evidence of broadcast and media rights
- 8177-87 Identify expenditures related to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    * Evidence of food and beverage options
    * Evidence of premium services such as at-seat service, suites, and touch screen order delivery
    * Evidence of exclusive sponsorships
    * Evidence of parking
    * Evidence of ticketing/registration
    * Evidence of restaurants
    * Evidence of retail stores and kiosks
    * Evidence of merchandising
- 8177-88 Analyze financial statements.
    * Evidence of balance sheet
    * Evidence of income statement (profit and loss)
    * Evidence of cash flow statement

PASSING STANDARD
The work passes when it shows evidence for every competency listed above, in the student's own words, applied to a real or realistic organization in sports, entertainment or events, and uses the unit vocabulary accurately (Cost of goods sold (COGS), Gross margin, Cost-plus pricing, Value-based pricing, Break-even point, Fixed cost, Variable cost, Revenue stream, Income statement). Each criterion needs specific examples, not general statements. Work that restates definitions without applying them, or leaves any listed competency without evidence, does not pass.
```
