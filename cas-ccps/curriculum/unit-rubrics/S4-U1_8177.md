Pricing Strategies & Revenue (S4-U1)

Students can identify budget components, identify revenue sources and expenditures, and analyze financial statements.

What you will make: Three deliverables: (1) Pricing worksheet — completed for every SKU in the product catalog, with strategy rationale. (2) Conglomerate budget v1.0 — income and expenditure projections for the semester, built by management students using the budget template below. (3) Break-even analysis — how many units of the first jersey run must sell to cover COGS and overhead?

Your role: Store Manager drafts the official price list and submits to General Manager for approval. Production Manager contributes COGS data from the first production run. Wholesale Manager contributes sourcing costs. General Manager consolidates into the budget v1.0. All 8177 students complete the financial statement analysis task (Task 88) using a provided sample income statement from a comparable school store operation.

The work: Every SKU in the product catalog gets a pricing worksheet completed: COGS from the Wholesale Manager's purchase order, overhead allocation, strategy selection, final proposed price, and a one-sentence rationale. Store Manager compiles into the store's official price list. Production Manager reviews for feasibility — does the margin justify the production run?

Go deeper:

- Budget components in depth (Task 85): students identify every line item in the conglomerate's operating budget — not just production costs, but time costs, equipment depreciation, and overhead allocation. What does it actually cost to run this program for one semester?
- Revenue source diversification (Task 86): students map all potential revenue streams for the conglomerate and rate each by feasibility and timeline. Which revenue sources can be activated immediately (store sales)? Which require more development (sponsorship)?
- Expenditure management (Task 87): students distinguish fixed costs (equipment, subscriptions) from variable costs (materials per unit) and calculate the conglomerate's break-even point at different production volumes.
- Financial statement analysis (Task 88): students analyze a provided sample income statement from a comparable school merchandise operation, identify key ratios (gross margin, operating margin), and benchmark against the conglomerate's projected figures.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Identify the components of a budget.
- Identify revenue sources related to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Identify expenditures related to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Analyze financial statements.

Use these terms accurately: Cost of goods sold (COGS), Gross margin, Cost-plus pricing, Value-based pricing, Break-even point, Fixed cost, Variable cost, Revenue stream, Income statement.

Write your response in the response zone below.
