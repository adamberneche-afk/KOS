ASSIGNMENT: Ethics, Media & Social Responsibility (S5-U1)
COURSE: 8177 Sports Entertainment and Event Management
UNIT OBJECTIVE: Students can identify ethical issues, investigate social/environmental concerns, examine media bias effects on public perception, and describe community value of the industry.
PLANNED WORK PRODUCT: Three deliverables: (1) Social Media & Communications Policy — one page, approved by General Manager. (2) Esports Code of Conduct — drafted by League Manager with input from both classes. (3) Ethical case analysis — each student analyzes one real ethical incident from the sports, entertainment, or esports industry and presents findings: what happened, what the organization did, what they should have done, and what the conglomerate learns from it.
COURSE ROLE: General Manager approves the Social Media Policy and Esports Code of Conduct. Events Manager reviews for event-specific implications (what can be posted during a live event, how complaints at events are handled). All 8177 students complete the media bias analysis using a provided pair of articles covering the same esports or sports event from different perspectives.
COMPETENCIES ASSESSED: 8177-63, 8177-64, 8177-65, 8177-66

CRITERIA

Criterion 1: Exploring Ethical Issues in the Sports, Entertainment, and Event Industries
- 8177-63 Identify ethical issues related to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * amateur, collegiate, and professional athletics
    * entertainment and event industries
    * sports and entertainment media
    * social media
- 8177-64 Investigate how social and environmental concerns affect decisions in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * community-driven projects
    * relationships with charitable partners
    * sustainable practices and procedures
    * corporate social responsibility guidelines
- 8177-65 Examine the effect of media bias on public perception and opinion.

- 8177-66 Describe the value of the sports, entertainment, and event industries to the community.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * encouraging a sense of community
    * advocating for health and wellness awareness
    * elevating community well-being
    * encouraging self-improvement through meaningful experiences
    * supporting economic and business development

Ethics, Media & Social Responsibility (S5-U1)

Students can identify ethical issues, investigate social/environmental concerns, examine media bias effects on public perception, and describe community value of the industry.

What you will make: Three deliverables: (1) Social Media & Communications Policy — one page, approved by General Manager. (2) Esports Code of Conduct — drafted by League Manager with input from both classes. (3) Ethical case analysis — each student analyzes one real ethical incident from the sports, entertainment, or esports industry and presents findings: what happened, what the organization did, what they should have done, and what the conglomerate learns from it.

Your role: General Manager approves the Social Media Policy and Esports Code of Conduct. Events Manager reviews for event-specific implications (what can be posted during a live event, how complaints at events are handled). All 8177 students complete the media bias analysis using a provided pair of articles covering the same esports or sports event from different perspectives.

The work: Students draft the conglomerate's Social Media & Communications Policy — a one-page document that establishes what can be posted, who approves it, how negative feedback is handled, and what the escalation path is for a reputational incident. This becomes a standing operating document for the Media & PR division.

Go deeper:

- Media bias examination (Task 65): students compare two pieces of coverage of the same real sports or esports event from sources with different editorial perspectives. They identify specific language choices, framing decisions, and omissions that reveal bias. How does this inform how the conglomerate produces its own media?
- Social and environmental impact (Task 64): students audit the conglomerate's environmental footprint — sublimation inks, heat press energy use, packaging, event waste. They propose one realistic operational change that reduces impact without eliminating the production capability.
- Community value articulation (Task 66): management students write a one-page community value statement for the conglomerate — what does this program contribute to the school, and how would the school community be different without it? This document is referenced in the administrator reporting artifact.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Identify ethical issues related to the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Investigate how social and environmental concerns affect decisions in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Examine the effect of media bias on public perception and opinion.
- Describe the value of the sports, entertainment, and event industries to the community.

Use these terms accurately: Business ethics, Reputational risk, Media bias, Social responsibility, Escalation path, Code of conduct, Transparency.

Write your response in the response zone below.
