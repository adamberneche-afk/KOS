ASSIGNMENT: Customer Service Skills (S5-U2)
COURSE: 8177 Sports Entertainment and Event Management
UNIT OBJECTIVE: Students can demonstrate customer service techniques, describe retention methods, respond to inquiries/feedback, explain company policy, and explain the value of constructive feedback.
PLANNED WORK PRODUCT: Three deliverables: (1) Conglomerate Customer Service Standards document — two pages, approved by General Manager. (2) Role-play assessment — each student completes two role-plays: one in-person store scenario and one digital/social media scenario. Assessed by a peer using a provided rubric. (3) Complaint resolution log template — a simple tracking document the Store Manager uses to record every complaint, resolution, and follow-up.
COURSE ROLE: General Manager approves the Customer Service Standards. Store Manager drafts the Complaint Resolution Log template and is responsible for maintaining it throughout the year. Events Manager adapts the standards for event-day scenarios. All 8177 students complete the constructive feedback exercise (Task 62) — giving and receiving written feedback on a peer's role-play performance using professional language.
COMPETENCIES ASSESSED: 8177-58, 8177-59, 8177-60, 8177-61, 8177-62

CRITERIA

Criterion 1: Developing Customer Service Skills
- 8177-58 Demonstrate customer-service techniques.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * using greetings appropriate for various professional situations
    * exhibiting politeness and patience
    * practicing active listening (e.g., paying close attention, asking clarifying questions, showing empathy)
    * practicing clear and effective communication (e.g., using simple language, being concise and direct, maintaining professional tone)
    * demonstrating problem-solving skills (e.g., proactively identifying issues, offering multiple solutions)
    * practicing time management and efficiency (e.g., prioritizing tasks, meeting deadlines)
    * being knowledgeable about the product and/or service, answering questions accurately, staying up-to-date
    * being flexible and open to new ideas
    * keeping in mind the value of a customer's positive experiences
    * implementing retention activities (e.g., fan appreciation events, newsletters, surveys)
- 8177-59 Describe methods used to encourage customer retention.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * learning and using customers' names
    * personalizing the experience by remembering customer preferences
    * providing equitable service to all customers
    * exceeding customer expectations
    * following up with sales and customer contacts via notes, emails, or surveys
    * offering incentives for referrals
- 8177-60 Respond to inquiries and suggestions from customers, coworkers, and supervisors in a sports, entertainment, and event context.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * clarifying inquiries
    * locating sources of relevant and accurate information
    * responding in a culturally appropriate, professional, and timely manner
    * using direct, descriptive, and specific language
    * following company policy regarding referral of inquiries to coworkers or supervisors
    * treating every customer contact as a potential sale and retention opportunity
- 8177-61 Explain company policy to a customer in a sports, entertainment, and event context.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * being well-informed regarding policies
    * describing relevant policies simply and accurately
    * keeping abreast of policy changes
    * knowing when to refer a policy matter to a supervisor or specialist
- 8177-62 Explain the importance of constructive feedback in personal and professional growth.
    Evidence can include (examples, not all required):
    * note that feedback given in a positive and courteous way should help the recipient grow, either professionally or personally
    * emphasize the importance of validity, tact, tone, and setting
    * focuses on improvement
    * is specific, actionable, balanced, positive, respectful and supportive
    * offers solutions
    * focuses on behavior, not personality
    * avoids “I” statements

PASSING STANDARD
The work passes when the planned work product does what the assignment asks and shows each competency listed above in at least one specific way, in the student's own words, applied to the class conglomerate (its divisions, school store, events and esports program) or a real organization in sports, entertainment or events, and uses the unit vocabulary accurately (Customer touchpoint, Complaint resolution, Customer retention, Internal customer, Constructive feedback, Service standard, Escalation). Specific examples, not general statements. Work that restates definitions without applying them, or leaves a listed competency with no evidence at all, does not pass. Judge each competency only by what this assignment asks the student to do. The lines under a competency are examples of what evidence can look like, not a checklist: one specific, well-done example that fits the task is enough, and a part of a competency the task does not ask for is never required.

Students can demonstrate customer service techniques, describe retention methods, respond to inquiries/feedback, explain company policy, and explain the value of constructive feedback.

What you will make: Three deliverables: (1) Conglomerate Customer Service Standards document — two pages, approved by General Manager. (2) Role-play assessment — each student completes two role-plays: one in-person store scenario and one digital/social media scenario. Assessed by a peer using a provided rubric. Your role: General Manager approves the Customer Service Standards. Store Manager drafts the Complaint Resolution Log template and is responsible for maintaining it throughout the year. Events Manager adapts the standards for event-day scenarios. All 8177 students complete the constructive feedback exercise (Task 62) — giving and receiving written feedback on a peer's role-play performance using professional language.

The work: Students draft the Conglomerate Customer Service Standards — a two-page document covering in-person store interactions, custom order consultations, social media response standards, event-day customer management, and the complaint resolution process. Each division reviews the standards for role-specific applicability.

Go deeper:

- Customer service demonstration (Task 58): management students conduct a formal customer service demonstration observed and assessed by the teacher — a full simulated store interaction from greeting through complaint resolution, evaluated against the Customer Service Standards they helped write.
- Retention strategy (Task 59): management students design one customer retention initiative for the school store — a loyalty mechanism, a follow-up process for custom orders, or a surprise-and-delight system — and present a simple implementation plan to the General Manager.
- Responding to supervisors and coworkers (Task 60): the conglomerate has internal customers too. Management students identify three scenarios where internal communication failed in a comparable school business program (provided as a case study) and recommend how each should have been handled.
- Constructive feedback cycle (Task 62): management students complete a structured feedback exchange — giving written professional feedback on a peer's role-play, receiving feedback on their own, and reflecting in writing on what they learned from both directions of the exchange.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Demonstrate customer-service techniques.
- Describe methods used to encourage customer retention.
- Respond to inquiries and suggestions from customers, coworkers, and supervisors in a sports, entertainment, and event context.
- Explain company policy to a customer in a sports, entertainment, and event context.
- Explain the importance of constructive feedback in personal and professional growth.

Use these terms accurately: Customer touchpoint, Complaint resolution, Customer retention, Internal customer, Constructive feedback, Service standard, Escalation.

Write your response under “Your response” below.
