ASSIGNMENT: Customer Service Skills (S5-U2)
COURSE: 8177 Sports Entertainment and Event Management
UNIT OBJECTIVE: Students can demonstrate customer service techniques, describe retention methods, respond to inquiries/feedback, explain company policy, and explain the value of constructive feedback.
COMPETENCIES ASSESSED: 8177-58, 8177-59, 8177-60, 8177-61, 8177-62

CRITERIA

Criterion 1: Developing Customer Service Skills
- 8177-58 Demonstrate customer-service techniques.
    * Evidence of using greetings appropriate for various professional situations
    * Evidence of exhibiting politeness and patience
    * Evidence of practicing active listening (e.g., paying close attention, asking clarifying questions, showing empathy)
    * Evidence of practicing clear and effective communication (e.g., using simple language, being concise and direct, maintaining professional tone)
    * Evidence of demonstrating problem-solving skills (e.g., proactively identifying issues, offering multiple solutions)
    * Evidence of practicing time management and efficiency (e.g., prioritizing tasks, meeting deadlines)
    * Evidence of being knowledgeable about the product and/or service, answering questions accurately, staying up-to-date
    * Evidence of being flexible and open to new ideas
    * Evidence of keeping in mind the value of a customer's positive experiences
    * Evidence of implementing retention activities (e.g., fan appreciation events, newsletters, surveys)
- 8177-59 Describe methods used to encourage customer retention.
    * Evidence of learning and using customers' names
    * Evidence of personalizing the experience by remembering customer preferences
    * Evidence of providing equitable service to all customers
    * Evidence of exceeding customer expectations
    * Evidence of following up with sales and customer contacts via notes, emails, or surveys
    * Evidence of offering incentives for referrals
- 8177-60 Respond to inquiries and suggestions from customers, coworkers, and supervisors in a sports, entertainment, and event context.
    * Evidence of clarifying inquiries
    * Evidence of locating sources of relevant and accurate information
    * Evidence of responding in a culturally appropriate, professional, and timely manner
    * Evidence of using direct, descriptive, and specific language
    * Evidence of following company policy regarding referral of inquiries to coworkers or supervisors
    * Evidence of treating every customer contact as a potential sale and retention opportunity
- 8177-61 Explain company policy to a customer in a sports, entertainment, and event context.
    * Evidence of being well-informed regarding policies
    * Evidence of describing relevant policies simply and accurately
    * Evidence of keeping abreast of policy changes
    * Evidence of knowing when to refer a policy matter to a supervisor or specialist
- 8177-62 Explain the importance of constructive feedback in personal and professional growth.
    * Evidence of note that feedback given in a positive and courteous way should help the recipient grow, either professionally or personally
    * Evidence of emphasize the importance of validity, tact, tone, and setting
    * Evidence of focuses on improvement
    * Evidence of is specific, actionable, balanced, positive, respectful and supportive
    * Evidence of offers solutions
    * Evidence of focuses on behavior, not personality
    * Evidence of avoids “I” statements
    * Evidence of avoids “I” statements

PASSING STANDARD
The work passes when it shows evidence for every competency listed above, in the student's own words, applied to a real or realistic organization in sports, entertainment or events, and uses the unit vocabulary accurately (What students do, Customer touchpoint, Complaint resolution, Customer retention, Internal customer, Constructive feedback, Service standard, Escalation). Each criterion needs specific examples, not general statements. Work that restates definitions without applying them, or leaves any listed competency without evidence, does not pass.
