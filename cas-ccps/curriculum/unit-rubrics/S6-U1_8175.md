Digital Media & E-Commerce (S6-U1)

Students can define current electronic marketing tools, identify methods for attracting/retaining customers digitally, identify target markets' preferred platforms, compare traditional and digital marketing, and explain digital revenue generation.

What you will make: Three deliverables: (1) Platform strategy document — which platforms the conglomerate operates, which audience segment each serves, who owns each channel, and what the posting cadence is. Based on Stage 3 market research data. (2) Four-week content calendar — planned posts across all active platforms, including match coverage schedule, store promotion posts, and event announcements. (3) Digital order intake process — a simple form or link that allows custom order inquiries outside of school hours, connected to the Store Manager's workflow.

Your role: Promotions & Sponsorship rotation students lead the platform strategy and content calendar. They are responsible for the first four weeks of scheduled content across all channels. Brand & Creative rotation students produce the visual templates for recurring content types — match day graphics, store product posts, event announcement formats — so that content production is efficient and brand-consistent.

The work: Students audit the conglomerate's current digital footprint, build a platform strategy using market research data, establish a content calendar for the next four weeks, and connect the school store's POS to a simple digital order intake process. The large format printer produces a digital channel map — which platform serves which audience segment — for classroom display.

Go deeper:

- Platform comparison (Task 80): students map each active platform against the school community's three market segments from Stage 3 — which segment uses which platform most, what content format performs best on each, and what posting frequency is sustainable with six computers and a shared production workstation.
- Traditional vs. digital comparison (Task 81): students identify one marketing action the conglomerate takes in each category — a poster printed on the large format printer (traditional) and an Instagram post (digital) — and compare reach, cost, targeting precision, and measurability for each.
- Revenue attribution (Task 82): students design a simple tracking system to measure which digital channel drives the most in-store traffic and custom order inquiries. How will the conglomerate know whether its Instagram account is worth the time investment?

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Define current electronic marketing tools and platforms.
- Identify methods for attracting customers and establishing and maintaining customer relationships.
- Identify target markets’ preferred platforms for various electronic marketing tools.
- Compare traditional marketing and digital media marketing.
- Explain how digital media can be used to generate revenue.

Use these terms accurately: Distribution channel, Content calendar, Conversion, Engagement, E-commerce, Streaming, Discord.
