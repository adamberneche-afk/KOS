Your role: Store Manager develops the digital order intake process and connects it to the POS workflow. General Manager reviews the platform strategy for alignment with the conglomerate's brand and ethical standards from Stage 5. Esports Division / League Manager builds and administers the Discord server for the school community league, establishing channel structure, moderation rules, and posting permissions.

The work: Students audit the conglomerate's current digital footprint, build a platform strategy using market research data, establish a content calendar for the next four weeks, and connect the school store's POS to a simple digital order intake process. The large format printer produces a digital channel map — which platform serves which audience segment — for classroom display.

Go deeper:

- E-commerce operations deep dive (Task 47): management students map the full digital transaction lifecycle for a custom order — from inquiry form submission through payment, production scheduling, fulfillment, and follow-up. They identify every point where a delay or error could occur and document the contingency for each.
- Digital security and data handling: the order intake form collects student names and contact information. Management students draft a simple data handling policy — what information is collected, how it is stored, who has access, and how it is disposed of. This connects to Task 16 (information security) from Stage 0.
- Cash flow implications of e-commerce: if the digital order intake process accepts payment at order submission, the conglomerate receives revenue before producing the item. Students calculate the cash flow advantage of this model vs. pay-at-pickup, and identify the risk if an order cannot be fulfilled.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Identify how e-commerce and digital transactions affect sports, entertainment, and event businesses.

Use these terms accurately: Distribution channel, Content calendar, Conversion, Engagement, E-commerce, Streaming, Discord.
