## Prompt template

```
Safety, Security & Risk Management (S6-U2)

Students deepen Task 22 (workplace safety) in the context of event venues, equipment operation, and live event environments.

What you will make: Three deliverables: (1) Conglomerate Risk Register — a table listing every identified risk across all divisions, rated by likelihood and severity, with a mitigation strategy for each. (2) Production Area Safety Protocol — equipment-specific safety procedures for the sublimation printer, heat press, and large format printer. Laminated and posted at each machine. (3) Event Safety & Emergency Preparedness Plan — a written plan covering crowd management, medical response, technology failure, and evacuation for the first home esports match. Submitted to administration.

Your role: All 8175 rotation students contribute to the Risk Register by identifying risks specific to their current rotation track — Creative Studio students identify design workstation risks, Sales students identify store floor risks. Students in the Promotions track identify digital security risks (account compromise, inappropriate content posted without authorization).

The work: Students produce three safety and security documents: the Production Area Safety Protocol (Apparel & Merch), the School Store Safety Checklist, and the Event Safety & Emergency Preparedness Plan. The third document is submitted to school administration before the first public event. Events Manager owns the preparedness plan; General Manager signs off; teacher reviews before submission.

Go deeper:

- Workplace safety application (Task 22 extension): students write the safety orientation script for a new student joining the Apparel & Merch division — a plain-language walkthrough of every piece of equipment they will use, what can go wrong, and what to do if it does. This becomes part of the onboarding process for new rotation students.
- Risk identification exercise: students spend 10 minutes in each division space (production area, store, event space) and document every physical hazard they observe. Results are compiled into the Risk Register and cross-referenced with the mitigation strategies management students propose.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Demonstrate workplace safety.

Use these terms accurately: Risk assessment, Risk register, Incident commander, Contingency plan, Life safety, Tabletop exercise, Risk matrix.

Write your response in the response zone below.
```
