Students can examine risk assessment and management strategies, examine uncontrollable variable challenges, explain security types, explain venue/event security needs, and explain emergency preparedness plans.

(2) Production Area Safety Protocol — equipment-specific safety procedures for the sublimation printer, heat press, and large format printer. Laminated and posted at each machine. (3) Event Safety & Emergency Preparedness Plan — a written plan covering crowd management, medical response, technology failure, and evacuation for the first home esports match. Submitted to administration.

Your role: Events Manager drafts and owns the Emergency Preparedness Plan. Production Manager drafts and owns the Production Area Safety Protocol. General Manager compiles the Risk Register and submits the preparedness plan to administration. All 8177 students complete a written risk assessment for their specific division role — what are the top three risks in my function, and what is my mitigation for each?

The work: Students produce three safety and security documents: the Production Area Safety Protocol (Apparel & Merch), the School Store Safety Checklist, and the Event Safety & Emergency Preparedness Plan. The third document is submitted to school administration before the first public event. Events Manager owns the preparedness plan; General Manager signs off; teacher reviews before submission.

Go deeper:

- Risk assessment framework (Task 67): management students build a formal 3x3 risk matrix — likelihood (low/medium/high) vs. severity (low/medium/high) — and plot every identified risk from the Risk Register. Risks in the high/high quadrant require immediate mitigation. Students present their matrix and top three priority risks to the class.
- Uncontrollable variables planning (Task 68): students develop contingency protocols for three specific uncontrollable scenarios at the first home match — streaming failure, no-show team, and severe weather affecting the venue. Each contingency specifies the decision point, who decides, and what the communication to spectators looks like.
- Venue security assessment (Task 90): Events Manager and League Manager conduct a walkthrough of the planned event space and complete a formal security assessment checklist — exits, sight lines, crowd capacity, ADA access, equipment security, cash handling points. Gaps are documented and resolved before the event.
- Emergency preparedness plan in depth (Task 91): the plan submitted to administration must include: incident command structure (who is in charge of what during an emergency), communication chain (who calls 911, who notifies the principal, who manages the crowd), evacuation route map, and post-incident documentation protocol.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Examine the importance of risk assessment and management strategies in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Examine the challenges posed by uncontrollable variables in the sports, entertainment, and event industries.
- Explain the importance and types of indoor and outdoor security (i.e., life and property safety).
- Explain the security needs of a venue and/or event.
- Explain the use of emergency preparedness plans to address threats to a venue and/or event.

Use these terms accurately: Risk assessment, Risk register, Incident commander, Contingency plan, Life safety, Tabletop exercise, Risk matrix.
