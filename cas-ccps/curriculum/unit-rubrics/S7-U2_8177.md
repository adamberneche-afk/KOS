The work: Students plan and execute the full on-site sales operation for the first home esports match. Pre-event: merchandise table layout, inventory selection and quantity, signage production, bundle offer finalization, and sales role assignments. Event-day: table setup, active selling throughout the event, transaction tracking. Post-event: revenue reconciliation, unsold inventory return to store, and sales performance report.

Go deeper:

- On-site revenue stream analysis (Task 46): management students calculate the revenue potential of each active stream at the first match under three scenarios — low attendance (40 students), target attendance (80 students), and high attendance (120 students). How does revenue scale with attendance, and what is the implication for event production investment?
- Revenue stream diversification: beyond merchandise and tickets, what other revenue streams could the conglomerate activate at future events — food partnerships, sponsor activations, charity auctions, streaming super-chats? Management students identify two realistic additions for the second match and present a one-paragraph implementation brief for each.
- Post-event financial close: Store Manager and General Manager conduct the post-event financial close together — reconciling cash collected against inventory sold, identifying any discrepancies, and updating the budget actuals. This is a real accounting process that management students document and sign off on.
