## Prompt template

```
Marketing Plan & Strategy (S8-U1)

Students can explain marketing plan components, describe research's importance in plan development, describe how to analyze ROI, and identify SWOT analysis components.

What you will make: Three deliverables: (1) Conglomerate Marketing Plan — full document assembled from prior stage deliverables, organized to the standard seven-component structure. Minimum 12 pages. Submitted to administration as the program's primary accountability document. (2) SWOT analysis — completed for the conglomerate using evidence from prior stages, with a one-paragraph strategy implication for each quadrant intersection. (3) ROI evaluation — a two-page document measuring the program's return across financial, audience, and brand dimensions against the objectives set at the start of the year.

Your role: Marketing Advisor rotation students lead the marketing plan assembly — they have the broadest view of all four rotation tracks and can integrate inputs from across divisions. All 8175 students contribute their section based on rotation history. Each student writes a one-paragraph reflection on how their rotation experience informed a specific section of the plan.

The work: Each division contributes its section of the marketing plan: Creative Studio contributes brand strategy evidence, Wholesale & Sourcing contributes supply chain context, Production contributes capacity and cost data, Store Manager contributes sales actuals, Media & PR contributes digital performance data, Events Division contributes event execution data, Esports Division contributes league performance data. The General Manager assembles and edits the full document. The final plan is presented to the class and submitted to administration.

Go deeper:

- Marketing plan components in depth (Task 102): students evaluate the conglomerate's marketing plan against a professional example from a comparable sports or esports organization — what sections are stronger, what sections need development, and what would make the plan more compelling to an external reader?
- ROI analysis methods (Task 104): students calculate three ROI metrics — financial ROI (event revenue vs. event cost), engagement ROI (social followers gained per dollar of digital investment), and attendance ROI (cost per spectator at the first match). They compare these against industry benchmarks and identify the highest-leverage investment for year two.
- SWOT to strategy (Task 105): students identify the single most important strategic implication from each SWOT quadrant and write a one-sentence strategic recommendation for year two of the program based on each.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Explain the components of the marketing plan.
- Describe the importance of research in developing a marketing plan.
- Describe the importance of analyzing the marketing plan to determine ROI of the spend.
- Identify the components of a SWOT analysis.

Use these terms accurately: Marketing plan, Situation analysis, SWOT analysis, Return on investment (ROI), Marketing objective, Executive summary, Implementation timeline.

Write your response under “Your response” below.
```
