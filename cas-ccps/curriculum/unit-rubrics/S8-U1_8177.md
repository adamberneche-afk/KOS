Marketing Plan & Strategy (S8-U1)

Students can develop a marketing plan for an event — applying the full planning framework to a real upcoming event.

What you will make: Three deliverables: (1) Conglomerate Marketing Plan — full document assembled from prior stage deliverables, organized to the standard seven-component structure. Minimum 12 pages. Submitted to administration as the program's primary accountability document. (2) SWOT analysis — completed for the conglomerate using evidence from prior stages, with a one-paragraph strategy implication for each quadrant intersection. (3) ROI evaluation — a two-page document measuring the program's return across financial, audience, and brand dimensions against the objectives set at the start of the year.

Your role: General Manager owns the final marketing plan document — editing for consistency, filling gaps, and presenting to administration. All 8177 management students author the section of the plan corresponding to their specialization. The Events Manager produces the 8177 event marketing plan (Task 97) for the second home match as a standalone deliverable within the larger plan.

The work: Each division contributes its section of the marketing plan: Creative Studio contributes brand strategy evidence, Wholesale & Sourcing contributes supply chain context, Production contributes capacity and cost data, Store Manager contributes sales actuals, Media & PR contributes digital performance data, Events Division contributes event execution data, Esports Division contributes league performance data. The General Manager assembles and edits the full document. The final plan is presented to the class and submitted to administration.

Go deeper:

- Event marketing plan (Task 97): the second home match marketing plan is a standalone document — not a section of the conglomerate plan, but a complete event-specific plan with its own situation analysis, objectives, strategies, budget, and success metrics. It is operational: the Events Manager uses it to run the event.
- Plan presentation to administration: the General Manager leads a 15-minute presentation of the marketing plan to the administrator. Management students support with division-specific sections. This is a formal business presentation — not a classroom report. Students dress professionally and use the marketing plan as the primary exhibit.
- Year-two planning: using the SWOT analysis and ROI evaluation, management students draft a one-page year-two strategic priorities memo — three things the program should do differently next year and why, supported by evidence from year one's data.

Your work must show evidence of each of these:

- Develop a marketing plan for an event.

Use these terms accurately: Marketing plan, Situation analysis, SWOT analysis, Return on investment (ROI), Marketing objective, Executive summary, Implementation timeline.

Write your response in the response zone below.
