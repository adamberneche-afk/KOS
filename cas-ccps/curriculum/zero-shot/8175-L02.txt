Something I do because I am inherently motivated is playing basketball at the park near my house, even when there is no game, coach, or team practice. I enjoy it because I love the feeling of improving a little bit every time, like finally making a move I have practiced for weeks, and it helps me clear my head after a stressful day at school. Nobody pays me or pushes me to go out there, and I keep going because the challenge of getting better and the fun of playing with friends is the reward itself.
