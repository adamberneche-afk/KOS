My Soft Skills Plan

The videos surprised me by showing that employers value patience and face-to-face communication as much as technical skills. This changed my career plans because I now see that event and sports jobs depend on people skills. Before starting a job, I will practice face-to-face communication by ordering food and asking questions in person instead of texting. I will build patience by volunteering at a youth camp, and resilience by asking my coach for honest feedback. For interviews, I will do mock interviews with a family member, practicing eye contact and a firm handshake. I will track my progress weekly, even when nobody checks.
