SWOT Analysis: The Washington Commanders

1. Internal Factors (Inside the Building)

Strengths
1. Young franchise quarterback and renewed excitement: After drafting quarterback Jayden Daniels, who won Offensive Rookie of the Year and led the team to the NFC Championship Game in the 2024 season, the Commanders have a young star to build their marketing around, and fan excitement is the highest it has been in decades.
2. New ownership and history: The new ownership group led by Josh Harris has improved the team's image after years of controversy, and the franchise still has a proud history with three Super Bowl titles and a large, loyal fan base in the Washington, D.C. area.

Weaknesses
1. Outdated stadium: Northwest Stadium (formerly FedExField) is one of the oldest and least popular stadiums in the NFL, with complaints about parking, traffic, and fan amenities, which hurts the game day experience.
2. Damaged brand reputation: Years of losing seasons, the name change, and off-field scandals under the previous owner caused many fans to stop attending, and the "Commanders" name has not yet built the emotional connection the old brand had.

2. External Factors (Outside the World)

Opportunities
1. New stadium in D.C.: The plan to build a new stadium at the old RFK Stadium site would bring the team back into the city, create a modern fan experience, and open up new revenue from premium seating, events, and concerts.
2. Growing reach through streaming and international games: The NFL is expanding into international markets and streaming platforms, giving the Commanders a chance to grow their fan base nationally and globally, especially with an exciting young quarterback.

Threats
1. Competition for fans' money and attention: The Commanders compete with the Baltimore Ravens, who are just an hour away and have been more successful recently, as well as other D.C. teams like the Capitals, Wizards, and Nationals. If the Commanders stop winning, fans in the region may spend their money on the Ravens or other entertainment instead.
2. Rising costs and economic uncertainty: Inflation is making tickets, parking, and concessions more expensive for families, and the costs and political debates around building a new stadium could create public backlash.

3. The Strategy

My recommendation to the General Manager is to launch a "New Era" fan rewards campaign built around Jayden Daniels and the future RFK stadium. Season ticket holders who renew now would get priority access to seats in the new stadium, and the team would add affordable family ticket packages with discounted concessions at the current stadium. This recommendation uses the team's strength (exciting young star and renewed excitement) and the opportunity (new stadium) to fix the weakness of an outdated game day experience and to protect against the threat of the Ravens and rising costs pulling fans away.
