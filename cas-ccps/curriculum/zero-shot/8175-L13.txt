CHHS Rebrand: The Pitch and Focus Group Deck

Slide 1: Title and Hook
On-slide text:
ONE CLOVER HILL
"Every Cavalier Counts"
Visual: Full-screen photo of the student section at a night football game, overlaid with the new streamlined Cavalier shield logo in the school colors.

Slide 2: The Target Persona
On-slide text:
Meet "Cam the Cavalier"
Ages 14-18 | Grades 9-12
Plays a sport, joins a club, or creates
Lives on TikTok and Instagram
Wants to belong and stand out
Visual: A collage of four photos: an athlete, a theater student, a robotics team member, and a student studying in the library, arranged around a simple icon of a student persona named Cam.

Slide 3: The Gap Analysis
On-slide text:
Current Reality | Desired Future
Outdated, mixed logos | One modern, consistent logo
Brand = football only | Brand = every student
Little online presence | Daily student-run social media
"What is a Cavalier?" | Clear values: Honor, Pride, Respect
Visual: Split screen. The left side is gray with old logo versions and an empty bleacher photo. The right side is bright, in school colors, with the new logo and a crowded, diverse student section.

Slide 4: The Vision
On-slide text:
Built for 2025 and Beyond
Social-first branding
Student voices front and center
Wellness and belonging
Visual: Three icons in a row: a phone with a TikTok-style video, a microphone for student spotlights, and a heart for a "Cavaliers Look Out for Each Other" mental health campaign. A mockup of an Instagram grid in the new brand style sits below.

Slide 5: Focus Group Questions
On-slide text:
1. When you hear the word "Cavalier," what is the first word or image that comes to mind, and why?
2. Looking at these two logos, which one would you be more likely to wear on a hoodie, and what makes you choose it?
3. Think about a time you felt proud to be at Clover Hill. What was happening, and how could the school's brand capture that feeling?
Visual: Photo of students sitting in a circle talking, with speech bubble graphics.

Slide 6: The Rollout
On-slide text:
Inside the School | Outside the School
Pep rally logo reveal | Social media launch video
Staff preview and brand guide | Family newsletter and email
Free spirit-wear giveaway | Local news and business partners
Student ambassadors | New signs at home games
Visual: A timeline graphic with three phases: Week 1 staff preview, Week 2 student pep rally reveal, Week 3 community launch at the homecoming game.

Slide 7: Thank You and Discussion
On-slide text:
Your School. Your Brand. Your Voice.
Let's talk!
Visual: Large new logo centered over a faded photo of the school's front entrance.

Speaker Notes Summary
The deck uses more visuals than text so the focus group reacts to images and ideas instead of reading. The focus group questions are open-ended so students share their real opinions instead of being led to a "right" answer. The rollout slide shows internal public relations (students and staff hearing it first so they feel ownership) and external public relations (families and the community seeing a polished, unified launch).
