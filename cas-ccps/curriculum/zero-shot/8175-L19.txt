The Album Drop: Product and Promotion Plan
Band: Static Bloom    Album: Neon Hours

SLIDE 1: TITLE
Static Bloom presents NEON HOURS
A two-month launch plan for our debut album
Release Date: Friday, April 17

SLIDE 2: THE PRODUCT (DAY 1 CHECKPOINT)
The Sleeve: A deep navy background with a hand-painted neon pink and electric blue city skyline. "STATIC BLOOM" runs across the top in bold silver paint pen and "NEON HOURS" sits across the bottom in glowing pink block letters so both names are readable from across a room.
The Vinyl: We painted the center label to match the skyline, added a thin ring of glow-in-the-dark paint around the outer edge, and hot-glued a small holographic star sticker to the label.
Perceived Value: Each change makes the record feel like a limited collector's item. The glow ring and hand-painted label mean no two copies look exactly the same, which justifies a higher price ($32 instead of $25) and makes fans want to display it, not just play it.
Safety: We cut only on the cutting mat with the blade pointed away, used the hot glue gun on its stand with a low-temp setting, painted on newspaper in a ventilated area, and cleaned and cleared our station at the end of each period.

SLIDE 4: BRANDING MESSAGE
"Neon Hours: songs for the nights you don't want to end."
Our brand is late-night, nostalgic and a little dreamy. Every post, photo and ad uses the same navy, neon pink and electric blue colors so fans instantly recognize Static Bloom content in their feed.

SLIDE 5: THE HYPE TIMELINE (2 MONTHS, 7 ACTIVITIES PER WEEK)
Month 1: Before Release (Build Interest)
Week 1: Teaser post, cryptic skyline image, countdown story, behind-the-scenes studio clip, Spotify pre-save link, band Q&A on Instagram Live (Fan Interaction), email newsletter launch
Week 2: Single #1 release, lyric video, TikTok sound challenge, playlist pitching, local radio interview (Interview), fan duet reposts, merch preview
Week 3: Album art reveal, vinyl pre-order opens, influencer unboxing, poster drop around town, podcast guest spot, fan art contest, story polls
Week 4: Single #2, acoustic video, record store listening party (Public Appearance), countdown posts daily, tracklist reveal, press release, release-eve livestream
Month 2: After Release (Keep It Selling)
Week 5: Release day post, signing at a local record store, Reddit AMA, track-by-track breakdown videos, thank-you post, playlist push, fan reaction reposts
Week 6: Music video premiere, college radio interview, TikTok trend using track 3, limited glow vinyl restock, giveaway, merch bundle, newsletter
Week 7: Pop-up acoustic set at a coffee shop, fan cover contest, live stream jam, BTS documentary clip, review reposts, Spotify Canvas update, stories
Week 8: Release show, deluxe track announcement, fan shout-out video, final vinyl push, "one month of Neon Hours" recap, survey, thank-you email
Required events: Public Appearance (listening party, Week 4), Fan Interaction (Instagram Live Q&A, Week 1), Interview (local radio, Week 2).

SLIDE 9: RETURN ON INVESTMENT
Estimated Costs:
Social media ads: $1,200
Vinyl pressing (300 copies): $1,800
Paint and materials: $150
Posters and flyers: $200
Listening party and release show: $400
Music video: $750
Total: $4,500
Estimated Revenue:
300 vinyl x $32 = $9,600
Merch bundles: $1,500
Streams (50,000 x about $0.004) = $200
Total: about $11,300
ROI: ($11,300 - $4,500) / $4,500 = about 151%
How we will know it paid off: We will track cost per result. If the $1,200 in ads brings 2,000 followers, that is $0.60 per follower. If every dollar spent returns about $2.50 in sales, the plan paid off.

SLIDE 10: WHY IT WORKS
Our plan mixes social media, live events and earned media so fans see us online, meet us in person and hear us interviewed. Promotion builds hype the month before release and keeps the album selling the month after, and every result is measurable.
