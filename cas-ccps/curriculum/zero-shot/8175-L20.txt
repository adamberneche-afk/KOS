My Cause My Cleats: Dak Prescott and Mental Health Awareness

The athlete I chose is Dak Prescott, quarterback for the Dallas Cowboys. For the NFL's My Cause My Cleats campaign, Prescott has represented his own non profit, the Faith Fight Finish Foundation, which focuses on cancer research and mental health awareness and suicide prevention.

The Why: Prescott's connection to this cause is deeply personal. His mother passed away from colon cancer in 2013 while he was playing in college, and in 2020 his older brother died by suicide. Prescott later spoke openly about his own struggles with anxiety and depression during the COVID-19 pandemic. Instead of keeping those experiences private, he chose to use his platform to encourage people, especially men and athletes, to talk about mental health and seek help. His cleats featured his foundation's colors and messaging to bring attention to these issues.

The Impact: From a marketing perspective, this partnership is a strong example of co-branding with a cause. For Prescott, it builds his personal brand as more than a football player. It shows him as authentic, vulnerable and a leader, which makes fans and sponsors trust him and connect with him emotionally. That kind of positive public relations can lead to more endorsement opportunities and a stronger reputation that lasts beyond his playing career.

For the non profit, the partnership delivers huge awareness. Millions of fans watch NFL games, and the cleats are shown on television, on social media and in news stories. The cleats are also auctioned through NFL Auction, with the money going to the charity. The NFL benefits too, because the campaign shows the league supporting players and important social causes. Everyone involved gains positive attention, and the people the foundation serves gain resources and a louder voice.
