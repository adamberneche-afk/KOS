My Cause My Cleats: The Campaign Pitch
Athlete: Justin Jefferson, Minnesota Vikings
Non Profit: Boys and Girls Clubs of America

SLIDE 1: TITLE
"Step Up for the Future"
A My Cause My Cleats campaign pitch featuring Justin Jefferson and Boys and Girls Clubs of America

SLIDE 2: THE PARTNERS
The Athlete: Justin Jefferson, star wide receiver for the Minnesota Vikings, known for his "Griddy" celebration and huge social media following with young fans.
The Non Profit: Boys and Girls Clubs of America, which gives kids safe places to learn, play and grow after school.
The Why: Jefferson grew up in a small town in Louisiana where he relied on community programs, coaches and mentors to stay on track. He wants every kid to have a safe place to go and an adult who believes in them. His fun personality and popularity with kids make him a natural voice for youth programs.
What each side gains: Jefferson strengthens his image as a role model, which adds value to his personal brand and future endorsements. The Boys and Girls Clubs gain national TV exposure, new donors and a celebrity voice that kids actually listen to.

SLIDE 3: THE STRATEGY – AWARENESS
How the cleats will get attention:
Reveal video on Jefferson's Instagram and TikTok showing him visiting a local club and kids helping pick the design
"Griddy for Good" TikTok challenge where fans post their Griddy and tag the campaign
Close-up shots on the Sunday Night Football broadcast and NFL social accounts
Vikings stadium video board feature and in-stadium announcement on game day
Local Minneapolis news story and podcast interviews
Channels: TikTok, Instagram, X, NFL Network, team website and the Boys and Girls Clubs newsletter

SLIDE 4: THE STRATEGY – FUNDRAISING AND PROMOTION
Fundraising Idea: "Griddy for Good." For every fan video posted with the campaign hashtag during the week of the game, a corporate partner donates $5 to Boys and Girls Clubs of America. The game-worn cleats are then auctioned on NFL Auction, with 100% of the money going to the clubs.
Sales Promotion: Limited-Edition Item. We will sell 2,000 numbered replica cleat keychains and youth T-shirts, available only during December.
Advantage: Limited quantity creates urgency and makes fans buy quickly, raising money fast.
Disadvantage: Once they sell out, fans who missed out may be disappointed, and the campaign loses a source of ongoing revenue.

SLIDE 5: THE VISUALS – THE CLEATS
Cleat Design Description: The base is Vikings purple fading into a bright royal blue, the main Boys and Girls Clubs color. The left cleat shows hand-drawn kids' artwork collected from a Minneapolis club, including a house, a basketball hoop and a sun, representing a safe place to grow. The right cleat has the phrase "GREAT FUTURES START HERE" in gold block letters along the side, Jefferson's number 18 on the heel and the Boys and Girls Clubs logo on the tongue. Gold laces tie it together with the Vikings color scheme.

SLIDE 6: THE VISUALS – THE MERCH
Item: Youth "Griddy for Good" hoodie
Front: A cartoon of a kid doing the Griddy wearing Jefferson's number 18 jersey
Left sleeve: Boys and Girls Clubs of America logo in blue
Right sleeve: Minnesota Vikings logo in purple and gold
Back: "JJ x BGCA – Great Futures Start Here"
The hoodie represents all three brands at once: the athlete through his number and dance, the non profit through its logo and slogan, and the NFL team through its colors and logo. Part of every sale goes to the clubs.

SLIDE 7: WHY THE NFL SHOULD CHOOSE THIS CAMPAIGN
It connects one of the league's most popular young stars with a cause that serves millions of kids
It uses social media where younger fans already are
It raises money in multiple ways: auction, donations per video and limited merch
It shows the NFL investing in the next generation of fans and communities
