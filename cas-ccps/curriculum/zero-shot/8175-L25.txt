Valentine's Day Card for Madam C.J. Walker

CARD DESIGN
Front cover: A deep red background with gold hearts scattered around the border. In the center is an illustrated portrait of Madam C.J. Walker in an oval gold frame, surrounded by pink roses. Elegant gold script across the top reads: "To the Queen of Self-Made Success."

Inside left panel: A soft pink background with a small timeline drawn like a ribbon: 1867 Born in Louisiana, 1905 Starts her hair care business, 1910 Opens her factory in Indianapolis, 1919 Leaves a legacy of empowerment.

Inside right panel (the verse):

Back of card: "Thank you for proving that hard work, vision and helping others can change an industry." A small gold heart logo with the words "Made with love for Black History Month."

THE THREE FACTS IN THE CARD
1. Madam C.J. Walker was born Sarah Breedlove on December 23, 1867, on a plantation in Delta, Louisiana. She was the first child in her family born free after the end of slavery.
2. She founded the Madam C.J. Walker Manufacturing Company, which made hair care and beauty products specifically for Black women, and built a factory and headquarters in Indianapolis.
3. She is widely recognized, including by the Guinness Book of World Records, as one of the first female self-made millionaires in America. She also trained thousands of Black women as sales agents, called "Walker Agents," giving them a path to financial independence.

WHY I CHOSE HER
Madam C.J. Walker started with almost nothing, working as a washerwoman for about a dollar a day, and turned an idea into a nationwide business. She used her success to support Black communities and education and to give other women jobs. Her story shows that entrepreneurship is not only about making money but about lifting other people up too.
