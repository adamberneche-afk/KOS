Elevator Pitch Preparation: The 30 Second Commercial

2. AP English Language: It taught me to write persuasively and support my ideas with evidence.

Top Strengths: Specific Skills
1. Communication: I am comfortable speaking in front of groups and explaining ideas clearly.
2. Social Media and Design: I can create graphics in Canva and edit short videos for Instagram and TikTok.
3. Organization and Time Management: I balance school, a part-time job and a varsity sport while keeping my grades up.

Achievements: Awards, Certifications, Personal Bests
1. Honor Roll for six straight quarters
2. Microsoft Office Specialist certification in Excel
3. Placed second at the DECA district competition in Sports and Entertainment Marketing
4. Personal best: ran a 5:12 mile during cross country season

1. Helped plan our school's "Pink Out" football game, which raised over $1,200 for breast cancer research.

Target Organizations: Companies That Interest Me
1. Professional sports teams and leagues, such as the NFL, NBA or a minor league baseball team
2. Sports apparel brands like Nike, Under Armour or Adidas
3. Event and entertainment companies like Live Nation that run concerts and festivals

1. My grandmother, who started her own catering business and taught me that hard work and kindness go together.

Interesting Facts: What Sets Me Apart
1. I run a small sneaker resale business and have learned pricing, customer service and marketing firsthand.
2. I am bilingual in English and Spanish.
3. I have volunteered as a youth soccer referee for three seasons, so I know how to stay calm under pressure and handle disagreements.

Sample 30 Second Pitch
"Hi, I'm Jordan, a junior at Clover Hill High School. I'm passionate about the business side of sports. I've placed second at DECA districts in Sports and Entertainment Marketing, helped plan a Pink Out game that raised over $1,200 and run my own sneaker resale business. I'm bilingual and certified in Excel, and I'm interested in marketing roles with pro sports teams or brands like Nike. I'd love to learn more about how your organization connects with fans."
