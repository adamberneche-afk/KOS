Bias Check
I rewrote Question 3. It originally said, "Wouldn't a study lounge with free snacks and Wi-Fi be a great way to spend your afternoon?" which suggested the right answer was "yes" and assumed students wanted those features. I changed it to an open, neutral question that lets students describe what they actually want without being led.

How these help us design the service: Question 1 tells us how much demand there is and how to staff the lounge, Question 2 tells us which features to prioritize, and Question 3 may reveal needs we have not thought of, like tutoring or a printer.
