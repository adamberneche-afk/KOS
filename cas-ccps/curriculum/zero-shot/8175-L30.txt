Market Research Survey: Clover Hill Athletic Events

Our Market Segments
Segment A: Student Superfans – students who attend most home games and sit in the student section
Segment B: Families and Parents – parents and relatives of athletes and younger siblings
Segment C: Casual Students – students who rarely attend but might come to big games or theme nights
Segment D: Community Members – alumni and local residents with no student currently at the school

Which Questions Check Each Segment

Segment A – Student Superfans
Exists: Q1 (current student), Q2 (6 or more games), Q4 (attend with friends), Q9 (school spirit or hanging out)
How to reach: Q7 and Q8 (likely Instagram and TikTok)

Segment B – Families and Parents
Exists: Q1 (parent or family), Q5 (family member plays), Q9 (support a family member)
How to reach: Q7 (school email newsletter or Facebook group)

Segment C – Casual Students
Exists: Q1 (student), Q2 (0-2 games), Q6 (likely to attend theme nights), Q11
How to reach: Q8 (most-used platform) and Q12

Segment D – Community Members
Exists: Q1 (alumni or community member), Q3 (which sports they follow), Q9 (love of the sport)
How to reach: Q7 (local newspaper or community Facebook group)

Why these questions matter: The "exists" questions tell us how big each segment really is, so we know if it is worth targeting. The "reach" questions tell us exactly which channels to use for each group, so we do not waste money advertising to students in the newspaper or to grandparents on TikTok. Question 10 also helps us set prices that each segment will accept.
