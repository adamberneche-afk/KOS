PANEL TWO: MEMORABLE PROJECT – THE ALBUM DROP
Our most memorable project was The Album Drop. Each group created a fictional band, then hand-altered a real vinyl record and sleeve with paint, cutouts and hot glue to add "perceived value." We mapped out a two-month hype timeline with seven promotions a week, including a public appearance, a fan interaction and an interview, and then pitched our campaign with a customer profile, ad mockup and ROI estimate. It was hands-on, creative and showed us how much work goes into making people care about a product.
Image: A photo-style graphic of a painted vinyl record with a glowing neon cover.

PANEL THREE: CAREERS THIS CLASS PREPARES YOU FOR
1. Sports Marketing Coordinator: Plans promotions, sponsorships and game-day events for pro and college teams.
2. Event Planner / Event Manager: Organizes concerts, festivals, tournaments and live entertainment from budget to show day.
3. Social Media and Digital Content Manager: Creates posts, videos and campaigns to grow fan engagement for teams, artists and brands.
Bonus: Public Relations Specialist, Brand Partnership Manager, Athletic Director

PANEL FOUR: THE TEACHER REVIEW
Pros:
- Real-world, project-based learning makes the content memorable and connects marketing to things students actually care about.
- Mr. Berneche brings real examples from the NFL, the Olympics and the music industry, which keeps class engaging.
- Students get creative freedom to choose their own athletes, brands and industries.
- Clear daily checkpoints keep big projects manageable.
Cons:
- Some projects moved quickly, and a few groups would benefit from an extra work day before presentations.
- Group projects sometimes put more work on certain members; individual check-ins or peer evaluations could help balance this.
- More example models of finished work at the start of each project would help students understand expectations sooner.
Overall: An engaging, hands-on approach that prepares students for real careers. With a little more pacing flexibility, it would be even stronger.
