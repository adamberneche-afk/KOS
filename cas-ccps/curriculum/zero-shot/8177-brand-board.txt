The Brand Board: Riverlight Nights

Part 1: The Idea

Riverlight Nights is a summer music festival held over three evenings on the banks of the James River in Richmond. It features local and regional indie, soul and folk bands, food trucks and a lantern walk along the river at sunset. It is for high school and college students, young professionals and families who want an affordable, relaxed night out with live music under the open sky.

Part 2: Three Words

Glowing: I want the festival to feel warm and magical, like the light of the lanterns on the water at dusk. This is the moment people will remember and post about.

Relaxed: Riverlight Nights is not a crowded, stressful mega-festival. I want people to feel like they can spread out a blanket, slow down and enjoy the evening.

Connected: the festival is about community, with local bands, local food and neighbors sharing the riverbank. I want customers to feel like they belong to something bigger than one concert.

Part 3: The Board

My brand board (built in Canva) is laid out on one page with the logo at the top, the three words across the middle, the color palette and font on the left, and four photos on the right.

Color palette (5 colors):
1. Lantern Gold (#F2B53A) – main color
2. Dusk Navy (#1F2A44) – main color
3. Sunset Coral (#F27D52) – supporting color
4. River Teal (#2E8B8B) – supporting color
5. Cream White (#FAF6EC) – neutral for text backgrounds

Font: Quicksand Bold for headlines, a rounded, friendly sans-serif font.

Logo versions:
- Full logo (for banners, signs and staff shirts): the words RIVERLIGHT NIGHTS in Quicksand Bold over a curved line of three glowing lanterns floating above a wavy river line, in gold on navy.
- Simple logo (for the app icon and digital tickets): a single gold lantern inside a navy circle with one wavy line beneath it. It stays clear even at the size of a phone icon.

Three words on the board: Glowing. Relaxed. Connected.

Four images:
1. A crowd sitting on blankets on the grassy riverbank while a band plays on a stage lit with gold string lights.
2. Hundreds of paper lanterns floating on the James River at sunset.
3. A group of friends laughing in line at a food truck with coral and teal umbrellas.
4. A parent and child dancing in front of the stage, both wearing glow bracelets.

Part 4: Explain It

Colors: my color palette connects directly to my three words. Lantern Gold and Sunset Coral are warm colors that feel glowing, like candlelight and the sky at dusk. Dusk Navy and River Teal are calm, cool colors that make the brand feel relaxed and tie it to the river. Cream White is the neutral that keeps text easy to read on posters and tickets.

Typography: Quicksand Bold has thin, rounded edges that feel friendly and modern instead of loud or aggressive. That matches the relaxed and connected feel I want. Blocky fonts would make it feel like a rock arena, which is not my brand.

Images: each photo shows a part of the brand identity. The lanterns and string lights show glowing. The blankets on the grass show relaxed. The friends at the food truck and the family dancing show connected, because they show people sharing the experience together.

Inside the venue (atmospherics): the brand comes off the screen and onto the riverbank. Gold string lights and paper lanterns hang over the walkways and the stage, so the whole space glows after sunset. Stage lighting shifts from coral at sunset to navy and teal after dark. Entrance arches and directional signs are painted Dusk Navy with the full logo in gold, and the simple lantern logo appears on the wristbands, trash and recycling bins, and the screens beside the stage between sets. Soft acoustic music plays between bands to keep the mood relaxed.

The big picture: because every digital ticket, wristband, social post, staff shirt and sign uses the same five colors, the same font and the same lantern logo, customers will recognize Riverlight Nights instantly and trust that it will deliver the same warm, relaxed experience every summer. Social posts would use the lantern photos with the gold-and-navy logo and the hashtag #RiverlightNights to build excitement before each weekend.
