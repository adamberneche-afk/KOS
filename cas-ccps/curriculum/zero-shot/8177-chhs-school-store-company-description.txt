Part 1: Plan It

What does the store sell, and who are its customers?
The Cavalier Shop sells Clover Hill spirit wear, hats, rally towels, stickers and accessories that students design and make in class. Its customers are CHHS students, parents and families, teachers and staff, and fans at home games.

Two reasons a student or parent would buy a Clover Hill shirt here instead of online:
1. It's right down the hall, so a student can get a shirt the same day for a spirit day or a game tonight instead of waiting days for shipping.
2. The money goes back into student activities, so the purchase supports the school.

The store's unique selling proposition (USP):
It is the official home of the Cavalier brand, and its gear is designed and made by students right here in the classroom, so it can't be bought anywhere else.

Part 2: Write It

The Cavalier Shop is the official school-based enterprise of Clover Hill High School, a real retail store operated by students as part of their business and marketing coursework. Its target market includes CHHS students, parents, faculty and staff, and community members who attend Cavalier athletic and school events. The Cavalier Shop is the only store that offers official Cavalier gear designed and produced by students on the store's own sublimation printer, heat press and large-format printer, so its products cannot be purchased anywhere else. Customers choose the Cavalier Shop over Amazon, Walmart and mall retailers because it is located inside the school and is open on spirit days and game days, allowing them to purchase a shirt or hat the same day they need it. Each purchase strengthens the Cavalier brand and helps students show their school pride at every pep rally, spirit week and home game. When customers shop at the Cavalier Shop, they are supporting student activities and giving student designers, makers and employees real experience running a business.
