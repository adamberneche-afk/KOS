The Buyer's Product Pitch: The Cavalier Cinch Bag

Part 1: The Product
SKU: CS-BAG-CINCH-WHT
The Cavalier Cinch Bag is a white polyester drawstring backpack (about 14 by 18 inches) with a full-color sublimated design: the Cavalier logo on the front, "CLOVER HILL" across the top, and a black-and-gold background. It's different from what the store already sells because it isn't clothing or a small accessory. It's something students can use every day for gym clothes, practice gear or game-day snacks, and the design can be customized for each sport (football, soccer, volleyball) using the same blank.

Part 2: The Why
The bag is for CHHS students, especially athletes and band members who carry gear to practice, plus parents who want a cheap gift. Students will want it at the start of each sports season, during spirit week, and around the holidays as a gift. It also works as a team order: a coach could order 30 for the whole team.

Part 3: The Materials
Supplier: Coastal Business Supplies, white polyester sublimation drawstring bag (https://www.coastalbusiness.com)
- Blank bag: $2.75 each
- Minimum order: 50 bags (we will order 100)
- Shipping: $15.00 for the order
- Sublimation ink and transfer paper: about $0.45 per bag (one 13 x 19 sheet per bag)
- Packaging (paper hang tag with price and SKU): $0.10 per bag

Cost per finished item = $2.75 + $0.45 + $0.10 = $3.30

Production: each bag takes about 1 minute to print and 60 seconds on the heat press, plus loading and cooling. We can make about 20 bags in one class period.

Part 4: The Price
Retail price: $11.99
Strategy: charm pricing. $11.99 feels like "about $11" instead of $12. It's priced below a hoodie or shirt but more than a sticker, so it's an easy add-on. Similar drawstring bags at sporting goods stores cost $12 to $18 without a school logo, so ours is a good deal. It covers our cost and leaves a big profit while staying under what students would pay at a mall store.

Part 5: The Math
Cost per finished item: $2.75 + $0.45 + $0.10 = $3.30
Profit per item: $11.99 − $3.30 = $8.69
Margin: $8.69 ÷ $11.99 = 0.725, about 72.5%
Total batch cost: ($3.30 × 100) + $15.00 = $345.00
Break-even point: $345.00 ÷ $8.69 = 39.7, so 40 bags. After the 40th bag sells, the rest is profit.
Expected profit if the whole batch sells: ($11.99 × 100) − $345.00 = $1,199.00 − $345.00 = $854.00
ROI: $854.00 ÷ $345.00 = 2.475, about 248%
Production time: 100 bags ÷ 20 per class period = 5 class periods
Turnover: if we sell about 15 bags a week during the fall sports season, the batch sells out in about 7 weeks.

The design uses only school logos and contains no inappropriate content, so it follows the CHHS Student Handbook. Sublimation works on white polyester, so the blank fits our equipment.
