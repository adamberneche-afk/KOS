The Cavalier Shop Flyer

Part 1: Design It
Built in Canva, 8.5 x 11 inches, black and white only. PDF link: (the flyer link)

Flyer text (exact words):
Headline: GEAR UP. CAVS.
Subheadline (white text in a black bar): THE CAVALIER SHOP IS OPEN
Items and prices:
- Custom Cavalier Tees ... $15
- Rally Towels ... $8
- Logo Stickers ... $2
Tagline: Made by students. Worn by Cavaliers.
Hours: Open Monday through Friday during all lunch blocks and before home games
Location: Main hallway, next to the cafeteria
Logo: Official Cavalier Shop logo in solid black at the bottom

Layout, top to bottom:
1. Headline "GEAR UP. CAVS." across the top in huge, bold, all-caps black sans-serif font (Anton), taking up about the top quarter of the page.
2. A solid black bar across the page with "THE CAVALIER SHOP IS OPEN" in bold white letters.
3. The three items listed in a bold list with large prices aligned on the right, with plenty of white space between each line.
4. The tagline "Made by students. Worn by Cavaliers." in smaller bold text.
5. Hours and location in a simple box with a thick black outline.
6. The Cavalier Shop logo in solid black, centered at the bottom.
7. A wide white border (about one inch) around the entire flyer so nothing gets cut off by the copier.

Part 2: Explain It
I made the headline the biggest element because people walking down the hall only read for about three seconds, so it has to stop them first. That creates a clear visual hierarchy: first the headline, then the store name, then the items and prices, and finally the details like hours and location. I used contrast by putting white text inside a solid black bar and using thick black text on a white background everywhere else, with no light grays that would turn muddy on the copier. I left negative space around the item list and around the logo on purpose so each part stands out and the flyer doesn't look crowded. I kept the copy short and used only two bold fonts so it's easy to read from a distance. I also checked the logo in grayscale in Canva and switched it to solid black because the original gold parts turned into a gray blob.

Part 3: The 10-Foot Test
I printed a test copy and taped it to the wall, then stepped back about 10 feet. From that distance I could clearly read the headline "GEAR UP. CAVS." and the black bar with the store name. The item names were readable, but the hours were too small. After testing, I made the headline even bigger, increased the prices to a bolder font size, and removed one extra line of copy so there was more negative space. I also made the hours box text larger so it's easy to read from about 2 feet away.

How it brings customers in: The headline speaks directly to students' school pride, and showing low prices like $2 stickers and $8 rally towels tells students they can stop in and buy something on the way to lunch.
