Emergency Action Plan: Lightning Delay at the Clover Hill Rivalry Game

Part 1: The Plan

Lightning has been spotted nearby, so staying on the metal bleachers is not an option. Metal bleachers and an open field are some of the most dangerous places to be in a thunderstorm, and there is no safe place outdoors. Our emergency action plan is a combination of evacuation and shelter in place: we will move everyone off the bleachers and into sturdy buildings or hard-topped cars.

Where fans go:
- Visitor bleachers (1,000 fans) and the north half of the home bleachers (about 500 fans) go through the north emergency gate to the school gym, about 300 yards away. That fills the gym's capacity of about 1,500.
- The south half of the home bleachers (about 1,500 fans) exits through the main gate to the parking lots and shelters in their own hard-topped cars. Fans without a car near the lot are directed by staff to the school building's main entrance, where the cafeteria and hallways serve as overflow shelter.
- Players, coaches, the band and cheerleaders go to the locker rooms and the school building.

Why: the gym can only hold about 1,500 people, so we can't send all 3,000 there. Splitting the crowd between the gym, the school building and cars gets everyone into a safe structure quickly, and it uses both gates so neither one becomes a bottleneck. Nobody returns to the bleachers until 30 minutes have passed since the last lightning or thunder.

Part 2: The Map

Map description (drawn in Google Drawings):
- The field is a rectangle in the center, with the track around it.
- Home bleachers are on the right (east) side, with Stairway H1 at the north end and Stairway H2 at the south end.
- Visitor bleachers are on the left (west) side, with one stairway in the middle.
- The main gate is at the bottom (south), with the parking lots below it.
- The emergency gate is at the top (north), with the school gym and school building above it.

Arrows:
- Blue arrows: visitors go down their stairway, follow the west side of the track north, and exit the north emergency gate to the gym.
- Green arrows: home fans in the north sections go down Stairway H1, follow the east track north, and exit the north gate to the gym.
- Red arrows: home fans in the south sections go down Stairway H2, follow the track south and exit the main gate to the parking lots.
- A yellow star marks the overflow entrance to the school building.

Avoiding a bottleneck: if all 3,000 fans tried to leave through the 20-foot main gate, it would become a dangerous bottleneck. By unlocking the north gate first and sending half the crowd that way, the main gate only handles about 1,500 people. Visitors are released first because they have only one stairway, then home fans by section, so egress happens in waves instead of one rush.

Part 3: The Staff

Police officers (6):
- 2 at the main gate to manage egress, direct traffic in the parking lots and keep cars from blocking walkers.
- 2 at the north emergency gate: one unlocks it immediately and both keep the flow moving.
- 2 at the gym entrance to manage crowd control, enforce capacity and handle any fights or safety threats.

Guest services staff (12, yellow shirts):
- 2 at each of the three bleacher stairways (6 total) to guide fans down calmly, one section at a time, and help anyone who is slow-moving.
- 3 along the route to the gym, including at the north gate, with flashlights to point the way.
- 2 at the main gate pointing fans toward the parking lots and the school overflow entrance.
- 1 inside the gym to help people find space and keep the doors clear.
Their job is to stay calm, give clear directions and de-escalate anyone who is upset.

Athletic trainers (2):
- 1 goes to the gym with a first aid kit and AED to treat anyone injured on the way, such as falls on the stairs.
- 1 stays with the players and then moves to the school building overflow area.

Communication: all staff use two-way radios on one channel. The director of security (me) gives the order to begin each wave, and stairway staff radio when their section is clear. If the gym fills up, the gym officer radios immediately and we redirect the remaining fans to the school building.

Part 4: The PA Announcement

"Ladies and gentlemen, because of lightning in the area, the game is delayed and everyone must leave the bleachers now for your safety. Visitor fans and home fans in the north sections, please follow our staff in yellow shirts through the north gate to the school gym; home fans in the south sections, please exit through the main gate to your cars or follow staff to the school building. Please walk calmly, and we'll update you on the restart once it is safe to return."

Part 5: Connect It

Our fire drill is an emergency action plan for the school as a venue. Every classroom has a posted evacuation route, each class uses a specific exit so the hallways and doors don't become bottlenecks, and teachers have assigned roles, like taking attendance at a meeting spot. Just like at the stadium, the plan is written and practiced before anything goes wrong, so during a real fire everyone already knows where to go and egress is fast and orderly.
