Part 3: The Supplier Email

Subject: Receiving Discrepancies on Purchase Order #CS-1042

Dear Blank Canvas Wholesale Customer Service Team,

Thank you for the recent delivery for Purchase Order #CS-1042. During receiving, we counted every item and compared it with our purchase order and your packing slip, and we found the following problems:

1. White polyester hoodies: we ordered 24 and the packing slip lists 24, but only 22 arrived. Please issue a credit of $28.00 for the 2 missing hoodies (2 x $14).

2. White polyester t-shirts: we received all 48, but 12 are black instead of white. Because we use sublimation printing, black shirts cannot be used. Please send 12 white polyester t-shirts as replacements and let us know how you would like us to return the black shirts.

3. Blank polyester rally towels: 3 of the 50 towels arrived torn. Please issue a credit of $4.80 for the 3 damaged towels (3 x $1.60).

4. Sublimation transfer paper: your packing slip shows 2 packs shipped and 2 backordered. Please confirm a ship date for the remaining 2 packs and confirm that we will not be billed the $30.00 for them until they ship.

Total credit requested: $32.80.

We have a production deadline coming up, so we would appreciate the replacement t-shirts and the backordered transfer paper as soon as possible. Please reply to confirm the credit, the replacements and the ship date.

Thank you for your help.

Sincerely,
Cavalier Shop Receiving Team
Clover Hill High School Sports and Entertainment Marketing

How this connects to the supply chain: our channel of distribution goes from the manufacturer to the wholesaler (Blank Canvas Wholesale), to our class, where we print the designs, to the Cavalier Shop as the retailer and finally to the fan. When one link in the supply chain breaks, production stops. Without white shirts we can only print 36 shirts instead of 48, and with only 2 packs of transfer paper (200 sheets) we may run out before finishing all our hoodies, shirts and towels. Careful receiving, comparing the purchase order and packing slip, and good inventory control prevent shrink and protect money that belongs to the school.
