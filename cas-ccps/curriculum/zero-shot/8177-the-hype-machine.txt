Pack the House: Clover Hill Playoff Campaign Storyboard

Campaign theme: "BLACK OUT THE HILL" (everyone wears black to the Friday night playoff game)

Part 1: The Ad (Paid Advertising)
Format: 15-second Instagram Reels and TikTok video ad.
Ad copy: Clips of big plays, the student section and the band, ending with the words: "PLAYOFFS. FRIDAY. 7 PM. BLACK OUT THE HILL. Tickets $5 at the link in bio. Students free with ID before Wednesday."
Audience: CHHS students ages 14 to 18, plus parents and adults ages 25 to 55 who live within 10 miles of Clover Hill High School and follow local high school sports pages.
Where it runs: Sponsored Instagram Stories and Reels, and TikTok in-feed ads, for the 7 days before the game.
Cost: $1,500 ($900 Instagram, $600 TikTok).

Part 2: The PR Stunt (Earned Publicity)
"The Hill Walk": On Wednesday before the game, the football team, cheerleaders and marching band will walk to the neighboring elementary school, where the players will read to classes and run a mini football clinic at recess. Each elementary student gets a free ticket for themselves and one adult, and an invitation to run onto the field with the team before kickoff.
Why it's news: It's a feel-good community story with great visuals (high school athletes with little kids), it connects to a playoff game, and it's something new. NBC12 and the school newspaper would want to cover it because it's about community, not just selling tickets. We will send a press release to NBC12, the Chesterfield Observer and the school paper on Monday. This is public relations: it builds the school's image and earns publicity for free.

Part 3: The Sales Promotion (Incentive)
"Early Bird Blackout": Anyone who buys a ticket online by Wednesday at 11:59 PM gets a free black "Black Out the Hill" rally towel at the gate, and every early ticket is entered in a drawing for four sideline passes and a team-signed football. Students who show their ID at the lunch table by Wednesday get in free.
Deadline: Wednesday at 11:59 PM.
Personal selling: Student-section leaders will also sell tickets at a lunch table Monday through Wednesday, talking one-on-one with classmates.

Part 4: The Budget
Instagram and TikTok ads: $1,500
1,000 black rally towels (early-bird giveaway): $1,800
Drawing prize (signed football and sideline pass lanyards): $150
Posters and banners for school and local businesses: $400
Lunch-table supplies (table banner, card reader fees, signs): $150
Elementary school ticket program (printing and wristbands): $200
Student section "Blackout" face paint and noisemakers: $300
Press release printing and media packets: $50
PA and video board halftime contest prizes: $300
Contingency fund: $150
Total: $5,000

Part 5: Keep It Honest
Every promise in this campaign will hold up on game night. We only promise 1,000 free towels to early buyers, and we will stop advertising the towel once 1,000 tickets are sold, so no one is promised a towel that doesn't exist. The ad says "Tickets $5" because that is the real price, and "students free with ID before Wednesday" only applies to the actual deadline we post. The drawing will be held publicly at halftime, and the winner will be announced over the PA. We won't use photos of players or students without permission, and we won't advertise anything like a celebrity appearance unless it is confirmed. This follows the FTC's truth-in-advertising rules and avoids a bait-and-switch, which protects the school's reputation and makes fans trust our promotions for the next game.

How the promotional mix works together: The paid advertising reaches people on their phones, the public relations stunt earns publicity on the news, the sales promotion pushes people to buy early, and personal selling at the lunch table turns interest into actual ticket sales.
