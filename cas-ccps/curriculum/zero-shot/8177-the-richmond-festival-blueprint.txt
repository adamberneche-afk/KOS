The Richmond Festival Blueprint: "RVA Courtside Fest"

Phase 1: The Vision

Name: RVA Courtside Fest

Theme: A one-day streetball and sneaker culture festival combining a 3-on-3 basketball tournament, a sneaker and streetwear market, live DJs and local food trucks.

The Crowd: High school and college students ages 15 to 24 in the Richmond area who play pickup basketball, collect sneakers and follow streetwear on social media. Our target market is young people who would rather be part of the action than sit in the stands.

The Vibe: Fans can sign up their own 3-on-3 team or watch the best local players compete on outdoor courts while DJs play. Between games they can trade and buy sneakers from local resellers, get custom shoe designs from local artists, enter a dunk contest and eat from Richmond's best food trucks. It's a full-day hangout that feels like a summer block party built around basketball.

Phase 2: The Logistics

Location: Main Street Station and its surrounding plaza in Shockoe Bottom. It has a large open area that can fit eight portable basketball courts, a covered train shed that can hold the sneaker market and serve as rain cover, nearby public parking decks and GRTC bus access, and it's in a commercial area, so music during the day won't break noise rules for neighborhoods.

The Team (5 jobs):
1. Security Guards: check bags at the entrance, manage crowd flow around the courts and handle any conflicts.
2. Audio Technician: sets up and runs the DJ stage sound system and the court announcer microphones.
3. Tournament Coordinator / Referees: run the bracket, schedule games and referee each 3-on-3 game.
4. Vendor Coordinator: assigns food truck and sneaker market spots, checks vendor permits and helps vendors set up.
5. EMT / First Aid Staff: run the first-aid tent and handle sprained ankles, heat exhaustion and other injuries.

Risk Management: The biggest risk is a thunderstorm in the afternoon. We will watch the weather radar all day with a staff member assigned to it. If lightning is within 10 miles, we will pause all outdoor games and move everyone under the covered train shed, which is already our sneaker market. The DJs will keep playing inside, and the tournament will switch to a shorter "first to 11" game format to finish on time. If the storm lasts more than two hours, the finals will be moved to a rain date the following Saturday, and tickets will still be valid. This plan is listed on the ticket page so everyone knows it ahead of time.

Phase 3: The Business

Money In (revenue):
- General admission tickets: $15
- 3-on-3 team registration: $60 per team (128 teams = $7,680)
- Sponsors: a sporting goods brand as the main sponsor and a local credit union as the court sponsor
- Vendor fees: $200 per food truck spot and $100 per sneaker market table
- Festival t-shirt and merchandise sales

Money Out (expenses):
The biggest expense will be renting and setting up the venue, including portable courts, tents, staging and sound equipment. Security and insurance are the next biggest costs, because an outdoor event with athletic competition needs liability insurance and enough security to keep the crowd safe. We'll also pay for DJs, permits from the City of Richmond, portable bathrooms and marketing.

The Pitch (social media post):
"Richmond, lace 'em up! RVA Courtside Fest hits Main Street Station on June 13 with 3-on-3 hoops, a sneaker market, live DJs and the city's best food trucks. Tickets and team spots are on sale now, so grab yours before they're gone! #RVACourtsideFest"

Code of Conduct: The event is alcohol-free and drug-free, open to all ages, and focused on sports, music and art, so it follows the CCPS Student Code of Conduct.
