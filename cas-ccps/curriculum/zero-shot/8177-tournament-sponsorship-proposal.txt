Part 1: Research

Business: Chick-fil-A Chesterfield Towne Center
Decision-maker: Mr. Daniel Brooks, Owner/Operator
Mailing address: Chick-fil-A Chesterfield Towne Center, North Chesterfield, Virginia

Why this business fits: Chick-fil-A's customers include teenagers, families with kids and parents looking for a quick meal, which are exactly the people who will be at our tournament. Most of our players and fans live within a few miles of the restaurant and already eat there after games and practices. Because it is locally owned and operated, the restaurant has a strong reason to connect its name to a community event in its own neighborhood.

Part 2: The Letter

Clover Hill High School
Sports Marketing Program
Midlothian, Virginia

November 20, 2026

Mr. Daniel Brooks
Owner/Operator
Chick-fil-A Chesterfield Towne Center
North Chesterfield, Virginia

Dear Mr. Brooks:

My name is Taylor Reed, and I am a student in the Sports Marketing program at Clover Hill High School. Our class is organizing the Clover Hill Winter Classic, a two-day high school basketball tournament on January 8 and 9, 2027, in the Clover Hill gymnasium. The tournament will feature eight teams from across Chesterfield County and is expected to draw more than 600 students, parents and fans over the two days. As part of our program, students are planning, marketing and running every part of the event.

We believe Chick-fil-A Chesterfield Towne Center is a perfect fit for our audience. Our tournament crowd is made up of local teenagers and families, the same customers who visit your restaurant after school, practices and games. Many of these families live just a few minutes from your location, and a weekend tournament means hungry fans looking for somewhere to eat between games. Sponsoring the Winter Classic would put your restaurant in front of hundreds of potential customers at the exact moment they are deciding where to eat.

As our Gold Sponsor, Chick-fil-A Chesterfield Towne Center would receive its logo printed on the back of every tournament t-shirt worn by all 96 players and staff, a banner displayed in the gymnasium for both days of the event, and a thank-you announcement over the PA system before every game, a total of 14 announcements. Your logo would also appear on the tournament program and on our class's social media posts promoting the event. Your sponsorship would help pay for referees, trophies and tournament t-shirts, and it would give students real experience running a business event.

Thank you for considering this sponsorship opportunity and for your support of young people in our community. Our program advisor will follow up with you by phone next week to answer any questions and discuss the details. We would be proud to have Chick-fil-A as a partner in the Clover Hill Winter Classic.

Sincerely,

(signature)

Taylor Reed
Sports Marketing Student
Clover Hill High School

Part 3: Proofread
I read the letter out loud and checked it for errors. I confirmed the contact's name and title, made sure the greeting uses his last name with a colon, removed any slang, checked that the dates and numbers match the tournament details, and made sure the letter fits on one page with single spacing and a blank line between paragraphs.

Key terms: This letter asks for a sponsorship, not a donation, because the business receives marketing value in return. It matches the business to its target market, states a clear value proposition in the third paragraph, and ends with a call to action.
