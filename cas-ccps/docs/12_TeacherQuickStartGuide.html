Teacher Quick-Start

Your first week
with the dashboard.

Lessons, student documents and enrollment run by themselves each night. This guide shows what to check, what students do, and where to act.

📅 Check lesson dates — 5 minutes 📊 Morning check — 2 minutes 🎓 Competency ratings — weekly
01

Open Your Dashboard

Bookmark the Teacher Dashboard link your admin gave you. You can also open it from your Teacher Manual: ⚙️ Assignment System → 📊 Open My Dashboard.

It opens on 📊 Assignment Dashboard, showing the current lesson for each course. The page doesn't update by itself: press ↻ Refresh to see the latest.

❓
Not sure what a word means? Press ❓ Terms in the header. The same list is at the end of this guide.
💡
Setting up a new teacher's workspace is your admin's job. You don't need to activate anything to use the dashboard.
02

Check Your Lesson Dates

Students get a lesson's document only when that lesson has a start date. Set the dates, and the rest happens overnight.

1
Open 📅 Lessons

Each course has a table: Lesson, Status (Current, Upcoming, Taught or No date), Starts, and Last rollout.

2
Set or change a start date

Type or pick a date in the Starts column, then press Enter or leave the box. The row says Saved ✓, or why it was not saved. A date in the next 2 days, in the past, or on the current lesson asks you to confirm first. A lesson runs from its start date until the next one starts.

3
Let the nightly rollout do the rest

Each night, the rollout enrolls students from the Canvas roster, makes each student's document for lessons starting within 2 days, and shares it with the student the night before the lesson. You don't register students one at a time.

⚠️
Read the Last rollout column. A ⚠ line there means a step failed, for example an old Canvas roster export. Tell your admin. A course marked “rollout in shadow (changes nothing)” is only practising: it makes no documents yet.

Need to change what a lesson says, or add one? Use Edit or + New lesson in 📅 Lessons. That makes a draft, which is reviewed before students see it. See the Teacher Reference Guide.

03

What Students Do

1
Write in their document

Under the grey “── YOUR RESPONSE BEGINS HERE ──” line. The page header has a “Your dashboard” link.

2
Press Submit for Feedback on the Student Dashboard

At least 25 words. The check takes a few minutes.

3
Read the feedback comment

Feedback is a comment on the document (Comments, top right), not text in it. You choose this per course in ⚙ Settings.

4
Turn in on Canvas

They paste the document's link: Start Assignment → Website URL. Passing a check first is a good idea, not a rule. You grade in Canvas.

📄
Hand students the Student Quick Start (STUDENT_QUICK_START.html). It uses the same words as their dashboard.
04

Your Morning Check

1
Look at the summary cards

Students, ✅ Passed, ↩️ Passed before, now revising, ✏️ Checked, not passing yet, ⚪ Not checked yet, and Needs attention. Click a card to show only those students. Click Clear filter to go back.

2
Deal with Needs attention first

These rows say Couldn't be checked ⚠: the check failed or timed out. It isn't the student's fault. You also get an email for a timed-out check, and it says what to do.

3
Open a student's work

Press Open document ↗ on their row. Their feedback is in the document's comments.

07

Words You'll See

The same list as ❓ Terms on your dashboard.

Competencies done
For each course, the competencies you have rated 1 to 3 (limited supervision or better) in Competency ratings, out of every competency in the course (the CompetencyRegistry tab). Only your own confirmed ratings count.
Check
One AI evaluation of a submission against the assignment’s criteria (Flow 2). A student can be checked many times.
Passed / Not passing yet
Whether the latest check met the assignment’s criteria. “Passed before” means an earlier check passed and the latest needs revision.
Waiting to be checked
Submitted and in line for its check.
Being checked now
The check is running.
Couldn’t be checked
The check failed or timed out, not the student’s work. The timeout email says what to do.
AI’s suggested score
On a passing check, the AI suggests 2 (Adequate), 3 (Solid) or 4 (Exceptional). Higher is better. It never suggests 1 or 5.
Competency rating (SCR)
The Virginia CTE Student Competency Record, 1 to 5, where 1 is the highest: 1 Can teach others, 2 Without supervision, 3 Limited supervision, 4 With supervision, 5 Cannot perform. The AI suggests 2 to 4 once there are 3 pieces of evidence; 1 and 5 are yours alone.
Competency tiers
Each assignment’s competencies are primary (must be shown to pass), secondary or tertiary (credited when shown).
Warm-up readiness
How much the system knows about each student, from checks and warm-up answers. Click a number to see those students.
Personalized learning profile
The system’s growing guess of what kind of warm-up works for a student; “ready for fully personalized feedback” means it’s confident.
Student Context
One document per student that gathers their lessons, checks and warm-ups, updated weekly.
Current lesson
The lesson whose start date in 📅 Lessons is the latest on or before today, per course.
Rollout
The nightly job that makes and shares each lesson’s student documents from its start date (2 days ahead).
Feedback as a comment
Where a student’s feedback goes: a comment on the document, or written into it. Set per course in ⚙ Settings.
⚙ Settings
Turns each feature on or off per course (feedback as a comment, warm-ups on the Student Dashboard, the nightly lesson rollout, the reflection on Submit, the warm-up style lock) and sets the marking period end dates. Each change asks first and is kept in the SettingsLog tab.
3-Odd / 3-Even
The two halves of period 3, which meet on alternate (odd or even) calendar dates.
Marking period
A term code like 2627_MP1 is marking period 1 of 2026–27.
