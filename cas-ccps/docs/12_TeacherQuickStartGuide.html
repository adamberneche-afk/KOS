Teacher Quick-Start

You're set up.
Here's what's next.

Your workspace has been created by the Admin Manual wizard. This guide covers the two activation steps and how to create your first assignment.

⚡ Activation — 3 minutes 📝 First assignment — 15 minutes 👥 First student — 2 minutes
01

Activate Your Workspace

Your workspace needs to be activated before you can use it. This takes about 3 minutes and only needs to be done once — ever.

Scroll to the ⚡ Two Quick Activation Steps section at the end of your Teacher Manual document. You'll find two links there.

Activation Step 1 — Connect your Rubric Upload Form

~90 seconds

Open the link labelled "Activation Step 1" from your Teacher Manual's setup summary page.

When the spreadsheet opens, a Google permission dialog will appear. Click Allow.

A confirmation message will appear: "✅ Rubric Form Activated — Your Assignment Rubric Upload Form is now fully connected and ready to use."

Close that tab and return here.

Activation Step 2 — Connect your Assignment Review system

~90 seconds

Open the link labelled "Activation Step 2" from your Teacher Manual's setup summary page.

The same permission dialog will appear. Click Allow.

Confirmation: "✅ Review System Activated — Your Assignment Review system is now connected and running."

Close that tab.

✅
Done. You never need to open those spreadsheets again. Everything runs automatically from here.
💡
Can't find the activation links? Open your Teacher Manual Google Doc and scroll to the end — they're on the "Your Teacher Setup Details" page. If the page isn't there, contact your admin.
02

Create Your First Assignment

Before registering students, you need at least one assignment. Creating one generates a Config ID you'll use for every student registration.

1
Write your prompt template in a Google Doc

Create a Google Doc with the student-facing assignment instructions. Set sharing to "Anyone with the link can view."

2
Open the Rubric Upload Form

In your Teacher Manual: ⚙️ Assignment System → Create New Assignment. Fill in your details, paste your rubric, paste the prompt template link, select the tier.

3
Wait for the review email (1–5 minutes)

The system extracts your criteria using AI and emails you a review link. If nothing arrives after 10 minutes, ask your admin to check the system.

4
Review and confirm

Click the pre-filled link in the email. Review the extracted assignment name, AI coach persona, four milestones, and passing standard. Edit anything wrong. Submit.

5
Record your Config ID

Your activation email contains a Config ID: VDOE-XXXXXX-YYYY. You'll paste this into the student registration form for every student in this assignment.

03

Register Your First Student

1
Open the Student Registration Form

⚙️ Assignment System → Register a Student

2
Fill in the student's details

Student Google account email (must be exact), name, block, class name, subject, course name, period, your details, and the Config ID.

⚠️
The Google account email must be exact. A typo means the student can't use the evaluation system.
3
Tell the student their document is ready

Within 1–2 minutes it appears in their Google Drive under "Shared with me." Their Turn-In Form link is already in the document footer.

💡
Registering a whole class? Submit once per student. Register in groups of 20–30. Check your dashboard after each batch.
