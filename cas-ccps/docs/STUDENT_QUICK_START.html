Student Guide

Your Assignment,
Step by Step

Write in your document. Press Submit for Feedback. Read the comment. Turn it in on Canvas. Here's how each step works.

📄 Open your document ✍️ Write under the grey line 📊 Submit for Feedback 💬 Read the comment ✅ Turn it in on Canvas
01

Open Your Document

Every lesson has its own Google Doc, made just for you. You don't make it. It shows up the night before the lesson.

1
Sign in with your school Google account

Your document and your dashboard only work with your school account.

2
Open it from your dashboard

On your dashboard, find the assignment and press Open My Document ↗. The Canvas assignment also has a link to your dashboard.

3
Or find it in Google Drive

Go to drive.google.com and click Shared with me. The document's name starts with the lesson's name and ends with your name.

⚠️
Use that document. Don't make a copy. The system only reads the one made for you.
02

What's in Your Document

Write in one place: under the grey line. Here's everything on the page, top to bottom.

Page header (small grey text)
Your name, class, teacher, block and period. On the next line, the lesson and a Your dashboard link. A Feedback: line that says where your work stands.
System writes here
📋 The lesson title and the assignment
The assignment is in a box: The assignment says what to do, and What your work needs to show lists your “I can” goals as a checklist. ↓ Go to your response jumps down to where you write.
Read this
“Your response” heading
At the top of a new page, so your writing never starts at the bottom of the assignment's page. Under it: “Write your answer under the grey line below.” and ↑ Back to the assignment.
Read this
✍️ ── YOUR RESPONSE BEGINS HERE ──
The grey line. Write under it, in the bigger text.
You write here
“↑ Write your response above. Nothing below this is checked.”
The last line of the page. Your assignment code is in the page footer. (Older documents have a grey “[CONFIG_ID: …] Do not delete this line.” line here instead: write above it.)
Do not edit
🚫
Keep your writing under the grey line and above the last line of the page. Anything above the grey line or below the last line is not checked. Do not change the assignment, the grey line or the last line.
💡
Your feedback isn't in the document text. It's a comment on the document. Click Comments (top right) to see it.
03

Get Feedback

Submit as many times as you like. Revising is the point, and there's no penalty.

1
Write at least 25 words

Write under the grey line. With fewer than 25 words, the dashboard says “Write a little more first.” and tells you about how many words it found.

2
Press Submit for Feedback on your dashboard

Open your dashboard (the Your dashboard link at the top of your document). Find the assignment and press Submit for Feedback. You'll see: “✅ Submitted. In a few minutes your feedback will be a comment on your document: open it and click Comments (top right).”

Your card may first ask one or two short questions about how you worked, such as where your work shows a skill, or the hardest choice you made. Answer each in at least 10 words, then press Submit for Feedback again. Your answers are not part of your work: they come back to you as a comment on your document, so you can look at them while you revise.

3
Wait a few minutes

Press ↻ Refresh on your dashboard. The card says Waiting for feedback…, then Being checked now….

4
Open your document and click Comments

Your feedback is a comment. Each time you submit, you get a new one. The newest is the one to read.

What your card says

Each assignment has a card on your dashboard. The coloured label tells you what to do next.

Not started yet

You haven't submitted this one. Write, then press Submit for Feedback.

Waiting for feedback…

Your work is in line to be checked. Nothing to do yet. Check back in a few minutes.

Being checked now…

Your work is being read right now.

Passed ✓ — feedback is ready: open your document and click Comments

Your work meets the standard. Turn it in on Canvas, or keep improving it and submit again.

Feedback ready — revise, then submit again: open your document and click Comments

Read the feedback, change your work, then press Submit for Feedback again.

Taking longer than usual ⚠ — your work is saved, and your teacher knows

The check took more than 12 minutes and was stopped. Your teacher has been told. If the feedback arrives late, the card changes to show it.

Couldn’t be checked this time ⚠ — your teacher knows. You can submit again

The check ran, but its result could not be used. Your teacher has been told. You can press Submit for Feedback again.

Couldn't be checked ⚠ — tell your teacher

Something went wrong on our side, not with your work. Your work is safe. Tell your teacher. The card has a link to email them.

The card also shows Last submitted (when you last pressed Submit for Feedback) and Last checked (when you last got feedback), or “Not checked yet”.

⏱️
Most checks take a few minutes. If a check takes longer than 12 minutes, the system stops it, your card says “Taking longer than usual ⚠”, and your teacher is told by email. You do not need to submit again unless they ask you to.
04

Read Your Feedback

Open your document and click Comments (top right). Each comment starts with the date and a result line. Here's the shape of one. Your own will talk about your work.

Example comment: passed

Feedback, Oct 22, 10:41 AM

Result: ✅ Passed. Your work meets the standard.

Overall assessment

A few sentences on what your work does well and what would make it stronger.

Milestone breakdown

Each milestone (a part of the task) marked MET, PARTIALLY MET or NOT MET, with a reason.

What to do next

Done with it? Turn it in on Canvas. Want to make it stronger? Keep revising, then press Submit for Feedback again.

If your work needs more, the result line is different, and there's a list of what to fix:

Example comment: revise

Result: ✏️ Revise, then submit again.

Required revisions

A numbered list of the changes to make.

What to do next

Change your work under the “Your response” heading, then press Submit for Feedback on your dashboard again. Repeat until you see ✅.

✅
Revising is normal. Feedback is a coach, not a grade. Your teacher grades your work in Canvas.
💬
Resolving a comment doesn't delete it. If you click Resolve, the comment is still in the document's comment history, and your teacher can still see it.
05

Warm-ups

A warm-up is a short question at the start of class. You answer it on your dashboard, in the Warm-ups card at the top.

1
Type your answer and press Save answer

Find Today's warm-up. Type in the box, then press Save answer. You can change it until the end of the day: the button then says Update answer.

2
Read your score and feedback

Warm-ups are checked overnight. Next time, Last warm-up shows your score out of 10 (Length, Grammar and Engagement) and your feedback. Length is about word count: 30 words or more gets the full length points.

3
Earn extra credit

Under the feedback, reply to it in at least 10 words by the date shown (the end of your next class day), and press Save reply. Replies are checked overnight. Once it counts, you'll see “✓ Extra credit earned” and “+1 extra credit”.

4
Missed a warm-up?

If you didn't answer last class's warm-up, Last warm-up shows it with a box until the end of your next class day. A late answer is scored, but you don't get feedback on it or a chance at extra credit.

📄
Some classes use a warm-up document instead. If the card shows Open warm-up ↗, answer in that document, and reply under the feedback there for extra credit.
06

Turn It In on Canvas

You turn in on Canvas. That's where your teacher grades it. Passing a check first is a good idea, but it isn't required.

1
Copy your document's link

With your document open, copy the address from the browser's address bar.

2
Open the same assignment in Canvas

Choose Start Assignment → Website URL.

3
Paste the link and submit

Paste the link and press Submit Assignment.

💡
Your teacher opens your document from Canvas. They see it as it is when they open it, with your feedback comments.
07

Your Dashboard

Your dashboard is called 📚 My Assignments. Open it from the Your dashboard link at the top of any of your documents.

→
The year menu

It starts on Whole school year. Pick a marking period to see just that part of the year.

→
The tabs

📋 Assignments is your work and today's warm-up. 🎓 Competencies lists every competency in your course by area, with your teacher's rating (or “Not rated yet”) and each piece of evidence your checked work gave: the assignment, the date, and Shown, Partly shown or Not shown yet. ✏️ Warm-ups has every past warm-up with its score, feedback, the question and your answer. 🗂️ My Context shows what the system has gathered about your work, one week at a time, with a link straight to that week in the document.

→
🎓 Competencies done

At the top, one line per course: how many of the course's competencies your teacher has rated you able to do with limited supervision or better, out of all of them (for example “12 of 113”). It goes up as your teacher rates your work.

→
ℹ️ My Info

What the system has on file for you: your name, class, period and teacher. If something is wrong, press ✉ Request a correction or tell your teacher.

→
❓ What do these mean?

Opens a list of the words on the page and what they mean. Press Escape or Close to shut it.

→
↻ Refresh

The page doesn't update by itself. Press Refresh to see the latest.

08

Common Questions

I pressed Submit for Feedback. Where's my feedback?⌄
Press ↻ Refresh on your dashboard. If the card says “Waiting for feedback…” or “Being checked now…”, it's on its way: give it a few minutes. When it's ready, open your document and click Comments (top right). If the card says “Couldn't be checked ⚠”, tell your teacher.
It says “Write a little more first.”⌄
You need at least 25 words under the grey line. The message says about how many words it found. If it says you have not written anything under “Your response” yet, check that you wrote under the “── YOUR RESPONSE BEGINS HERE ──” line and above the last line of the page.
It says “This is already waiting for feedback.”⌄
You already submitted, and it's in line. You don't need to press it again. Check back in a few minutes.
It says my response “has text copied from a feedback block.”⌄
Your writing has a line the system uses itself, like one starting with [SYSTEM:. The message shows which. Delete that line and submit again. Your own words are fine.
Do I have to pass before I turn it in on Canvas?⌄
No. Passing first is a good idea, because it means your work meets the standard. But you can turn it in on Canvas whenever you're done.
Can I submit more than once? Will it hurt my grade?⌄
Submit as often as you need. There's no penalty. Write, get feedback, improve, repeat.
I changed my work after I turned it in on Canvas. What now?⌄
Canvas has your document's link, not a copy. Your teacher sees the document as it is when they open it. You can still press Submit for Feedback to get your changes checked. If you're not sure, ask your teacher.
My assignment isn't on my dashboard.⌄
Check that you're signed into your school Google account. A lesson's document shows up the night before the lesson. Check the year menu says “Whole school year”. If it's still missing, tell your teacher.
I don’t see a “📊 Feedback” menu in my document.⌄
That's normal. That menu doesn't work for student accounts. Use Submit for Feedback on your dashboard instead.
I deleted part of my document by accident.⌄
Try File → Version history → See version history and restore an earlier version. If you deleted the grey line or the end of the page and cannot get it back, tell your teacher.
Something in My Info is wrong.⌄
Open ℹ️ My Info and press ✉ Request a correction, or tell your teacher.
09

Words You'll See

The same list as ❓ What do these mean? on your dashboard.

Competencies done
Competencies in your course that your teacher has rated you able to do with limited supervision or better (1 to 3 on the state's 5-point scale), out of every competency in the course.
Competency rating
Your teacher's rating of a competency on Virginia's 1 to 5 scale, where 1 is the highest: 1 Can teach others, 2 Without supervision, 3 Limited supervision, 4 With supervision, 5 Cannot perform. “Not rated yet” until your teacher decides.
Evidence
What a checked piece of your work showed for a competency: Shown, Partly shown or Not shown yet. Each assignment counts once, at its best result, so an early draft doesn't count against you.
My Context
What the system has gathered about your work, week by week: your checked assignments and your warm-up answers. The same document your teacher sees.
Submit for Feedback
Sends what you wrote under “Your response” to be checked. Press it when you’re ready; you can submit again after you revise.
Waiting for feedback
Your work is in line to be checked. Nothing to do yet; check back in a few minutes.
Being checked now
Your work is being read right now.
Passed ✓
Your work meets the standard for this assignment. You can still improve it and submit again.
Feedback ready — revise
Read the feedback, change your work, then press Submit for Feedback again.
Couldn’t be checked
Something went wrong on our side, not with your work. Tell your teacher.
Comments
Your feedback appears as a comment on your document. Open the document and click the Comments button (top right). Resolving a comment doesn’t delete it.
Last submitted
When you last pressed Submit for Feedback on this assignment.
Last checked
When your work was last checked and given feedback.
Warm-up
A short question at the start of class. Type your answer here and save it before the end of the day. Missed one? You can answer it late until the end of your next class day, for points but no feedback or extra credit.
Extra credit
After a warm-up is checked, reply to its feedback in at least 10 words by the date shown (the end of your next class day) for an extra point.
Marking period
Part of the school year. Use the menu at the top to see one marking period or the whole year.
Block and period
When your class meets. 3-Odd and 3-Even are the two halves of period 3 that meet on alternate days.
My Info
What the system has on file for you: your name, class, period and teacher. Tell your teacher if something is wrong.
