Your Assignment,
Step by Step

Everything you need to know about writing your response, getting feedback, and submitting your final work.

📄 Find your document ✍️ Write your response 📊 Get AI feedback ✅ Submit when ready
01

Finding Your Assignment Document

Your teacher has created a personal document for you in Google Drive. It's already shared with your Google account — you don't need to create anything.

1
Open Google Drive

Go to drive.google.com and make sure you're signed into the correct Google account — the one your teacher used when they registered you.

2
Look in "Shared with me"

Click "Shared with me" in the left sidebar. Your assignment document will be there, named after the assignment and your name.

3
Open it and bookmark it

Open the document. Bookmark this page in your browser — you'll return to it every time you work on this assignment.

⚠️
Use your original document. Don't copy it or create a new one. The system tracks your specific document — any other version won't work for submission.
02

Understanding Your Document

Your document has four distinct zones. Each one has a specific purpose.

📊 Feedback Zone
Your evaluation reports appear here after each check. The most recent is at the top.
System writes here
📋 Assignment Prompt
Your teacher's instructions. Read this carefully before you start writing.
Read only
✍️ Your Response
Everything below "── YOUR RESPONSE BEGINS HERE ──" is where you write.
You write here
Footer / Tracking
Contains your assignment tracking code and the Turn-In Form link.
Do not edit
🚫
One rule: Only write in the response zone. Don't edit the feedback zone, the prompt, or the footer. The system uses those sections to track and verify your work.
03

Getting Your Feedback

When you're ready to get feedback on your work, the assignment has a built-in evaluation tool. You can use it as many times as you need — there's no penalty for running multiple checks.

1
Write your response

Write your work below the "── YOUR RESPONSE BEGINS HERE ──" line. Aim for at least 25 words — shorter responses don't give the system enough to evaluate meaningfully.

2
Click the evaluation menu

In the document menu bar, click 📊 AI Evaluation Panel → Run Assignment Check. A dialog will confirm your work has been submitted.

3
Wait 1–3 minutes

Keep the document open. Your feedback will appear at the top of the document automatically. Refresh the page if it doesn't appear after 3 minutes.

4
Read your feedback

Scroll to the top of the document. Your evaluation report will be there, along with instructions for what to do next.

💡
Check your status anytime. Click 📊 AI Evaluation Panel → Check My Status to see where you stand without waiting for feedback to arrive.
04

Reading Your Evaluation

Every evaluation report ends with one of two results. Here's what each one means and what you should do.

Example Evaluation Report
Overall Assessment

Your response demonstrates a solid understanding of the core concepts. Your argument in the second paragraph is particularly well-reasoned. The connection to real-world examples strengthens your analysis.

Milestone Breakdown

✓ Milestone 1 — Clear thesis statement present
✓ Milestone 2 — Evidence cited and explained
⚠ Milestone 3 — Counterargument not addressed
✓ Milestone 4 — Conclusion ties back to thesis

Result

✅ YOUR WORK MEETS THE STANDARD

You may submit using the Turn-In Form link at the bottom of this document.

If your work needs revision, the result will look like this instead:

Example — Revision Required
Required Revisions

1. Address the counterargument in paragraph 3
2. Add at least one specific piece of evidence for your second claim
3. Expand your conclusion — currently only one sentence

Result

✏️ REVISIONS REQUIRED

Make the changes listed above, then run another check. You can submit once you receive a passing result.

✅
Revisions are normal. Most students revise 2–3 times before passing. Each round of feedback shows you specifically what to improve — it's not a grade, it's a coach.
05

Submitting Your Final Work

Once you've received a passing evaluation, you're ready to submit. The Turn-In Form link is at the bottom of your document.

1
Scroll to the bottom of your document

Find the section labelled "── READY TO SUBMIT? ──". Your Turn-In Form link is there.

2
Open the Turn-In Form

Click the link. Make sure you're signed into the correct Google account — the same one you use for this document.

3
Paste your document link

Copy the URL from your browser's address bar while your assignment document is open. Paste it into the "Assignment Document Link" field.

4
Check the box and submit

Tick "I confirm this is my own original work" and click Submit. Your teacher will receive a notification and your submission is recorded.

⚠️
Only submit after a passing result. If you submit before receiving a ✅ result, the system will reject the submission and write a notice in your document explaining what to do.
06

Common Questions

I don't see the "📊 AI Evaluation Panel" menu in my document. ⌄
This usually means you're not signed into the correct Google account, or you opened a copy of the document rather than your original. Close the document, sign out of Google, sign back in with the correct account, and reopen the document from "Shared with me" in Drive. If the menu still doesn't appear after refreshing, contact your teacher.
I submitted for feedback but nothing appeared after 5 minutes. ⌄
First, refresh the page — feedback sometimes appears without the page updating automatically. If it's still not there, use 📊 AI Evaluation Panel → Check My Status to see if your submission is queued. If status says "Being Evaluated Right Now", give it another minute. If status says "In Progress" for more than 10 minutes, let your teacher know.
The system says "Not Enough to Evaluate Yet" when I try to run a check. ⌄
Your response needs to be at least 25 words before the system can give you meaningful feedback. Check how many words you've written — if it's close, try adding a few more sentences to fully express your ideas.
The Turn-In Form says my submission was rejected. ⌄
Open your document — a notice has been written in the feedback zone explaining exactly why and what to do. The most common reasons are: no passing evaluation yet (run another check first), or a document link problem (make sure you copied the URL while your document was open). Follow the instructions in the notice and try submitting again.
Can I run more than one evaluation? Will it hurt my grade? ⌄
Yes — run as many evaluations as you need. There is no penalty for revising multiple times. The system is designed for iteration: write, get feedback, improve, repeat. Your teacher sees your final submitted work, not how many checks you ran.
I accidentally deleted part of my document — the footer or the zones. ⌄
Don't try to fix it yourself. Contact your teacher immediately and let them know what happened. They can restore the document or set up a new one for you. The document's version history (File → Version history) may also let you undo the change.
I submitted and then made more changes. Do I need to resubmit? ⌄
Once you submit and your submission is accepted, your work is recorded. Minor edits after submission don't change your submission record. If you made significant changes you want your teacher to see, contact them directly.
