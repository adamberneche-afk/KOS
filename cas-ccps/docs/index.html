Whoever you are — student, teacher, or the person deciding whether to adopt this — one of the four below is written for you. Pick that one.

The Classroom Agency System gives students AI feedback on written work within minutes, as many times as they need, while the teacher defines the rubric once and reviews only what's ready. It runs entirely inside Google Workspace — no third-party vendor, and no student data leaves the school.
