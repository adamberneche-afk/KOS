# Drive curation

Filed here for reference during Round 3 reconciliation, at the user's
explicit request — **this is not part of either software system in this
repo.** It is included so nothing is lost, not because it integrates with
`kos-personal/`, `cas-ccps/`, or `leader-hub/`.

## What this actually is

Personal Google Drive housekeeping/audit work: duplicate detection,
deletion candidates, and an organizational tier model for Adam Berneche's
entire personal Drive. Its own `00_MASTER_INDEX.md` explicitly treats both
`kos-personal` and `cas-ccps` as "side projects" it deliberately leaves
alone — this material is *about* Drive organization, not about either
system's code or content.

## The `curriculum/` subfolder is a different course

The nine `Unit_*.md` files are real curriculum content, but for
**"Principles of Business & Marketing" / "Marketing Exploration"** — a
different, unrelated course from cas-ccps's Sports/Entertainment/Event
Marketing pair (8175/8177). Confirmed by direct content comparison against
cas-ccps's Stage0 lesson cards and pacing guide during the Round 3
investigation: different structure, different content. Any apparent
overlap in VDOE competency numbers is coincidental — those are
standardized, reused identifiers across courses, not evidence of shared
material. Do not merge or cross-reference this content with
`cas-ccps/curriculum/`.
