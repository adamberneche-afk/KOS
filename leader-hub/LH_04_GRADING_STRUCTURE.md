## The Rules

| Category | Weight | What belongs here |
|----------|--------|------------------|
| **Summative** | **60%** | Final presentations, major project submissions, tests, quizzes, portfolio assessments |
| **Formative** | **40%** | Classwork, scaffolding activities, checkpoints, workday grades, drafts |

**The 25% cap:** No single assignment may exceed 25% of the quarter grade. Since summative = 60%, this means:

- **Minimum 3 summatives** per quarter (3 × 20% = 60%)
- **Maximum 5 summatives** typical (5 × 12% = 60%)
- Equal-weight summatives are simplest: 3 items → 20% each, 4 items → 15% each

**Checkpoints and workday grades** count as **formative**, not summative. They go in the 40% pool. Use them liberally for large multi-day projects — they keep students on pace and protect your gradebook from zero-at-the-end disasters.

---

## Two Summative Types in Synergy/Canvas

Update the TYPE system from the naming conventions:

| Canvas TYPE | Grading pool | Synergy category | When to use |
|------------|-------------|-----------------|-------------|
| `SUMMATIVE` | Summative 60% | **Summative** | Final project submission, presentation, test, quiz — the assessed knowledge |
| `FORMATIVE` | Formative 40% | **Formative** | Classwork, checkpoint, workday, draft, participation grade |
| `TEACH` | Not graded | — | Instruction day (no grade ever) |
| `REVIEW` | Not graded | — | Review day (no grade ever) |

> **Replace `DUE`, `ASSESS`, and `PRESENT` with either `SUMMATIVE` or `FORMATIVE`** when entering in Synergy. Keep `DUE`, `ASSESS`, and `PRESENT` in Canvas assignment titles as student-facing descriptors — they tell students what the assignment format is. The Synergy category is what drives the grade calculation.

**Canvas title:** `8175 | Q3 | PRESENT | Elevator Pitch`
**Synergy category:** Summative

**Canvas title:** `6115 | Q2 | DUE | Brand Creation Lab`
**Synergy category:** Formative (classwork scaffolding)

## Checkpoint Grade Pattern

For any project that spans more than two class periods, add these grades:

```
[COURSE] | Q[N] | FORMATIVE | [Project Name] — Workday Progress
[COURSE] | Q[N] | FORMATIVE | [Project Name] — Checkpoint
[COURSE] | Q[N] | SUMMATIVE | [Project Name] — Final Submission
```

The workday/checkpoint grades go in the formative pool. The final submission is summative. Students get credit for showing up and working; you get data to intervene before the final is due.

## Canvas Naming — Updated Type Usage

When creating Canvas assignments, use the assignment format from the naming conventions doc, then set the **Synergy category** based on the table above. Canvas students see `DUE`, `PRESENT`, or `ASSESS` — Synergy sees `Summative` or `Formative`.

```
Canvas title:   6115 | Q3 | DUE | The Pitch Room
Synergy cat:    Summative  ← this drives the grade weight
Canvas points:  100
Synergy weight: 20% of Q3 (1 of 3 summatives)
```

```
Canvas title:   6115 | Q2 | DUE | Brand Creation Lab
Synergy cat:    Formative  ← counts toward the 40% pool
Canvas points:  50
Synergy weight: part of formative pool
```

## The Checkpoint Grade Template

For any project lasting 3+ class periods:

```
Day 1 of project:  [COURSE] | Q[N] | FORMATIVE | [Project] — Day 1 Checkpoint
                   (Did they start? Do they understand the task? 0-10 pts)

Mid-project:       [COURSE] | Q[N] | FORMATIVE | [Project] — Workday Progress
                   (How far along? Is quality on track? 0-20 pts)

Final due date:    [COURSE] | Q[N] | SUMMATIVE | [Project] — Final Submission
                   (Full rubric. This is the summative grade.)
```

This structure means a student who does good work but turns in late still has some grade protection from the checkpoints. It also means you're not waiting until the end to find out who's lost.
