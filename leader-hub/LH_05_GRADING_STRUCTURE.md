# Grading Structure & Pacing Alignment
## Formative / Summative Weighting — All Three Courses

**Version:** 1.0 (Q4 2025–26)
**Teacher:** Adam Berneche · Clover Hill High School · CCPS
**Courses:** 8177 · 8175 · 6115

---

## The Framework

| Category | Weight | What goes here |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| **Formative** | **40%** | Classwork, scaffolding, checkpoints, workday grades, process evidence |
| **Summative** | **60%** | Tests, quizzes, presentations, major projects — assessed developed knowledge |

**The 25% rule:** No single assignment may exceed 25% of the quarter grade.
- 60% summative ÷ 25% max = **minimum 3 summative per quarter**
- Typical range: 3–5 summative per quarter
- 3 summative = 20% each · 4 summative = 15% each · 5 summative = 12% each

**Checkpoint and workday grades** are always Formative — they credit students for process and keep larger projects from being all-or-nothing. Plan 1–2 checkpoints per major summative project.

---

## Canvas Setup in Synergy

### Formative assignments in Synergy
- Category: **Formative (40%)**
- Includes: daily classwork, exit tickets, participation, workday grades, project checkpoints, scaffolding activities
- Canvas TYPE: `TEACH` (if graded), `DUE` (classwork/short product), `REVIEW`

### Summative assignments in Synergy
- Category: **Summative (60%)**
- Includes: major projects, unit tests, quizzes, presentations, final products
- Canvas TYPE: `ASSESS`, `PRESENT`, `DUE` (major final product)

## Checkpoint/Workday Grade Guide

Use these to give students credit for process on larger projects without front-loading the summative grade:

| Project | Formative Checkpoint | Timing |
|---------|---------------------|--------|
| Any presentation | Draft/outline workday grade | 2–3 days before live pitch |
| Any major written product | Concept approved or outline | Midpoint of project window |
| Research-based project | Sources/data collected | After research phase, before writing |
| Multi-part project (capstone) | One checkpoint per section | After each completed section |

**Canvas title format for checkpoints:**
```
[COURSE] | [QUARTER] | DUE | [Project Name] — Checkpoint
Example: 6115 | Q4 | DUE | The Big Idea — Checkpoint
```
Always: Category = Formative · Points = partial (10–20% of final product points)
