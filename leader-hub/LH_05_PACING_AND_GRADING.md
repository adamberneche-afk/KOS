## Grading Structure — The Rules

### Two Categories, One Simple Idea

| Category | Weight | What Goes Here |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| **Formative** | 40% | Classwork, scaffolding, checkpoints, workday grades, building knowledge |
| **Summative** | 60% | Tests, quizzes, presentations, final products — assessment of developed knowledge |

### The 25% Cap

No single summative assignment may be worth more than 25% of a student's quarter grade. This means:

- **Minimum 3 summative assignments per quarter** (100% ÷ 25% = 4 max, so 3 at ~33% is technically over — see Q4 notes)
- **Maximum 5 summative assignments per quarter** (typical range)
- When you have exactly 3 summatives, each can be no more than 25% — meaning you carry 75% of the summative weight. That leaves 25% unaccounted. **Solution: split one into a checkpoint + final, or add a fourth summative.**

### The TYPE → Category Mapping

| Canvas TYPE | Synergy Category | Grade Weight | Notes |
|-------------|-----------------|-------------|-------|
| `TEACH` | Not graded | — | Instruction day — no grade needed |
| `DUE` (checkpoint/draft) | **Formative** | Part of 40% | Process grade — completion + effort |
| `DUE` (final product) | **Summative** | Part of 60% | Polished product — rubric-graded |
| `ASSESS` | **Summative** | Part of 60% | Quiz, test, or written evaluation |
| `PRESENT` | **Summative** | Part of 60% | Live presentation or performance |
| `REVIEW` | Not graded | — | Review day — no grade needed |
| `INTRO` | Not graded | — | Unit launch — no grade needed |

**How to tell a formative DUE from a summative DUE:** If students are still building toward something, it's formative. If it's the finished, polished product they're being evaluated on, it's summative. Drafts, outlines, research notes, workday progress checks, and practice runs are always formative.

### Synergy Setup

In Synergy, create exactly two grade categories per course per quarter:

| Category Name | Weight |
|--------------|--------|
| Formative | 40% |
| Summative | 60% |

All checkpoint/workday/classwork grades go in Formative. All final products, presentations, tests, and quizzes go in Summative. Within each category, Synergy averages the grades equally (all formatives count equally, all summatives count equally) unless you manually weight individual assignments — which you typically don't need to do if you've built 3–5 balanced summatives.

## Checkpoint & Workday Grade Practice

**Rule of thumb:** Every summative project that takes more than one class period deserves at least one checkpoint grade. The checkpoint is Formative — it's a progress grade, not a quality grade.

**What to grade at a checkpoint:**
- Completion: did the student use the time productively? (100/80/50/0)
- Effort: is there visible progress from the last check?
- You don't need a rubric — a simple completion/effort check is enough

**What to put in Canvas:**
```
8177 | Q2 | DUE | Employee Handbook — Workday Progress
```
Points: 10 (or whatever your formative scale is)
Category: Formative
Due date: the workday class period date

**What goes in Synergy:** Same title, Formative category. Enter the grade the same day.
