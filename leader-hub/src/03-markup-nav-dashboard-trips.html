💊 Medical Info Needed Per Student
Each student's parent must complete the Medical Information section. Remind parents to include:
  • Medical history (asthma, diabetes, genetic disorders)
  • All allergies (medication / seasonal / stings / food)
  • Current medications & dosage
  • Any special needs or accommodations (noted on school file)
  • Emergency medication — self-carry or clinic
⚠️School-supplied epinephrine will NOT be sent on any field trip per CCPS Policy 4134.
ℹ️For overnight trips: medication must be brought to the clinic by a parent/guardian no later than 3 business days before the trip.

🔑 Category Guide
Cat I — Professional Leave (PR1): Off-site conferences, workshops, recruiting. Needs Principal + Director + (out-of-state: CAO).
Cat II — District Leave (PR2): In-district PD, curriculum development, department training. Needs Principal.
