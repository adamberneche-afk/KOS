What is an SBE?
A School-Based Enterprise is a HQWBL experience where students design, plan, operate, and grow a real business on campus. The Cavalier Shop (8177) qualifies as a Retail Store SBE under CCPS Group III procedures.
Required: Training Agreement · Student Reflection · Business Plan · Principal Approval

Procurement Overview
Every purchase at the Cavalier Shop follows a 3-step approval cycle per the Store Scaling & Reinvestment Plan: student-led market research → formal Purchase Requisition → CTE Chair + Admin approval. Items ship to the back room where students execute the full receiving process.
