## The Ledger cycle's trigger time (measured 11-15; built 11-15, not pushed)

`showSetupStatus()` just after the push: an idle cycle takes about 14.5 s,
so 1,440 a day is about 5.8 h of the 6 h daily trigger quota every
trigger in the Ledger project shares. The operator approved the fix
("yes"): each job on the way to feedback runs only when a cheap check
finds work, a job that ran hands on to the next step in the same run,
quiet hours hold the jobs without a check, and every 15 minutes the cycle
runs everything (the backstop). Operator steps:

1. **Until it is pushed:** set the Script Property `LEDGER_CYCLE_EVERY` in
   the Central Ledger to slow the costliest jobs, e.g.
   `{"backPropagateCompletions":2,"unlockReadyParts":5,"runTurnInCheckFlow":2}`.
2. **After the push:**
   - remove `LEDGER_CYCLE_EVERY`, or keep it (a slowed job is checked only
     when due, but still runs at once when the step before it hands it work);
   - run `installLedgerChangeProbe()` in the Central Ledger;
   - submit one test assignment as the test student and wait for its
     feedback;
   - paste what `reportLedgerChangeProbe()` and `showSetupStatus()` print
     (the line "At this rate: about N h a day (P% of the 6 h)", WARN over
     half, ALERT over 80%, and each job's "checked … nothing waiting …").
3. **If anything looks wrong:** `LEDGER_CYCLE_GATES` = `off` puts the cycle
   back as it was, without a push. `LEDGER_CYCLE_QUIET` (`off`, or JSON
   hours) and `LEDGER_CYCLE_BACKSTOP` (minutes) change the rest.

A follow-up moves the harvest onto onChange only if the probe shows
Studio's writes fire it.
