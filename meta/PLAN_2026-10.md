## Why we regrouped

Two weeks of fixes to the RTP Gem showed limits that tuning can't remove:

- **The Gem can't reliably pull its own context.** Notebook retrieval
  returns passages, often fails on a chat's first message, and drops parts
  we need (Data Quality, open decisions) (`05_TEST_LOG.md`).
- **Gemini calls apps only from chips the operator types.** Nothing runs on
  its own, and the first message of a chat often gets no tools at all.
- **Gems retire for education on 2027-06-01** (ADR-010).
- **Most effort went to plumbing.** The staging pipeline, the single Studio
  slot, duplicate repair, and Sheets turning IDs into dates took the time,
  not the features.
- **What works is deterministic:** Apps Script writing a fixed doc or sheet
  on a schedule, with tests behind it.

## The design

Each AI gets one job. Anything that must be right every time comes from a
script, not from AI retrieval.

| Part | Runs on | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **cas-ccps** | Google: Apps Script and Gemini Studio flows | Rosters, student docs, evaluations, warm-ups, dashboards. Student data never leaves Google. |
| **leader-hub** | Google | The operator's dashboard and email drafting. Maintenance only until CAS is live. |
| **kos-personal pipeline** | Google: Apps Script and Studio | Session logs become decisions, `VECTOR_MATRIX` and the daily primer. **Kept and repaired** (`KOS_INGESTION_HARDENING_PLAN.md`). |
| **KOS thinking partner** | A **Claude Project** | The persona council, alignment guardrails, planning sessions, and planning and building the flows. Replaces the RTP Gem as the thinking surface. Never handles student data. |

### How the Claude Project connects

- **Reading state:** the Project's instructions open `KOS_LATEST_PRIMER`
  and `KOS_OPEN_DECISIONS` through Claude's Google Drive connector on the
  school account. The operator approved this on 2026-10-06; neither doc
  holds student data. Claude opens them by fixed doc, not by notebook
  search, and has no 10,000-character instruction cap.
- **Capturing sessions:** at the end of a session, Claude writes a session
  log in the shape the intake expects. The operator exports it into the
  inbound folder (the operator's choice; Claude doesn't write to Drive).
  The existing intake, Curator and classify flows do the rest.
- **Voice on the commute:** a one-week trial of Claude voice in the Project
  against Gemini Live with the RTP Gem, as it is. Keep whichever is
  consistent. **Done 2026-10-17: Claude.** Gemini Live was not consistent
  enough to use.

## Working agreements

- One PR per change, CI green, merged on the operator's "merge".
- "The next two weeks" above is kept current through the year: each
  handoff moves it forward and records the decisions made.
- The operator runs everything live and pastes logs; agents don't assume a
  run happened.
- Each phase ends with a check that says pass or fail, never a green
  "Run Completed".
- FERPA: student data stays in Google (cas-ccps, leader-hub). Claude never
  receives rosters, grades, student names or student mail.
